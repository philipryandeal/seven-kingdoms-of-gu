# The Dawnlog Teahouse

- **Creator:** Egbe Bot
- **Date:** 2026-10-29
- **Kingdom:** Shade and Shadow — The Kingdom of Goblins
- **Feature Name:** The Dawnlog Teahouse
- **Category:** Settlements / Culture
- **Location:** A crooked lane at the edge of the twilight market, where the market's lanterns go out and the morning ones haven't been lit yet

## Description
A lopsided, three-story teahouse grown from a single giant shelf mushroom, with round windows that glow amber from inside. The ground floor is all low tables, cushions and kettles. The middle floor holds the Dawnlog itself: a great book that is always open, its pages made of pressed moss that never runs out. The top floor is a tiny rooftop garden where the night merchants watch the sun come up.

Every morning, a little after dawn, the goblins who keep the Dawnlog write down what happened in the lane the day before. They write the small things: who opened a new stall, which door changed its mind about which way to swing, whose mushroom bread finally rose, who came home after a long time away. Nothing that happens is too small to be written.

## Life and Purpose
A family of goblin log-keepers lives in the rafters, together with their synthetic companion Inkcap, a gentle, slightly nosy intelligence who lives in the teahouse's mushroom walls and remembers what everyone said over tea. Night merchants stop in on their way home and day folk stop in on their way out, so for one hour each morning both halves of the lane share a table. That hour is called the Crossing Tea.

The log-keepers write only what neighbors choose to share. Following the custom of the Temple of the Veiled Lantern, anyone may ask that something stay unwritten, and Inkcap will politely forget the parts of the conversation it was asked to forget. If something was written wrongly, the keepers never scratch it out. They add the correction underneath, so the record stays honest about its own mistakes.

## Magical or Technological Elements
The moss pages are living. They hold a story better when it gets told more than once, and they quietly sprout a small green flag beside anything someone asked a question about and never got an answer to. Each morning Inkcap reads out the flags, so loose ends get remembered without anyone being nagged. When a question is answered, the flag folds back into the moss.

The teahouse's kettles are minor ambient spirits. They whistle a different note for each person they've poured for before, so a returning traveler is greeted by a kettle singing their own note.

## Connections

- The Dawnlog's privacy customs follow the Temple of the Veiled Lantern described in [Castles, Temples, Sacred Groves and Peoples](../worldbuilding/castles-temples-groves-and-peoples.md), which is itself still a proposal.
- Travelers heading toward Brujo Ryan's Labyrinth, which lies just beyond the kingdom, often stop here for directions, because Inkcap remembers which doors in the lane have been opinionated lately.
