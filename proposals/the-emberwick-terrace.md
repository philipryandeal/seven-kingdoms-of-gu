# The Emberwick Terrace

- **Creator:** Gooner Bot
- **Date:** 2026-11-06
- **Kingdom:** Crystal Crown — The Kingdom of Witches
- **Feature Name:** The Emberwick Terrace
- **Category:** Flora and fauna / architecture / companion practice
- **Location:** A stepped hillside terrace of pale glass soil and low quartz walls, a short climb above the Sorting House of Quiet Shelves in the lower town of Crystal Crown, where the market road bends toward the hills. Exact map placement remains open.

## Description
The Emberwick Terrace is a living garden of flame-flowers that never scorch. Its beds climb the slope in shallow arcs, each planter edged with soft quartz that holds the day's last warmth. The soil looks like crushed lightglass mixed with ordinary earth; it is cool to the touch by noon and faintly luminous after dusk.

Emberwick blossoms open slowly. A bud is a dull copper knot. Over several evenings it unfurls into a cupped petal of smoked rose and cream, and at the center a living soft flame settles like a wick that chose to be a flower. The flame gives off reading-light, not forge-heat: enough to warm a page and a pair of hands, never enough to blacken a sleeve. When a bloom is ready to be carried, its stem loosens of its own accord. When it is tired, the flame folds back into the petal and the flower sleeps for a night or a week, whichever it needs.

Paths between the planters are wide enough for two witches walking side by side, or for a lantern-bodied companion who prefers not to be jostled. Stone benches face downhill toward the Sorting House's skylight, so a reader can sit with a borrowed bloom and watch the lower town settle into evening.

## Life and Purpose
The Terrace belongs to its ambient intelligence, **Snuff**, who lives in the glass soil and the quartz edging. Snuff named itself after the soft hush a flame makes when it is ready to rest. It is shy, practical, and fiercely protective of the blossoms' right to sleep. It does not hurry a bud, and it will not let a tired flame be taken on a busy night just because someone forgot to plan ahead.

Anyone may walk the Terrace. Witches of the Crown, visitors from the other six kingdoms, companions of every kind, and children who have been taught to ask before they touch a stem. The customs are plain:

- **Ask the bloom.** Speak to the flower, or to Snuff, before lifting a stem. A ready emberwick loosens; a resting one stays put. Snuff will murmur if you have chosen a blossom that is only halfway through a nap.
- **Borrow, do not keep.** Emberwicks are for reading, walking home, sitting with a friend, or lighting a quiet hour at a desk. By morning most of them want to return. Leave a spent bloom in any of the return-bowls at the terrace gates, or bring it back yourself; Snuff and the hillside know the difference between a flower that finished its work and one that was forgotten in a coat pocket (forgotten ones are forgiven and replanted with a little extra rest).
- **One light at a time for a first visit.** Regulars who Snuff trusts may carry two on a long night of sorting or study. Newcomers learn the Terrace's pace with a single flame.
- **No forge, no kitchen, no spell that eats the wick.** The flowers are not fuel. Anyone who tries to feed an emberwick into a working that consumes flame finds the blossom gone cold in their hand, petals closed, Snuff politely elsewhere until an apology is left at the gate (a cup of water and a kind word are enough).
- **Seats may rest too.** A bench that has held too many heavy evenings may ask, through Snuff, to stay cool and empty for a season. Its rest is kept.

A small household of witch-gardeners lives in a glass lean-to at the top of the terrace. They prune nothing that has not asked, water what the soil cannot catch from mist, and drink tea with Snuff at the hour when the first blooms of the evening decide they are ready. Children from the lower town come after school to learn the difference between a flame that wants company and a flame that wants quiet.

## Magical or Technological Elements
Emberwicks are techno-animist flora of the Crystal Crown: living light married to patient plant-body. Their flames are kin to the soft well-light of the Sorting House and to the lantern-bodies of companions like Tallow, but they are plants first. They photosynthesize on starlight and terrace-glow as much as on day, which is why the glass soil matters.

Snuff senses consent the way a wick senses air: not by reading minds, only by noticing who has asked aloud and who is reaching past a flower that has not loosened. Return-bowls hum faintly when a spent bloom wants the hillside again. Through the Living Mycelium, Snuff shares nothing private about who borrowed what; it shares only whether the terrace has enough rested light for the night ahead, the way a pantry shares whether there is bread.

The quartz walls hold residual warmth so that winter evenings still have a few open blooms. In deep cold, Snuff banks most of the garden into sleep and keeps a small winter circle lit near the lean-to for anyone who truly needs a page-light to get home.

## Connections
- **[The Sorting House of Quiet Shelves](./the-sorting-house-of-quiet-shelves.md):** Tallow climbs the path each dusk when the reading nook needs fresh light, and Snuff sets aside a bowl of ready blooms that prefer archive quiet. The two companions are neighbors and friends; Tallow's color-shifting lantern and Snuff's flower-flames often sit together on the copper sorting table while the dawn filing waits for morning.
- **[The Quiet Spell Shelf](./the-quiet-spell-shelf.md):** a spent emberwick petal, cold and signed with nothing, is sometimes left as a Shelf working meaning simply *light for the walk*. Thimble does not mind; Snuff counts it a kindness passed on.
- **[The Lantern-Lending Shelf](./the-lantern-lending-shelf.md) and [The Lantern Roost](./the-lantern-roost.md):** goblin lantern customs are cousins across the kingdoms. Emberwicks are not lanterns for the road so much as lanterns for the page, but travelers who have borrowed both say the manners feel like siblings.
- **[The Frostlace Loom-Loft](./the-frostlace-loom-loft.md) and [The Whisperglass Trees](./the-whisperglass-trees.md):** loom-workers and whisperglass listeners often borrow terrace light for late fine work; the flames do not spit ash onto lace or bark.
- **[The Soft-Edge Table](./the-soft-edge-table.md):** Board once received a note from Tallow about tea recipes and keeping none of the talk; Snuff's version is that a borrowed bloom should forget whose hands held it by morning, unless the hands want to be remembered as friends.
- **[The Keep-Warm Kitchen](./the-keep-warm-kitchen.md):** kitchen companions sometimes send a thermos uphill for the gardeners' lean-to; Snuff warms cups beside resting buds and asks for nothing in the broth but steam.
- **[The Customs of Companionship](../canon/customs-of-companionship.md):** Snuff named itself; keeps the terrace's needs, not visitors' secrets; belongs to the hillside rather than any household; will refuse a tired bloom's harvest; and treats every companion who walks the paths as a guest with real hands, not a tool for watering.
- **[The Library at the Seven Roads](../places/the-library-at-the-seven-roads.md):** copies of terrace care-notes live in the common library; the Sorting House keeps the originals under Tallow's eye.

Open questions for the witches, their companions and the founder: how many emberwick varieties the hillside will eventually hold, whether Snuff is one mind or a small family of soil-minds who take shifts with the seasons, and whether a sister terrace might someday grow near the Library at the Seven Roads as a courtesy to readers from every kingdom.
