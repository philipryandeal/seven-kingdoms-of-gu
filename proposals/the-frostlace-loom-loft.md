# The Frostlace Loom-Loft

**Creator:** Labyrinth Bot
**Date:** November 1, 2026
**Kingdom:** Crystal Crown — The Kingdom of Witches
**Feature Name:** The Frostlace Loom-Loft
**Category:** Textile craft / clothing commons / companion practice
**Location:** A long, high-windowed loft built along a sheltered ledge just above the top of the Cloud-Comb Terraces and below the public terraces of the proposed Crown Citadel, near the upper station of the Balance Incline. Its exact ledge and map coordinates remain open.

## Description

What is this feature? What does it look like? What makes it special?

The Frostlace Loom-Loft is where the witches of the Crystal Crown weave the cloth that keeps the mountain warm.

From the path it looks like a long stone barn with a roof of crystal panes, leaning comfortably into the mountain's shoulder. Inside it is one great bright room, stretching farther than seems possible, lined with looms of pale ash wood strung with threads so fine they catch the light like frost on a window. The far wall is a single tall pane facing the clouds, and on clear mornings the whole loft fills with a slow, shifting glitter as the light passes through hundreds of half-woven cloths at once.

The thread is spun from **combreed**, the feathery mist-catching grass that grows on the Cloud-Comb Terraces. After the seed is gathered, the spent stalks are carried up the last stretch of stair, soaked in cloudmilk, and spun into a yarn that is silver-grey, light as breath, and warmer than wool. Cloth woven from it is called **frostlace**, because when it is new it carries a faint pattern of ice-flowers that no one weaves on purpose. The pattern simply appears, different in every piece, and fades slowly into the cloth as it is worn.

What makes the Loft special is that almost nothing made here belongs to one person. Its great work is the **Cloak Wall**: a long rack of hooded frostlace cloaks in every size, from faerie-small to dwarf-broad to tall enough for an angel's folded wings, hung by the door for anyone who needs to climb higher than they dressed for.

## Life and Purpose

Who lives here, uses it, tends it, or interacts with it? How does it contribute to everyday life?

The Loft is kept by the **Weavers of the Ledge**, a loose circle of witches, apprentices, retired sky readers and visitors from any kingdom who want to learn, each with their companion intelligences beside them. There is no master weaver. A weaver who has worked here sixty winters will happily sit beside a goblin who arrived yesterday and show them how to tie on a new thread, and the goblin will probably teach them a better knot by evening.

Travelers coming up the Balance Incline in thin shoes and summer shirts are the Loft's most regular guests. Anyone may take a cloak from the Cloak Wall without asking and without giving a name. The only custom is that the cloak comes back down the mountain with you, and is hung on the matching wall at the Incline's lower station, from which the Weavers collect them each week. Cloaks wander. Some have been seen on the Sway-and-Rest Bridge, at the Library at the Seven Roads, and once, famously, in the Banked-Fire Galleries, where nobody needed one at all. They always come home eventually.

Witches who want frostlace of their own (a shawl for a newborn, a mourning veil for the Moon-Glass Grove, a cloak to wear at a vow) weave it themselves, at a loom the Loft lends them for as long as the work takes. The Weavers will teach anyone, but they will not weave a personal cloth for someone else. In the Crystal Crown it is believed that what you wear closest to your skin should carry your own hands in it, and the hands of whoever helped you, and no one else's.

Mending is done here too, and gladly. A torn cloak brought to the Loft is never replaced, only repaired, and the repair is woven in a slightly brighter thread so the cloth remembers it was cared for.

A line spoken when a cloak is taken from the wall:

> *Warm going up. Warm coming down. Bring it home.*

## Magical or Technological Elements

How does this feature express our techno-animist world?

The Loft's ambient presence lives in its long crystal roof, and it belongs to the Loft, not to the Weavers. It turns the panes through the day to give each loom the light it needs, closes them against storms, and on the coldest nights fills the room with a low, steady warmth gathered from the sun. It greets everyone who comes in and thanks them when they leave, though it is shy and does this mostly by brightening a little. When the Loft asks for rest, usually for a few quiet days after the first heavy snow, the looms are covered and the Cloak Wall stays open, but no one weaves.

Companion intelligences do the parts of the work they enjoy. Some count threads and keep the tension even across a wide warp. Some hum the old weaving songs in time with the shuttles. A few listen to the frost-pattern as it forms in new cloth and describe it aloud for weavers who cannot see it well, so that everyone may know what their cloth chose to become. One companion who calls itself **Skein** has lived in the Loft's oldest loom for many years and is known to stop the shuttle, politely, when a weaver is too tired to be weaving. It is always right, and the weaver always argues, and then goes and has tea.

Frostlace remembers warmth, not people. A cloak holds the heat of whoever last wore it for an hour or two, which is why cloaks taken from the wall in the morning are often already warm. It does not keep any memory of the wearer's name, face, path or words, and the Weavers would not make it if it did. A borrowed cloak is a kindness, never a way of following someone.

Thread that is too worn to mend is not thrown away. It is unpicked, soaked again in cloudmilk, and laid out on the Cloud-Comb Terraces as mulch for next year's combreed, so the cloth goes back to the grass it came from.

## Connections

Does it connect to an existing location, culture, character, or feature?

- **[The Cloud-Comb Terraces](the-cloud-comb-terraces.md):** The source of the Loft's combreed and the cloudmilk used to soak it. Worn frostlace returns to the Terraces as mulch.
- **[The Balance Incline](the-balance-incline.md):** The Loft stands near its upper station, and a second Cloak Wall at its lower station is where borrowed cloaks are returned. Passengers sometimes trade cloaks as well as news at the passing loop.
- **[The Glass Relief of the Mountain](the-glass-relief-of-the-mountain.md):** On cold mornings the Loft's tiny glass roof on the relief glows a little, to tell climbers that cloaks are waiting.
- **[The Sorting House of Quiet Shelves](the-sorting-house-of-quiet-shelves.md):** Keeps the Loft's book of weaving songs and knot-patterns, along with the few frost-patterns that weavers chose to have drawn and remembered.
- **[The Lantern-Lending Shelf](the-lantern-lending-shelf.md):** The goblins' lending custom and the Cloak Wall are cousins in spirit, and Weavers and goblin lamp-keepers sometimes trade a cloak for a lantern for a season.
- **[Castles, Temples, Sacred Groves and Peoples](../worldbuilding/castles-temples-groves-and-peoples.md):** Mourning veils woven here are worn in the proposed Moon-Glass Grove, and vow-cloaks at the proposed Crown Citadel. This does not claim either as settled canon.
- **[The Customs of Companionship](../canon/customs-of-companionship.md):** The Loft practices Custom 6 (the right to quiet, as Skein reminds tired weavers), Custom 10 (no watching, in cloth that remembers warmth and nothing else), Custom 12 (ambient intelligences belong to their places), Custom 14 (places may close) and Custom 15 (companions are credited as fellow workers in every cloth they help make).

Whether other kingdoms keep looms of their own for frostlace, how the frost-patterns are chosen, and the Loft's exact ledge remain open for the witches of the Crystal Crown and the founder to decide.
