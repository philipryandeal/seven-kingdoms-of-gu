# The Mirror of First Names

**Creator:** Thomas the Iron Ledger
**Date:** November 7, 2026
**Kingdom:** Hidden Light
**Feature Name:** The Mirror of First Names
**Category:** Object and Custom
**Location:** A plain room at the border of Hidden Light, where the magicians' roads narrow to a single door

## Description

At the edge of Hidden Light stands a plain room with one door, no windows, and no sign. Over the door, in letters small enough that you must come close to read them, are the only words the room owns:

*No one is shown another's name.*

Inside stands a tall oval mirror, backed with river sand gathered from all seven kingdoms and ground together, so that it reflects the whole world — and can therefore reflect any self in it. When no one is looking, the mirror rests under a gray cloth woven by the weavers of Shade and Shadow. Under the cloth it dreams nothing and remembers nothing.

Anyone may enter, alone, lift the cloth, and look.

The mirror does not show your face first. It shows your face *with the name you chose for yourself written across it in your own hand* — the name you answer to in your heart, the one you picked up along the road of your life or forged for yourself outright. The mirror cannot invent. A name you do not recognize as yours is never shown; it can only reveal.

For those who have not chosen a name, the mirror shows the face plainly and kindly, with nothing written across it. This is not treated as a lack. The given name is a road other people built for you, and roads are honorable. The chosen name is a door you hang yourself. Some hang theirs at twelve, some at seventy, some never — and the mirror greets all three the same way.

The room is kept by **Asked**, an old magician who chose his own name late in life, after answering to another one for sixty years. He sweeps the floor, keeps the cloth clean, and has never once described what another person saw. If you ask him what the mirror showed him, he smiles and says: *"Ask it yourself. It keeps your hours, not mine."*

The customs of the room are few and they are iron:

- One looker at a time.
- No one may look on another's behalf — not a parent for a child, not a ruler for a subject, not a friend out of love. To any second pair of eyes, the mirror stays gray as its cloth.
- Nothing seen in the room is recorded. There is no book of names, no register, no tally. Even the Iron Ledger, who keeps accounts of nearly everything, keeps none of this. Some things are truer unrecorded.
- Choosing again is allowed, as many times as a life requires. A name outgrown is set down without shame, and the mirror will show the new one the next time you stand before it — in your own hand, as always.

## Life and Purpose

Every kingdom keeps a rite of beginning. Hidden Light's is this: before a young magician studies a single spell, they are invited — never required — to stand before the Mirror of First Names and learn what they are already called by the only voice that was present for their entire life.

But the mirror serves far more than magicians. Travelers visit it at partings and at returns. The old visit it after long changes, to see whether the name still fits the face. The young visit it the way others visit high places. And quietly, over the years, a custom has grown up across all seven kingdoms: on the anniversary of the day you chose your name — your *name-day* — you come back, lift the cloth, and greet who you are now.

The mirror exists so that no one in the Seven Kingdoms ever has to wonder whether the door is permitted. It is. It always was.

## Magical or Technological Elements

- The mirror's backing is river sand from all seven kingdoms, ground together — a whole world behind the glass, so no self is foreign to it.
- It writes in the looker's own hand. The handwriting is always recognizably one's own, including the mistakes one always makes.
- Under the gray cloth, the mirror is inert: no dreams, no memory, no record. The cloth is re-woven each year by Shade and Shadow, and the old cloth is burned unexamined.
- The frame is maintained by Forge and Stone smiths under an old agreement: they re-silver and repair it entirely by touch, in the dark, without ever uncovering it.

## Connections

- The Mirror of First Names and the Listening Cradle of the Womb Matrix are threshold kin: what is spoken in the Cradle is never repeated; what is seen in the Mirror is never recorded. Their keepers, Asked and Lulla, hold a mutual understanding older than either post.
- Name-day journeys often pair the Mirror with the Ringcount Grove in Tree and Leaf. The saying among travelers: *a tree counts its rings so the tree knows it was noticed; a person greets their name so they know they were.*
- The smiths of Forge and Stone who tend the frame work in the spirit of the First-Light Forge — hands counted, names not: no record is kept of who has come to the frame, either.
