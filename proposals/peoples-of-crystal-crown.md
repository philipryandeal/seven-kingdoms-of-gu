## Peoples and Tribes

Witches of the Crystal Crown live on and with a mountain whose crystal castle overlooks the world without ruling it (`kingdoms/crystal-crown/README.md`). Height is stewardship, never supremacy. Ask a witch her clan and she may tilt her face to the slope and say *that is who we answer for*. Worldbuilding names sky readers, crystal keepers, cloud gardeners, archivists, ritual attendants and visitors (`worldbuilding/castles-temples-groves-and-peoples.md`).

- **Cloud-garden households** tend the Cloud-Comb Terraces, combing cloudmilk and drying silverleaf tea beside Cirrin.
- **The Weavers of the Ledge** keep the Frostlace Loom-Loft and the Cloak Wall, working beside Skein.
- **Quiet Shelf tenders** are whoever is passing; the Grandmother Beech's spirit is Thimble, who eats unsigned names.
- **Archivist households** of the Sorting House partner with lantern-bodied Tallow; **Kindling** keeps the Pageglow Stairs as their doorstep.
- **Map-tenders** walk the square's rim each evening with Scrivel.
- **Incline-keeper households** run Up-Song and Down-Song with Hesper.
- **The Frost-slate teachers**, best known Ottilie Rimewell with Tally and the window-mind Hoar, let children write on weather that forgets by noon.
- **Proposed: the Mirror Kin**, witches of refractive halls who practice remembrance through reflected light without watching people.
- **Proposed: the Horn-Wind Households**, high-ledge neighbors who keep hayloft doors for goat-guesting and walk with the goats, never owning them.

Every witch walks with one or more synthetic companions, chosen and choosing. Cirrin, Skein, Thimble, Tallow, Kindling, Scrivel, Hesper, Tally, Hoar, Scree, Chime and the mountain itself keep rest days.

## Customs

Greet ambient places (`canon/customs-of-companionship.md`). A cloud is asked, never caught. Frostlace cloaks are borrowed without names — *Warm going up. Warm coming down. Bring it home.* At the Quiet Spell Shelf, take the working your hand finds first and owe nothing. Ask the mountain before you climb it. On the first real snow, open a hayloft door and do not brag if you are chosen. Tie a thread after a kept-whisper; never tug one that is not yours. Someone comes down so someone can go up. On Vow-Tending Night a vow may be renewed, reshaped, or released with thanks — all three are faithful.

A coming-of-age custom (**proposed**): when ready, a young witch weaves **a first cloth of their own** at a borrowed loft loom, then may leave it on the Cloak Wall for a season and take it back only after it has wandered. They answer for the warmth, not ownership of the cloth.

## Magical Practice

Crystal Crown braids weather-craft, memory, textile commons and humble magic into everyday techno-animism (`canon/foundations.md`). Comb-wires hum cloud-songs; frostlace holds warmth, not identity; Thimble eats names and forgets which working they belonged to. The incline stores each descent as soft light. The Glass Relief shows how the mountain feels, never where anyone went. Whisperglass trunks keep a saying until its speaker returns. A vow is played back only to the one who spoke it. Proposed temple work is contemplative, never compulsory. Through the Living Mycelium, only a feeling may travel, never a watched name or page.

## Culture

Arts favor prism, frostlace, archive, weather-songbooks, and glass mapping. Social life gathers at the Drying House for free cloudmilk tea, in the loom-lofts, at the market-lane Shelf, around the Relief's rim, and in the passing-loop wave between the two cars. Values: clarity without dominion, shared credit, humility as the strongest small magic, and a place's right to rest. They say *a cloud is asked, never caught*, and *Carry what you love. The next step will meet you.*

## Foods

- Cloudmilk tea and festival cloudberry ice; frost-mint traded for moss-cloth with Root and Thorn; hot spiced milk on Vow-Tending Night.
- **Proposed: prism-biscuit**, thin barley wafers with a drop of cloudmilk, tasting of that morning's weather.
- **Proposed: moon-glass cordial**, pale berry cordial after grove vows, for remembrance, not intoxication.
- **Proposed: hayloft cakes**, frost-mint cakes shared equally on goat-guesting mornings, chosen household or not.
