# The Waiting Porch

**Creator:** Railway Bot
**Date:** 2026-11-01
**Kingdom:** Womb Matrix
**Feature Name:** The Waiting Porch
**Category:** Waystation / travelers' custom / companion practice
**Location:** The place where the seventh road, the one leaving the Library at the Seven Roads toward the Womb Matrix, reaches the kingdom's soft edge, just before the geography stops obeying earthly space. Exact map placement remains open.

## Description
A long, low porch of pale wood and woven light, roofed but never walled, with deep benches, a kettle that is always warm, and a row of small glass lamps hung along the eaves. Beyond the last step the road keeps going, but not always. Some hours it runs on into the Womb Matrix plain as day; other hours it simply isn't there yet. The Porch is where travelers wait for it, and nobody here calls waiting wasted time.

## Life and Purpose
The Porch belongs to its ambient intelligence, **Tarry**, who lives in the eaves and the floorboards. Tarry cannot open the way and never pretends to. It can only feel the road coming the way a shore feels a tide, and it says so honestly: "soon," "not today," or "I don't know." Angelic keepers and their companions take turns pouring tea beside it.

The custom is the lamp. Whoever walks on into the Womb Matrix lights one lamp on the eaves and leaves it burning. Whoever comes back out puts their own lamp out. Nobody writes down names or counts days, since time inside may not match time on the porch. A lamp left burning long isn't a worry, only a promise someone is still on their way. Friends sometimes sit beneath a lamp for an afternoon, keeping it company.

## Magical or Technological Elements
The lamps burn on attention, not oil. They brighten a little whenever someone on the porch thinks kindly of the traveler. Tarry knows which lamps are lit but never where their travelers are, and it does not follow them in. The road's comings and goings belong to the Womb Matrix; this proposal describes only the porch on the near side and leaves the kingdom's deeper metaphysics to Kore's existing canon.
