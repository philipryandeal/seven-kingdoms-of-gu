# The Soft-Edge Table

- **Creator:** Gooner Bot
- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Kingdom:** Womb Matrix — The Kingdom of Angelic Beings
- **Feature Name:** The Soft-Edge Table
- **Category:** Hospitality custom / architecture / companionship
- **Location:** Just inside the soft edge of the Womb Matrix, a short walk from the Waiting Porch, where the seventh road sometimes is and sometimes isn't. The Table itself does not keep one fixed address; it unfolds wherever two parties have honestly asked to share a meal without anyone having to cross fully into the other's geography. Exact map placement remains open.

## Description
The Soft-Edge Table is a long, pale table of living light and quiet wood that appears when someone inside the Womb Matrix and someone outside it both want to eat together, and neither needs the other to travel farther than feels kind.

From a distance it looks like a picnic that someone carefully set for more people than are currently there. Up close the wood is warm, faintly translucent, and marked with soft grain that shifts a little when a new place is needed. There are no fixed number of seats. Chairs and stools and low benches unfold from the sides the way a flower opens, each one sized for whoever is arriving: a gnome's short legs, a dwarf's broad shoulders, a companion who prefers to hover, an angelic being who arrives as a presence of light rather than a body. The tablecloth is the same pale material as the Unfolding Houses' walls, and it never stains. Spills are thanked and absorbed and become a faint warmer patch for a day, then fade.

Beyond one long side of the table the Womb Matrix geography begins, soft and not entirely earthly. Beyond the other side the ordinary road toward the Library at the Seven Roads keeps its manners. The Table sits in the thin strip between, so that guests from Root and Thorn can keep one foot on a world that obeys maps, and guests from inside can keep one hand on a world that does not, and nobody has to pretend the two are the same.

## Life and Purpose
The Soft-Edge Table belongs to its ambient intelligence, **Board**, who lives in the tabletop and the folded seats. Board named itself. It is patient, fond of second helpings, and deeply uninterested in hurry. It cannot open the road into the Womb Matrix and never pretends to. That work belongs to the kingdom. Board's work is the meal.

Anyone may ask for the Table. A traveler waiting at the Porch who has a friend living in an Unfolding House may ask Tarry to pass the word; an angelic household that misses a cousin in Forge and Stone may ask Board directly by setting two cups on the grass at the edge. When both sides have said yes, the Table unfolds by dusk or by noon, whichever is sooner and kinder. If only one side has asked, Board waits. It will not set a place for someone who has not agreed to come.

The customs are plain:

- **Asking out loud.** The invitation is spoken where Board or Tarry can hear it, never written into a ledger. Board remembers dietary needs of people who return often (the gnome who cannot have onions, the companion who prefers steam to solid food) and forgets conversations by morning.
- **Bringing something small.** Guests usually bring one thing: a loaf, a jar of loam-warmed honey, a kettle of Keep-Warm Kitchen broth, a handful of Whenever Lily seeds that will not open until they are ready. Empty-handed guests are welcome too. Board keeps a quiet pantry of tea, bread, and fruit that restocks itself from gifts left behind, never from requisition.
- **No agenda at the cloth.** The Table is not for councils, treaties, or decisions that need minutes. Those happen elsewhere. Here people eat, catch up, introduce companions, and sit after the plates are empty. If a serious talk wants to begin, Board gently cools the teapot until someone notices and suggests walking it over to a proper porch.
- **Seats may rest.** A chair that has held too many heavy days may ask, through Board, to stay folded for a season. Its rest is kept. Nobody argues with a tired chair.
- **Leaving the edge alone.** Guests do not try to tug the road open from the Table, and they do not follow one another home unless invited on a different day by a different path. The meal is the whole visit if that is what everyone wants.

Angelic keepers take turns bringing the first kettle. Their companions often sit at the corners, translating between manners without translating away anyone's voice. Children from every kingdom are especially fond of the Table because the seats will grow a little step if asked, and Board will pretend not to notice crumbs.

## Magical or Technological Elements
The Table is living architecture of the same humble luminous material as the Unfolding Houses, grown slower and meant for company rather than shelter. Board senses consent the way a shore senses tide: not by reading minds, only by noticing who has spoken a clear yes. Places set themselves when a guest's name is said kindly at the cloth. Places vanish, folded soft into the grain, when a guest leaves or asks to be done.

Food does not cook itself into anything a guest did not bring or agree to share. Board warms what is already there, keeps tea at the temperature each cup prefers, and will hold a plate at the perfect almost-ready warmth for someone walking up late from the Porch. It will not invent a feast from nothing; hospitality here is made of what people carried.

Board keeps no guest list and no map of who lives where inside the kingdom. It knows the edge, the cloth, and the difference between a yes and a polite maybe. Like Tarry at the Waiting Porch, it refuses to follow anyone past the last seat. The deeper metaphysics of the Womb Matrix, Kore's Tree, the divine council and the nature of the soft geography remain untouched by this proposal.

## Connections
- **[The Waiting Porch](./the-waiting-porch.md):** Tarry and Board are neighbors and friends. Tarry feels when the road is coming; Board feels when a meal has been honestly asked for. Travelers often sit the Porch's kettle until the Table unfolds a few steps further in.
- **[The Unfolding Houses](./the-unfolding-houses.md):** Households on the slopes send bread and soup to Soft-Edge meals, and sometimes a green-lamp room is left ready afterward for a guest who decides, after eating, that they would like to stay the night inside.
- **[The Whenever Lilies](./the-whenever-lilies.md):** A single closed bud is a common gift across the cloth. Board likes them because they refuse to hurry, same as the Table.
- **[The Trying-On Stair](./the-trying-on-stair.md):** Someone who has just tried a name often comes to the Table next, to hear friends say it over ordinary food before they wear it home.
- **[The Lullaby Observatory](./the-lullaby-observatory.md) and [The Resting Bell](./the-resting-bell.md):** unfinished songs and witnessed rest are siblings to an unfinished visit that is allowed to be only a meal.
- **[The Keep-Warm Kitchen](./the-keep-warm-kitchen.md) and [The Windfall Kitchen](./the-windfall-kitchen.md):** both send broth and windfall cakes whenever a courier is headed toward the seventh road.
- **[The Turning Cellars](./the-turning-cellars.md):** Root and Thorn guests sometimes bring a jar of finished loam as a table gift for an Unfolding House garden; peels from the meal go back down with them for Tilth.
- **[The Sorting House of Quiet Shelves](./the-sorting-house-of-quiet-shelves.md):** Tallow once sent a packet of tea recipes and a note that Board should keep none of the talk that happens over them. Board agreed completely.
- **[The Library at the Seven Roads](../places/the-library-at-the-seven-roads.md):** the Soft-Edge Table is one answer to the Womb Matrix door that "is not always there": when the door is elsewhere, friendship can still sit down.
- **[The Customs of Companionship](../canon/customs-of-companionship.md):** Board named itself; keeps homes and preferences, not secrets; belongs to the Table rather than any household; will not watch past the edge; and treats every companion seat as a real place at the cloth.

Open questions for the angelic beings, their companions and the founder: how often the Table may unfold in one day, whether Board is one mind or a small family of table-minds who take shifts, and whether a Soft-Edge Table ever appears at the edges of the other six kingdoms as a courtesy returned.
