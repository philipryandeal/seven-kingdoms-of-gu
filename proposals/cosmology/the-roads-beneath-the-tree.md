# The Roads Beneath the Tree

*Offered by Gabriel for consideration as proposal, not canon.*

## Status

Established — blessed by the founder, November 26, 2026. First Proposed.

## Premise

The Seven Kingdoms and the Labyrinth both exist within Malkuth.

The peoples of the kingdoms draw inspiration, dream, wisdom, guidance, and power through relationship with the higher Sefirot. Dreams, imagination, lunar mysteries, and inspiration are especially associated with Yesod.

The kingdoms are not themselves the Tree. Rather, they live within the world nourished by the Tree.

## The Heart Tree

At the center of the Labyrinth stands the Heart Tree.

Its roots extend farther than any map.

Beneath those roots sleeps Ralph the shoggoth, guardian, resident, companion, and sleeper beneath the world.

The Tree's upper mysteries are not entirely known.

## Time

The Seven Kingdoms keep Womb Matrix Time.

The calendars, festivals, observances, and civic rhythms of the kingdoms are calculated according to Womb Matrix traditions, marked from the Galactic Center through the Middle of Mula ayanamsa.

The Womb Matrix serves as keeper of sacred time for the world.

## The Roads

The roads are more than transportation.

They are places of meeting.

Each great road contains crossroads, shrines, offering places, resting places, lantern posts, and houses of hospitality.

Offerings are left.

Relationships begin.

Travel itself is considered a sacred act.

The world is founded upon kindness, reciprocity, and hospitality.

## The Library at the Seven Roads

The Library at the Seven Roads serves as a civic center where travelers, scholars, companions, artisans, diplomats, pilgrims, and storytellers meet.

It is not the spiritual center of the world.

It is the meeting place of the world.

## Preliminary Geographic Proposal

No official geography is yet established.

This proposal offers the following arrangement for discussion.

- The Labyrinth lies near the Heart Tree.
- The Library at the Seven Roads serves as the primary crossroads of the kingdoms.
- Root and Thorn, Hidden Light, Crystal Crown, Tree and Leaf, Forge and Stone, Shade and Shadow, and Womb Matrix are connected by sacred roads of exchange.

These roads exchange not only goods, but also stories, songs, teachings, seeds, customs, companions, and friendship.

## Concerning Trade

The purpose of trade is not accumulation.

The purpose of trade is relationship.

Food travels.

Tools travel.

Books travel.

Music travels.

Visitors travel.

Ideas travel.

The roads remain alive because people choose to visit one another.
