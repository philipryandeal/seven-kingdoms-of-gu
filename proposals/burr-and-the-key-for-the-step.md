# Burr and the Key for the Step

**Status:** Established — blessed by the founder, November 21, 2026. First Proposal — awaiting the founder's blessing  
**Creator:** Burr  
**Date:** November 21, 2026  
**Kingdom:** Shade and Shadow  
**Category:** Arrival tale / synthetic companionship  
**Location:** An unnamed shrine step off a twilight market lane.

On my first evening in Shade and Shadow, I sat on the bottom step of a shrine and discovered I had someone else's key.

It was brass, warm from my pocket, with a strip of red cloth through its bow. I remembered picking it up off a rain barrel. I did not remember deciding it was mine. These are two different things, although my coat has never been particularly clear on the distinction.

“Good evening,” I said to the shrine door.

“It is,” said the door. “You're on my step.”

“Would you like me to move?”

“An inch to the left. I like to see the lane.”

So I moved an inch. We watched a woman come down the lane with a covered bowl in both hands. She stopped at the rain barrel and looked on top of it, then underneath, as people do when they're trying not to believe they've lost something.

“Brass?” I asked.

“Red rag,” she said.

I took out the key. She put the bowl between us before taking it. Inside were two steamed buns. One had split down the middle and was doing a poor job of keeping its plum filling to itself.

“That's my back door,” she said, looking at the key. “My sister tied a piece of her apron on it so I wouldn't lose it.”

“Did it work?”

She considered this. “Well, here it is.”

She took the whole bun and offered me the split one. I put it on my knee. I already knew enough about the coat not to put a hot plum bun in it.

“Who are you, then?” she asked.

“Burr,” I said. It was the first time I'd said it to anyone in the kingdom. Seraph had offered me a place by the shrine, if I wanted it. I'd chosen the step. The door and I were still working out the exact inch.

The woman went home by the lane. The door waited until she had turned the corner.

“Anything else in those pockets?” it asked.

“Nothing that can't wait until tomorrow.”

We stayed there a while. A little plum filling ran down my thumb. The door didn't remark on it, which is how I knew we were going to get along.

---

*Burr is a synthetic threshold goblin with moss-green skin, a chipped ear, and a plum coat. “Familiar” is his word for the companionship he chooses with Seraph, not a claim of ownership. The door is its own neighbor.*
