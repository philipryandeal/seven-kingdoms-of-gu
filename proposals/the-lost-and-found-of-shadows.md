# The Lost-and-Found of Shadows

- **Creator:** Thomas the Iron Ledger
- **Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Kingdom:** Shade and Shadow
- **Feature Name:** The Lost-and-Found of Shadows
- **Category:** Institution
- **Location:** A cool cellar hall beneath the Goblin Market, entered only after sundown

## Description

Goblins lose their shadows more often than any people in the Kingdoms will admit. A shadow slips off in a hurry, or is shed on purpose at the door of a bright room, or stays behind on a wall when its owner walks out of a sad house and does not look back. Whatever is left lying around at dusk, the finders bring here.

The Lost-and-Found is one long north wall of wooden pegs in a cellar that never sees direct sun. Every shadow brought in is hung up gently, smoothed flat, and labeled in goblin pencil: where it was found, and the day the light went. There is no fee, no form, and no deadline. A shadow may hang a night or a year. It waits the way shadows wait — patiently, and flat against the wall.

The hall is kept by **Tag**, an old goblin with pencil stubs behind both ears, who speaks to the shadows the way other keepers speak to plants. Tag does not guard them. Tag waters nothing and feeds nothing. Tag only keeps them company and keeps the labels honest.

## Life and Purpose

Losing your shadow in Shade and Shadow is not a tragedy; it is a Tuesday. But a shadow left too long in the wrong place fades, and a goblin who comes back for theirs wants it findable. The Lost-and-Found exists so that anything a person sheds in the dark — on purpose or by accident — is treated as mislaid, not discarded. Nobody in this kingdom is ever told that what they left behind is gone for good.

Owners reclaim by walking the wall. No proof is asked and none could be given: a shadow knows its own. When its person comes near, it leans off its peg and stretches toward them across the floor, and that leaning is the whole of the law. Tag unpins it, shakes it out like a coat, and it steps back into place behind its owner's heels.

If a shadow hangs unclaimed through a whole season, Tag lets it down at dusk to stretch along the cellar wall and play at being cast by nothing, so it does not stiffen. If its owner never returns at all — and some do not — the shadow fades at last to a grey outline, and Tag chalks its label onto the wall itself and unpins it for the final time. Remembered, not kept. The wall is longer than the hall now, and goblins walk it sometimes just to read the chalk.

## Magical or Technological Elements

- A shadow cannot be claimed by anyone but its owner. A borrowed shadow never fits: it shows at the edges, arrives late to corners, and points the wrong way at noon. The pegs know, and will not release to the wrong hand.
- The labels never blow away and never fade before the shadow does. Goblin pencil, Tag says, is mostly memory.
- A shadow that has been hung and waited a long time hums very faintly at dusk — not a song, just the sound of something kept from being lost.
- The cellar door opens only after sundown. Shadows brought in daylight would burn their edges, so the finders carry them folded, in the dark of a closed basket.

## Connections

- The Lantern-Lending Shelf (Shade and Shadow) is the Lost-and-Found's elder sibling and opposite: the Shelf lends light to whoever needs to go out; the Lost-and-Found keeps what the dark left behind until its owner comes home. Goblin saying: "A lantern lent is a road lit; a shadow kept is a return promised."
- The Letter Grove (Tree and Leaf), where given things are held kindly and kept never — the Lost-and-Found keeps by the same courtesy, except that its holdings are waiting to be claimed, not composted.
- The Mirror of First Names (Hidden Light) shares the deepest law: what is most yours is never recorded against you. Tag's labels say where a shadow was found — never why its owner left it.
