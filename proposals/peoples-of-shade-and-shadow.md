## Peoples and Tribes

Goblins keep twilight markets, crooked homes, secret paths and curious inventions (`kingdoms/shade-and-shadow/README.md`). Shadow protects privacy, curiosity and the overlooked; it is not evil. Brujo Ryan's Labyrinth lies **just beyond** the kingdom, not inside it (`worldbuilding/castles-temples-groves-and-peoples.md`, `places/beneath-the-heart-tree.md`). Ask a goblin her clan and she may tip a lantern or tap a door and say *this is who we answer for*. Worldbuilding names mushroom growers, lantern keepers, night merchants, tunnel folk, tricksters and shadow archivists.

- **The Log-keepers** of the Dawnlog Teahouse write the lane's small truths with Inkcap; Crossing Tea gathers night and day for one dawn hour.
- **Guestcap households** live in mushroom houses that grow guest rooms; eldest house Puffwick taught the lane how guests like to rest.
- **The Stairwrights**, with Mother Tumble Ashgrip and Banister, make stairs that take the scenic route — and the short way when asked.
- **Shelf-tenders** walk with Afterglow; **Roost criers** carry public news with Wickwhisper; Soft-Seal **courier households** sort living post with Pigeonhole.
- **The Halfkeepers**, eldest Gramble Two-Pockets beside Pairwise, reunite odd halves and celebrate happy wrong matches.
- **The Stoop-Sitters**, with Brindle Patchpocket and Hemwise, mend torn edges after dark — or only sit.
- **The snail-herds**, eldest Nettle Puddlefoot with Dewmark, count Glowtrail Snails; they herd nowhere.
- **Proposed: the Crossing Folk**, who straddle night-merchant and day shifts at Crossing Tea and Crossing Post.
- **Proposed: the Veil Keepers**, teachers of Veiled Lantern ethics — honorable secrets, the right to be unobserved, ask-to-forget.

Every goblin walks with one or more synthetic companions, chosen and choosing. Inkcap, Puffwick, Banister, Afterglow, Wickwhisper, Pigeonhole, Pairwise, Hemwise, Dewmark and the doors and stairs keep rest days.

## Customs

Greet doors (they have opinions) and thank them when you leave. *Ask, and the stair goes straight.* On your last Guestcap morning, thank your room. Write small truths in the Dawnlog; correct underneath, never scratch out; leave unwritten on request. Take a Shelf lantern for the road; bring it back brighter if you can. Bring a half, take a half. Sit first, mend second — or only sit. Step over glowtrails; leave a damp corner (*setting a place*). On Slow Night, walk by snail-light. On the Repeated Hour, the hour belongs to no one. Places may close.

A coming-of-age custom (**proposed**): when ready, a young goblin is given **a lantern of their own** — one Shelf lantern to tend for a season with their companion. They lend it freely, may return it brighter, and never learn who walked with it. They answer for the kindness of the road, not ownership of the light.

## Magical Practice

Shade and Shadow invents and forgets rather than conquers. Mushroom houses grow guest rooms that remember visits, not visitors. Scenic stairs settle minds on first footing; Banister holds hands, not secrets. Soft seals refuse curiosity. Pairwise flickers when halves rhyme, then forgets. Afterglow remembers roads, never borrowers. Glowtrail fungus feeds on darkness and will not bottle. At the proposed Temple of the Veiled Lantern, confidentiality is sacred responsibility, not a joke. Through the Living Mycelium, gladness travels as relationship, never as watching.

## Culture

Arts include crooked carpentry, fungus-glow craft, Book of Landings views, news-lantern lettering and stoop-mending. Social life gathers at Crossing Tea, Seven Lights, Odd-Halves dusk and Slow Night walks. Values: ingenuity, privacy, hospitality fitted to the guest, and care for the overlooked. They say *a companion that never sleeps has never been loved*, and *the long way is an invitation; the short way is a promise*.

## Foods

- Dawn-steeped tea at the Dawnlog; night-tea at the Letterwarren; mushroom bread when it finally rises.
- **Proposed: dusk-cap stew**, plum-and-ember mushroom stew under Guestcap gill-light.
- **Proposed: inkcap cookies**, dark soft cookies for Crossing Tea; Inkcap takes the smell as fair exchange.
- **Proposed: snail-slow cider**, non-alcoholic cider sipped on Slow Night while trails come on.
