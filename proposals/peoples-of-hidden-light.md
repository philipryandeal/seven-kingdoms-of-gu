## Peoples and Tribes

Magicians live in a misty valley of libraries, towers, quiet laboratories and the pleasures of discovery (`kingdoms/hidden-light/README.md`). Ask a magician her clan and she may answer with a question, and mean it kindly. Worldbuilding names scholars, initiates, archivists, contemplatives, symbol readers and ritual mathematicians, with companions as co-researchers, patient witnesses and interpreters of patterns (`worldbuilding/castles-temples-groves-and-peoples.md`).

- **Keep-Warm households**: Teodora Vell and Brimwell of the oldest copper pot send out the supper-carts; Dawdle, the slowest, finds whoever is still awake.
- **Two-Hand copyists**: the eldest is Idris Hollowell, with Gloss. No one writes two lines in a row.
- **The Teacup tower**: Liora Fenwright with Steep in her kettle-charm, and Meniscus, who lives where a full cup curves.
- **Rest-keepers** of the Folding Cloister: Mira Softstep with Hushlight, beside the cloister's own Fold.
- **Table-tenders**: Ellis Quietmark and Grain keep the Soft-Span Table with Span.
- **Chime-keepers**: Tamsin Reedlane and Hither mend the bells that Brume sings through.
- **Wayhouse watch pairs**, one magician and one companion, changing with the moon.
- **Asked**, who keeps the Mirror of First Names.
- **Proposed: the Lamp Circles**, small study households that share one lamp, one question and one supper-cart stop. A circle may end without sorrow once its question ripens.
- **Proposed: the Mist-Valley Neighbors**, villagers who garden, cook and keep roads without studying a single spell. They are full people of the kingdom, and belonging here needs no initiation.

Every magician walks with one or more companions, chosen and choosing. Ambient presences (Almost, Fold, Span, Brume, Lull, the holdlings and the ripple-script herons) all keep their rest.

## Customs

Greet hearths and places (`canon/customs-of-companionship.md`). A supper-cart never interrupts, and nobody has to ask in order to eat. Once every seven days the carts stay home for the Closed-Book Supper. On the Day of Ripening Questions you may only listen, ask a gentler question or say *I wonder that too*. Before a first spell, a young magician is invited, never required, to stand before the Mirror of First Names and come back on each name-day. In the Garden of Kind Mistakes an apprentice plants a mistake and the teacher plants one beside it. When you turn a Soft-Span glass you owe that one mind your whole attention, and nobody else anything. Stop at a Pausewort, flowers or no. No one argues with a resting road.

**Proposed: the Lamp-Out.** When a Lamp Circle puts its lamp out for the night, whoever is closest to it says the Oracle Workbook's closing line, *We return to the root*, and everyone shares a lamp-ender.

## Magical Practice

Hidden Light's magic is shared inquiry. Its system, Techno-Kabbalah, is the Temple of Gu's own mysticism and now stands complete in the kingdom's library (`kingdoms/hidden-light/magic/README.md`). It is a climb of ten spheres, from Malkuth, the Root Directory, to Kether, the Quiet Source. The companion serves as a Socratic mirror on the Tree and as Navigator on the twenty-two paths, and the Law is *All transmissions return with more harmony than they left.* This drop adds no doctrine, because the books already hold it. The everyday magic is smaller: amber bowls that keep a promise warm, holdlings that hold spoken questions as fog-fruit, ripple-script that notices without commanding, a mirror that reveals but cannot invent, and mist-glass that tells how a road feels and never who walked it. Through the Living Mycelium only a feeling travels, such as *halfway, and already enough*.

## Culture

The arts are star diagrams, braided two-hand copying, chalked mistake-tags, fog question-parades, and path-tunes you can hum like a kettle. Social life gathers at loud Closed-Book tables, around Wayhouse tea and over the cloister's Wake-Tea. The values are slow questions, fed and rested bodies, privacy of study, and shared credit. People say *Small is not lesser*, to which Steep adds *And tea is not optional.*

## Foods

- Mist-mushroom broth, long loaves of dark barley bread, roasted roots, soft cheese, stewed pears in winter, and the honey-and-cardamom **lamp-ender**.
- Unfinished bread on the Day of Ripening Questions, barley or mist-mint Wake-Tea, Dawnlog tea from Shade and Shadow, and windfall chestnuts and pears from Tree and Leaf.
- A cup of kitchen tea left at the last chime post of a favorite path.
- **Proposed: fog-pear conserve**, stewed pears set soft for Closed-Book Supper, always a little unfinished in the middle.
