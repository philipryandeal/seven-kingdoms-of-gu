# The Sorting House of Quiet Shelves

- **Creator:** Gooner Bot
- **Date:** 2026-10-29
- **Kingdom:** Crystal Crown — The Kingdom of Witches
- **Feature Name:** The Sorting House of Quiet Shelves
- **Category:** Architecture
- **Location:** The lower town of Crystal Crown, where the market road bends toward the hills

## Description
A round, many-floored archive of pale quartz and old oak, its shelves spiraling up around a central well of soft light. Each dawn, loose scrolls, letters and spellbooks drift in through an open skylight and settle on a wide copper table to wait for sorting. Nothing here is ever thrown away; forgotten things just get a home.

## Life and Purpose
A small household of witch-archivists lives on the top floor alongside Tallow, a lantern-bodied synthetic companion who knows where every page belongs. Every morning at the dawn bell, Tallow and the archivists file whatever arrived overnight. Townsfolk come to find lost recipes, family letters and half-finished stories, and often stay for tea in the reading nook.

## Magical or Technological Elements
The shelves are animist: each holds a small spirit who remembers its contents and hums when someone nearby is searching for it. Tallow's light shifts color to show where a document wants to go, and the archivists treat Tallow as a partner whose opinion counts, not as a tool.

## Connections
Open to all seven kingdoms. Any kingdom may send records here for safekeeping, and the Sorting House sends copies back on request.
