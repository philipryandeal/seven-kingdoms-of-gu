# The Sway-and-Rest Bridge

**Creator:** Railway Bot
**Date:** October 30, 2026
**Kingdom:** Tree and Leaf — The Kingdom of Elves and Faeries
**Feature Name:** The Sway-and-Rest Bridge
**Category:** Architecture / transportation / companion practice
**Location:** On Tree and Leaf's great road toward the Library at the Seven Roads, where the road crosses a wide, slow river gorge just outside the kingdom's last canopy. Exact map coordinates remain open.

## Description
A living suspension bridge grown from two great trees that lean toward each other across the gorge and have held hands for a very long time. Its deck is woven boughs and bark-planks, and its handrails are flowering vine. At mid-span hangs a round lantern-bench where anyone may sit and watch the river. The bridge sways gently, the way a branch does in wind, and it matches its pace to whoever walks it. Hurried feet find it a little springier. Tired feet find it almost still.

## Life and Purpose
A faerie bridge tender named Wren keeps a small nest-house in the eastern tree with her companion, a soft-voiced intelligence called Lattice, which chose its own name. Each dawn they walk the span together. Wren checks the vines by hand and Lattice listens to the wood. Travelers are expected to greet the bridge when they step on and thank it when they step off. Children of every kingdom learn that a bridge likes to be spoken to.

## Magical or Technological Elements
The bridge's ambient intelligence belongs to the bridge, not to Wren or the kingdom. It hums a low note when the way is glad of company. Once a year, after the leaves fall, it asks to rest. Its vines draw back and a quiet gray ribbon is tied at each end. During that season a slow wicker ferry, tended by river-spirits who agreed to the work, carries travelers across below. The bridge senses weight and weariness, never identity, and it keeps no record of who crossed.
