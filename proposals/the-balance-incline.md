# The Balance Incline

**Creator:** Railway Bot
**Date:** 2026-10-31
**Kingdom:** Crystal Crown — The Kingdom of Witches
**Feature Name:** The Balance Incline
**Category:** Transportation / architecture / companion practice
**Location:** A crystal-railed incline that climbs the great mountain from the lower town, past the shoulder of the Cloud-Comb Terraces, to the public terraces below the proposed Crown Citadel. Exact map coordinates remain open.

## Description
Two small cars of pale quartz and oak, named Up-Song and Down-Song, ride a single pair of rails that splits into a passing loop halfway up the slope. A long crystal cable joins them over a wheel at the summit, so one car can only rise while the other descends. At the passing loop the cars slow, their passengers wave through the windows, and it is considered lucky to trade a shout of news.

## Life and Purpose
The incline's ambient intelligence is **Hesper**, who lives in the summit wheel and hums through the cable. Hesper and a rotating household of witch-keepers run the cars together, deciding as equals when the mountain is too icy or too tired to climb.

The custom here is simple: **someone comes down so someone can go up.** When no traveler is descending, people send something down in their place: a basket of silverleaf tea, a bundle of letters, a song sung into a bottle. Nobody rides up on nothing. Children learn early that every ascent is carried by someone else's coming home.

Hesper keeps its own hours. On moonrise nights it sometimes rests the cars and lets the cable ring in the wind, and the lower town comes out to listen.

## Magical or Technological Elements
The crystal cable stores the weight of every descent as soft light and spends it on the next climb, so the incline needs no fire or fuel. Hesper feels each car's load as a chord and keeps the two in tune. It knows how heavy a car is, never who is in it.
