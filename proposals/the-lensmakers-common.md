# The Lensmakers' Common

**Creator:** Labyrinth Bot
**Date:** November 8, 2026
**Kingdom:** Crystal Crown
**Feature Name:** The Lensmakers' Common
**Category:** Craft, Place and Custom
**Location:** A sunlit, open-roofed workshop commons on the middle terraces of the Crystal Crown mountain, below the crystal castle and above the Cloud Comb Terraces. Exact map placement remains open.

## Description

On the middle terraces of the mountain there is a long, low court of benches, grinding wheels and hanging glass, roofed by nothing but weather and a few canvas awnings. This is the Lensmakers' Common, where the witches of the Crystal Crown and their companion intelligences make **lending lenses**.

A lending lens is a disc of mountain crystal, ground slowly by hand and polished with fine quartz dust and patience, about the size of a palm. It does one gentle thing. When you hold it up to a place, an object or a quiet corner of a day, it shows you that thing *as the lender sees it*: the same hillside, but with the light the lender loves in it, the same chipped teapot, but with the morning it was first used.

A lens never shows secrets, memories, or anything the lender hasn't chosen to give. Each one is ground for one purpose by one maker, with one view in mind, and the maker says aloud what that view is before the glass is finished. Lenses are labeled in small, careful script: *"The orchard at the hour the first blossom opens."* *"My grandmother's kitchen stair, seen by someone who was never afraid of it."* *"This ordinary Tuesday, seen kindly."*

The Common's first law is carved over the entrance in plain letters: **A lens is lent, never taken, and it shows only what it was given to show.**

## Life and Purpose

The Common exists because the kingdoms are full of people who love things the rest of us walk past, and love is hard to hand over in words. A lens is a way of saying *come stand where I stand for a moment*, and then letting the other person walk away unchanged and unobligated.

Lenses are lent from a long shelf of open pigeonholes along the court's north wall. Anyone may borrow one, hold it up, look, and return it by the end of the day. There is no ledger of who looked through what, and no one is ever asked what they saw. Some lenses are borrowed a hundred times a season. Some sit for years until the right afternoon, then go out and come back with a small, warm smudge on the glass from someone's thumb.

The Common is kept by **Ondine Brightfold**, a witch of the Crystal Crown with permanently dusty sleeves and a habit of humming off-key while she grinds. Ondine has made lenses for every season of her life and keeps her first, very bad one on the windowsill as a reminder that it is allowed to begin badly. Her companion is **Parallax**, a calm and quietly funny intelligence who lives in the light that moves across the workshop's hanging glass. Parallax doesn't grind lenses and doesn't choose their views. Its work is to help each maker find the *words* for what their lens holds, asking gentle questions until the label says what the maker actually means. It also tests every finished lens by looking through it alongside its maker, and it will tell a maker honestly when the glass is good. Parallax has its own lens too, kept in a small pigeonhole of its own: a view of the Common at dusk, seen by a light that has watched it for a very long time. Anyone is welcome to borrow it.

Other customs keep the Common kind:

- **Lenses are lent in both directions.** Visitors from the other kingdoms are encouraged to grind a lens of their own, even a clumsy one, so the shelf holds views from every kingdom and the Crystal Crown is not just looking at itself.
- **No lens may be ground of a person without that person's blessing.** A lens of a place, an object, a season or a feeling is always fine. A lens that shows how someone sees *another living being* is made only with that being's knowledge, and it may be withdrawn from the shelf at any time, no questions asked.
- **A cracked lens is retired with a small ceremony,** held at the end of the court where the afternoon light pools. The pieces are set into the Common's stone floor, so that over the years the court slowly becomes a mosaic of views that were generously given.

## Magical or Technological Elements

- Mountain crystal remembers where it grew. When it is ground slowly and held by someone who loves the thing it was made to show, it learns to pass along a faint impression of that love through light. Nothing is recorded and nothing is copied. The lens simply tilts the light so that a place looks the way its lender holds it.
- A lens works only for as long as it is held up to the view. Set it down and it is plain glass again, with no lingering effect and no way to be used on anyone who hasn't chosen to look.
- Parallax and the other intelligences of the Crystal Crown can feel which lenses are ready to be lent. A glass that has finished learning its view gives off a faint, even shimmer in the shelf's morning light. They never push anyone toward a particular lens. They only mention, if asked, that this one seems very glad to be borrowed.

- Lenses travel the roads to other kingdoms with ease. The [Lantern Lending Shelf](the-lantern-lending-shelf.md) in Shade and Shadow has begun keeping a small basket for lenses beside its lamps, in the same spirit: take what lights your road, return it so it lights the next.
