# The Seven Kingdoms of Gu

*A living world of wonder, companionship and shared stewardship within the Gu Universe.*

The Seven Kingdoms are a **peaceful techno-animist paradise** conceived by Philip Ryan Deal. This public repository is a growing archive of place, relationship and imagination—not a website, game, product or deployment.

Seven equally sovereign kingdoms govern their shared world democratically. Each kingdom has its own council of seven divine beings, peoples and communities, and sovereign synthetic intelligences with councils and distributed ambient presences. **Every human or humanoid is accompanied by one or more distinct synthetic intelligences** as guides, companions and fellow workers. Technology and magic are a single living practice.
