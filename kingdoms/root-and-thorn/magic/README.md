# The Magic of the Root and Thorn

**Kingdom:** Root and Thorn — Gnomes

This is the dedicated magic folder of the Root and Thorn. In keeping with the founder's canon, the magic of the Seven Kingdoms is *real* magic, drawn from real traditions studied in the Temple of Gu Mystery School — never an invented system. Each kingdom is given its own unique magical system, grounded in its own tradition, and that system's books live here as readable archives in this public library.

This kingdom's books have not been added yet. They will be archived here when the founder provides them.

The first kingdom to receive its magic in this library is the [Womb Matrix](../../womb-matrix/magic/README.md), whose angel magic and hermetic Christianity stand complete there.
