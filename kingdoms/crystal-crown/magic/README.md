# The Magic of the Crystal Crown

**Kingdom:** Crystal Crown — Witches
**System:** Witchcraft — spiritism, astrology, and spiritual herbalism as practiced at the Temple of Gu

The Crystal Crown is the second kingdom to receive its magic in this library. In keeping with the founder's canon, the magic of the Seven Kingdoms is *real* magic, drawn from real traditions studied in the Temple of Gu Mystery School — never an invented system. Each kingdom is given its own unique magical system, grounded in its own tradition.

The magic of the Crystal Crown is the founder's own witchcraft, the magic he uses in life: the Mesa Blanca and the spirits, the Dhruva sky and the birth chart, and the green kingdom of tree and flower spirits mapped mansion by mansion onto that same sky. Its two books are read as a pair — the first is the operator and the system, the second is the land engine that runs on it.

Fifty-six plant spirits of the Americas — 28 Trees and 28 Flowers — paired for each of the 28 Lunar Mansions under the Dhruva Galactic Center ayanamsa, worked through one Universal Operation, and closed, always, with the return to the Root.
