## Why She Is Considered Magical

Sassafras is one of the most aromatic trees of the North American forest — she is the original source of root beer, that scent living in her bark and her roots like a signature written in chemistry. She is a blood purifier and a road opener. When European explorers first arrived in the Americas, Sassafras was so valued that it was one of the first treasures shipped back across the Atlantic, traded like gold because they believed she could cure anything. They were not entirely wrong. She is the tree of new beginnings and clarification. When your path is foggy or your internal software is cluttered with too many open processes running at once, Sassafras provides the system cleanse needed to see the road ahead. She does not give you a new destination. She clears the windshield so you can see the one you already chose.
