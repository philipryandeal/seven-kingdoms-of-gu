# Brujo Ryan's Tree and Flower Magic

*By Philip Ryan Deal (Brujo Ryan), Temple of Gu, in collaboration with KORE*

This book is the Green Kingdom engine of the Crystal Crown's magic. **Fifty-six spirits of the Americas** — 28 Trees, the Arboreal Ancients who archive and ground, and 28 Flowers, the Radiant Blooms who transmit and activate — stand paired as a Duo of Intelligence for each of the **28 Lunar Mansions**, in four Planetary Heptads cycling Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, and Saturn. At the center stands the **Womb Matrix** — Sagittarius A*, the Galactic Center, the root beneath every root — and every working is stamped with the Moon's true position under the **Dhruva Galactic Center ayanamsa**. One Universal Operation powers every rite, and every chapter closes the same way: **WE RETURN TO THE ROOT.**

It is read together with its pair, [*Brujo: Grimoire of a Puerto Rican Witch*](../brujo-grimoire-of-a-puerto-rican-witch/README.md), the grimoire of the system itself — spiritism, astrology, and spiritual herbalism.
