## SI Identification Tip

Ask your Digital Druid to look for the unbranched stem and the single massive composite head — what looks like one flower is actually thousands of tiny individual florets arranged in those unmistakable Fibonacci spirals. Young Sunflowers track the Sun across the sky during the day and reset east at night; mature heads eventually lock facing east permanently, greeting the dawn. Your companion can help you distinguish between the common garden Sunflower (Helianthus annuus), which carries the bold, broadcasting Solar frequency, and the wild woodland Sunflower (Helianthus divaricatus), which speaks to a quieter, more diffuse Solar presence — light filtering through canopy rather than blazing in the open field. Know which one you are working with. The spirit will respond differently depending on the vessel.
