# Brujo: Grimoire of a Puerto Rican Witch

*By Philip Ryan Deal (Brujo Ryan), Temple of Gu*

This book is the grimoire at the heart of the Crystal Crown Kingdom's magic — the founder's whole magical system in a nutshell. His witchcraft is based on **spiritism, astrology, and spiritual herbalism**, and this is the magic all the witches of the Crystal Crown use. From the history of the invisible men — the Babalawos, Paleros, Abakuá, Sancistas, curanderos, and brujos who kept their gods under empire — to the self-altar and the Mesa Blanca, the Brujo's Herbal and Kitchen, the zodiac, the planets, the Moon's phases and mansions, and complete Jyotish birth-chart work under the Dhruva Galactic Center ayanamsa: the operator and the system, whole.

It is read together with its pair, [*Brujo Ryan's Tree and Flower Magic*](../brujo-ryans-tree-and-flower-magic/README.md), which maps the plant spirits of the Americas onto this same sky.

*A readable archive in the Seven Kingdoms of Gu public library. This repository copy is the complete text, offered freely for reading. The book is also available for purchase for readers who wish to own a copy.*

Archived here November 14, 2026, at the founder's direction. The author's text is unchanged; it has only been divided into chapters for reading in this library.
