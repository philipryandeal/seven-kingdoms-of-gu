# Putting It All Together

You now have every piece of the system. You have the zodiac. You have the planets. You have the Moon — her phases and her twenty-seven mansions. You have the planetary hours and the days of the week. You have the Gunas. You have the herbal correspondences. You have the Dhruva ayanamsa anchoring it all to the Galactic Center.

Now what do you do with it?

You cast a spell.

## The Method

Here is the method I use every time I plan a working. It is simple. It is sequential. It works. Do not overcomplicate it.

**Step One — Pick your spell.** Decide what your intention is and how you want to express it in ritual. What do you need? Money. Protection. Love. Healing. A road opened. An enemy stopped. A blessing on your home. Get specific. "I want more money" is not a spell. "I want the job interview on the fourteenth to result in an offer" is a spell. "I want protection" is not a spell. "I want my home sealed against the spiritual interference that has been disrupting my sleep for two weeks" is a spell. Specificity is the difference between sending a letter with an address and throwing a letter into the wind.

**Step Two — Pick your Moon phase.** Is this a building working or a diminishing working? If you are drawing something toward you — money, love, opportunity, health, strength — work during the waxing Moon or the Full Moon. If you are pushing something away — an enemy, a sickness, a bad habit, a hex, a blockage — work during the waning Moon. If you are starting something entirely new, the New Moon is your window. If you are charging or consecrating, the Full Moon is your night.

Then refine. Check the nakshatra. Is the Moon in a mansion that supports the kind of work you are doing? If you are doing a money working and the Moon is in Rohini — which favors building finances — you are in the sweet spot. If the Moon is in Bharani — which favors destructive magic — your money working is fighting the current. Wait a day. The Moon moves fast. A better mansion is always coming.

At minimum, check that the nakshatra does not actively oppose your intention. You do not need perfect alignment every time. But you should never work *against* the mansion's energy when you could wait twenty-four hours and work *with* it.

**Step Three — Pick your planet.** Which planetary energy are you trying to harmonize with? Money working — Jupiter (expansion, abundance) or Venus (attraction, magnetism). Protection working — Mars (force, defense) or Saturn (structure, endurance). Love working — Venus. Communication working — Mercury. Healing working — the Moon or the Sun, depending on the type of healing. Once you have identified the planet, you know two things: the day and the hour.

Cast the spell on the planet's day. A Jupiter working on Thursday. A Venus working on Friday. A Mars working on Tuesday. Then calculate the planetary hour — use Deluxe Moon or any planetary hours app — and perform the working during that planet's hour on that planet's day. Thursday in the hour of Jupiter. Friday in the hour of Venus. Tuesday in the hour of Mars. That double signal — the day and the hour aligned — is the strongest planetary frequency you can work with.

**Step Four — Gather your materials.** Now you know the what, the when, and the planetary energy. Pull the corresponding materials. What color candle does this planet use? What herbs? What incense? What stones, if any? All of this is in the Planets chapter. Dress your candle with the appropriate infused oil from your kitchen. Select herbs from your stock that carry the right planetary signature. Prepare any baths, floor washes, or sachets you will need. If you are working with spirits — ancestors, Orisha, Misterios, the Nfumbe — prepare their offerings: food, drink, cigars, rum, whatever they take.

**Step Five — Perform the ritual.** At the designated time, in the designated space, with your materials prepared and your intention clear — do the work. Light the candles. Burn the incense. Speak your petition. Make your offerings. Pray. Sit with the spirits. Do whatever the working requires, fully and with attention. Do not rush. Do not cut corners. Do not substitute materials because you were too lazy to go to the store. The sky is aligned. Your altar is set. Your hands are in the work. Be present for it.

**Step Six — Record everything.** Immediately after the working, open your grimoire and write it down. Date. Moon phase. Nakshatra. Planetary day. Planetary hour. Materials used. What you did. What you felt. What the spirits said or did not say. This is your data. Without it, you are guessing. With it, you are building a verified record of what works for you, with your spirits, on your altar, in your specific magical practice.

**Step Seven — Watch and follow up.** Give the working time. Check back in your grimoire after a week, after two weeks, after a month. What happened? Did the situation shift? Did the money come? Did the protection hold? Did the enemy stop? Did something unexpected happen that might be the spirits answering in a way you did not anticipate? Write the follow-up alongside the original entry. This is how your grimoire becomes an almanac — not just a record of what you did, but a record of *what happened when you did it*.

## A Worked Example

Let me walk you through a real example so you can see how the pieces fit.

**The situation:** You have been struggling financially. Bills are tight. You need a shift — not a windfall, but a sustained improvement in your financial flow.

**Step One — The spell:** A money-drawing working. Specifically: "I petition for steady, sustainable improvement in my financial situation over the next three months."

**Step Two — The Moon:** You need a waxing Moon because you are building, not diminishing. You check your app. The Moon is currently waning — wrong phase. You wait. In five days, the New Moon arrives. Two days after that, the Moon will be waxing and entering the nakshatra of Rohini, which explicitly favors building finances. That is your window.

**Step Three — The planet:** Jupiter. Abundance, expansion, long-term growth. Jupiter's day is Thursday. You check the planetary hours for that Thursday. The hour of Jupiter falls at 9:45 AM. That is your time.

**Step Four — Materials:** A blue candle (Jupiter's color). Dressed with an infused oil of basil and cinnamon in olive oil (money-drawing herbs with solar and Venusian warmth). Sage incense (Jupiter's herb). A glass of water on the bóveda for the ancestors. A small dish of honey as an offering of sweetness. A petition paper with your specific intention written clearly.

**Step Five — The working:** Thursday morning. Moon waxing in Rohini. Hour of Jupiter begins at 9:45 AM. You light the bóveda candle and greet the ancestors. You tell them what you are doing and ask for their support. You light the sage incense. You dress the blue candle with the basil-cinnamon oil, rubbing from base to wick — drawing toward you. You place the petition paper under the candle. You light the candle at 9:45. You speak the petition aloud, once, clearly. You offer the honey. You sit for ten to fifteen minutes and let the work settle. You thank the ancestors. You let the candle burn down if you can, or snuff it and relight it in subsequent Jupiter hours until it is consumed.

**Step Six — Record:** You write everything down. Date, phase (waxing, two days past New Moon), nakshatra (Rohini), day (Thursday), hour (Jupiter, 9:45 AM), materials (blue candle, basil-cinnamon oil, sage incense, honey offering, petition paper), what you did, what you felt.

**Step Seven — Follow up:** Over the next three months, you track shifts. A freelance opportunity appears. A debt you forgot about gets forgiven. Your hours increase at work. Your spending naturally tightens without effort. You write each development in your grimoire next to the original entry. After three months, you read the whole sequence and assess: did the working land? What would you do differently? What would you repeat?

That is the system. That is astro-rootwork in action.

## When the Timing Is Not Perfect

I need to say this plainly because I do not want this system to paralyze you.

You will not always get perfect timing. The Moon will not always be in the ideal nakshatra on the right day in the right hour. Life does not arrange itself around your altar schedule. The car breaks down on a Wednesday and you need a Mars protection working *today*, not next Tuesday. The client calls in crisis and needs spiritual help *now*, not when Rohini rolls around next month.

Do the work.

Imperfect timing with strong intention, clear petition, and genuine spiritual connection will outperform perfect timing with weak intention every single time. The celestial timing is the wind at your back. It is not the boat, and it is not the sailor. *You* are the sailor. Your relationship with your spirits is the boat. The timing is the wind. When the wind is with you, the sailing is easier and faster. When the wind is against you, you row harder. But you still get there.

The ancestors I wrote about in the history chapter — Don Pedrito, El Niño Fidencio, Tonio Lacén, Juan Cob — did not have astrology apps on their phones. They did not calculate planetary hours. They healed and divined and fought on the spiritual plane and their magic *worked* because they were in alignment with forces they felt in their bodies and received from their spirits. The system I am teaching you does not replace that alignment. It supports it. It gives you a framework for being intentional about your timing. It stacks the odds. But the odds were never the whole story.

The whole story is you, your altar, your dead, your spirits, and the work of your hands. Everything else — the nakshatras, the planetary hours, the herbal correspondences, the Galactic Center — is the frame around the painting. The painting is your practice.

## There Is Never a Bad Time to Take Care of Yourself

One more thing before you move on.

Everything I have said about timing applies to *targeted* workings — spells with specific intentions aimed at specific outcomes in the external world. Money workings. Protection workings. Love workings. Court case workings. These benefit from celestial timing because they are trying to move something in a specific direction, and the sky can help or hinder that movement.

But magic that is for *you* — for your mental, emotional, physical, and spiritual health — can be done anytime. There is no wrong Moon phase for a spiritual bath to cleanse your own energy. There is no wrong nakshatra for sitting at your bóveda and talking to your dead. There is no wrong planetary hour for lighting a candle on your self-altar and asking your Orí how you are doing. Self-care magic is always in season. Do not wait for the perfect window to take care of yourself. Take care of yourself now. The stars will not judge you for it.

Offensive and protection magic — the regular maintenance of your spiritual defenses — is also always appropriate. Keep your house washed. Keep your resguardo fed. Keep your protections active. You do not wait for Tuesday in the hour of Mars to lock your front door at night. You lock it every night because that is what responsible people do. Your spiritual protections are the same. Maintain them daily. Use the planetary timing when you are doing *intensive* protection work — building a new shield, confronting a specific threat, sealing after a heavy attack. But the daily maintenance? Just do it.
