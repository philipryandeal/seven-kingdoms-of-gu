# Planet / Sign Analysis

## Aries

The indication is that you can be manipulative or say what you must to get what you want, especially sex. And if boredom sets in you can generate risky situations to feel alive. When you get set back it frustrates you. It also makes you a gambler or risk taker. You might turn to substances to calm yourself down. So be careful with overusing alcohol or cannabis to calm yourself. The combination of Mars and Mercurial energies makes you brilliant like a shooting star. That could lead you to depression, and depression is an indication in people who have Saturn in Aries. Keep your chin up, it's good for posture if nothing else.
