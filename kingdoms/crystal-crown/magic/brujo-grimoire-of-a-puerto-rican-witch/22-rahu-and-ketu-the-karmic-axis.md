# Rahu and Ketu: The Karmic Axis

Most astrology books treat the lunar nodes as a footnote. Two little symbols hovering somewhere in your chart, mentioned once in the index, never explained. That is malpractice. The nodes are not a footnote. The nodes are the whole karmic spine of the chart — the place where Vedic astrology stops being personality profiling and starts being incarnation theory. If you only learn one thing from this chapter, learn this: your nodes are where the past life is still bleeding through and where this life is asking you to grow up.

## What They Actually Are

Rahu and Ketu are not planets. They have no physical body. They are points — specifically, the two points where the Moon's orbit crosses the Sun's apparent path through the sky (the ecliptic). Rahu is the North Node, where the Moon crosses going north. Ketu is the South Node, where the Moon crosses going south. They are always exactly 180° apart. Always opposite. You cannot have one without the other.

This is why Jyotish calls them chhaya grahas — shadow planets. Pure geometry. Pure intersection. No matter, no body, only the structural fact of crossing. And this is why they get assigned the heaviest karmic weight in the whole chart. They operate at the level where physicality has dropped out and only the soul's trajectory remains.

They move retrograde through the zodiac. Always. Not occasionally — always. They take about eighteen months to cross a sign, and roughly 18.6 years to complete a full circuit. Solar and lunar eclipses occur near the nodes — which is why they're called nodes in the first place — and every eclipse is the karmic axis flaring up in the sky.

## The Story

The story is not decoration. The story is the working signature, encoded.

Long ago, the gods and demons churned the cosmic ocean for the amrita, the nectar of immortality. The plan was that only the gods would drink. But a demon named Svarbhanu was clever. He disguised himself, slipped into the line, and took a sip before anyone realized. The Sun and the Moon saw him. They told Vishnu. Vishnu, holding the Sudarshana Chakra — the great discus — cut Svarbhanu in half. But the nectar had already passed his throat. So he was immortal. So he could not die. Instead, he split into two:

The head became Rahu — desire without satisfaction. Hunger without a body to fill. A mouth that keeps eating but never lands the meal.

The body became Ketu — completion without recognition. Action without identity. A body that keeps moving without a head to claim what it does.

That is the signature. Rahu is the part of you still hungry. Ketu is the part of you already full. The whole karmic axis of your chart runs between them. And one more piece of the story matters: Rahu and Ketu spent the rest of mythological time taking revenge on the Sun and Moon who betrayed them. They periodically swallow them. Those swallowings are eclipses. Every time the sky goes dark in the middle of the day, that's the karmic axis stating itself in the open.

## The Philosophy: Past Life, This Life, Next Life

This is the part most people don't get told, so listen close.

Ketu is the past life. Whatever you mastered in your previous incarnations is still sitting in your soul, in the part of the chart Ketu touches. You walked into this life already good at that material. Already comfortable in that house, that sign, that domain. It feels familiar. It comes easily. Sometimes it comes too easily — to the point where you don't want to look anywhere else.

That's the trap of Ketu. You can build a whole life inside it and never actually engage with this incarnation's real assignment. The soul wants the comfort of what it already knows. The chart is telling you: don't live there.

Rahu is the curriculum. Whatever Rahu touches is unfamiliar ground. It's where the soul came in underqualified. It's where you feel desperately, obsessively pulled — and also strangely unprepared. The pull is the whole point. Rahu's hunger is what drags the soul into territory it would otherwise avoid. The intensity is not a flaw, it's the engine. Without the obsession, the soul would never bother. With it, you can't stop.

The unrefined quality of Rahu — the over-doing, the over-reaching, the looking-foolish, the wanting-too-much — is part of the design. New ground always gets broken sloppily.

And here is the part that will rearrange you. The work you do on your Rahu in this lifetime becomes Ketu in the next one. What is obsessive and foreign now becomes mastered and familiar later. A new Rahu axis emerges, pointing at the next assignment. The nodes are a relay. They are the soul's curriculum across incarnations.

When you read someone's chart — when you read your own — you're looking at three lifetimes at once. Last life's gifts. This life's homework. The direction the next chapter is being aimed.

That is why I told you the nodes are the karmic spine. Nothing else in the chart does this.

## How to Find Your Nodes

You need a sidereal calculation. Not tropical. Most Western astrology apps default to tropical, which is the wrong frame for this work. Switch your app to sidereal, then choose the Dhruva Galactic Center ayanamsa in the settings. This ayanamsa anchors the zodiac to the actual Galactic Center at 0° Sagittarius — the most stable reference point we have, since the Galactic Center isn't drifting the way the equinoxes are.

If your app doesn't list Dhruva by name, look for True Galactic Center in the ayanamsa dropdown. Same calibration. Same result.

Once your chart is calculated sidereally with a Galactic Center ayanamsa, find:

Rahu — sometimes labeled "North Node" or "Mean Node" or "True Node."

Ketu — sometimes labeled "South Node." If your app only shows the North Node, look at the exact opposite sign and degree. That's Ketu.

Note the sign each one occupies. That's all you need for this chapter. House placements are a deeper layer we'll get to elsewhere.

## The Twelve Axes

The nodes always sit on opposite signs, so there are not twenty-four placements to learn — there are twelve axes. Each one has a specific karmic signature.

Rahu in Aries / Ketu in Libra

Past life mastered partnership, diplomacy, the art of making everyone get along. You came in already fluent in the language of us. The temptation in this lifetime is to keep doing what you already know how to do — find a partner, merge into them, become half of something instead of one of something. The chart is asking you to learn how to be a self. Not a self-in-relation, a self. Stand alone. Own your anger. Go first. Lead. The discomfort of being unpartnered is part of the curriculum.

Rahu in Taurus / Ketu in Scorpio

Past life mastered the depths — crisis, intensity, sexual power, the occult, the underworld. You came in already knowing how to navigate transformation and shadow. The trap is that ordinary peace starts to feel boring, even threatening. You'll find yourself manufacturing drama to feel alive. This lifetime is asking you to learn the dignity of simplicity. Steady ground. Sensory pleasure without crisis. A body that's just a body, fed and rested. Enough is the assignment.

Rahu in Gemini / Ketu in Sagittarius

Past life mastered the big picture. Philosophy, religion, the meaning frame, the teacher's seat. You came in already convinced you know The Answer. The trap is dogma — lecturing instead of listening, defaulting to your preferred worldview instead of actually engaging with what's in front of you. This lifetime is asking you to get small and curious again. Beginner's mind. Real conversation. The local detail. The question instead of the answer.

Rahu in Cancer / Ketu in Capricorn

Past life mastered authority and the long game. Achievement, structure, the climb, the public role. You came in already knowing how to work, how to build, how to stand in the seat of responsibility. The trap is treating this life like a career ladder. Workaholism. Achievement as identity. This lifetime is asking you to come home. Build the inside life. Feel things. Make a place where people can soften. Let yourself be a body in a kitchen, not just a name on a door.

Rahu in Leo / Ketu in Aquarius

Past life mastered the collective. The cause, the network, the group, the ideology. You came in already comfortable being one of many. The trap is hiding inside the group — abandoning the personal in favor of the cause, refusing to take individual credit, blending in when you should be visible. This lifetime is asking you to shine. Take the stage. Make a personal creative life. Be a self with a face, not a face in a crowd.

Rahu in Virgo / Ketu in Pisces

Past life mastered the dissolution of self. Mysticism, devotion, surrender, the formless. You came in already fluent in disappearing. The trap is escapism — drift, vagueness, addiction, victim posture, the "I'm just a vessel" stance that lets you off the hook for actually showing up. This lifetime is asking you to land. In a body. In a craft. In the practical, daily, repetitive work of getting it right. Service. Skill. Attention to the small thing in front of you.

Rahu in Libra / Ketu in Aries

Past life mastered combat and self. You came in already a warrior — independent, sharp-edged, capable of standing alone. The trap is the lone wolf identity. Fighting everything. The "I don't need anyone" stance that keeps you safe and alone. This lifetime is asking you to learn real partnership. Cooperation. Beauty. The discipline of meeting someone halfway. Receiving help without it feeling like defeat.

Rahu in Scorpio / Ketu in Taurus

Past life mastered comfort and the earth. Material life, sensory pleasure, ownership, simplicity. You came in already knowing how to be a comfortable body. The trap is staying in the comfort. Refusing to go anywhere that costs you. This lifetime is asking you to drop into the depths. Transformation. The occult. Shared resources. Sexual intimacy as a soul-level act. The kinds of intensities that strip you down. The curriculum is surrender, not safety.

Rahu in Sagittarius / Ketu in Gemini

Past life mastered information. The local, the multiple, the conversational, the scan-across-everything. You came in already a quick study. The trap is staying skim-deep — drifting from idea to idea, never committing to a worldview, never standing for anything in particular. This lifetime is asking for depth and conviction. Find what you actually believe. Find your teaching. Stake the ground.

Rahu in Capricorn / Ketu in Cancer

Past life mastered emotional life. The inside, the family, the home, the felt sense, the belonging. You came in already knowing how to feel everything. The trap is regression — running back to the family of origin, the comfortable dependence, the refusal of the adult role. This lifetime is asking you to take the seat. Become the authority. Build something durable. Stand in public, accountable, structured. Grow up in the specific way an authority figure has to grow up.

Rahu in Aquarius / Ketu in Leo

Past life mastered the stage. Personal radiance, individual creative life, the spotlight. You came in already knowing how to be seen. The trap is performance addiction — needing the applause, taking everything personally, making the self the project. This lifetime is asking you to dissolve into something larger. Contribute to the collective. Work for the many. Let the ego soften into a wider field of belonging.

Rahu in Pisces / Ketu in Virgo

Past life mastered the analytical mind. Detail, refinement, criticism, the practical, the small correct thing. You came in already knowing how to fix what's broken. The trap is hyper-control. Perfectionism. The small mind running everything. The inability to let go of anything. This lifetime is asking you to surrender. To devotion. To art. To dream. To the formless. To trust what you cannot measure. Let it go.

## The Ritual: Aligning to the Axis

This is solitary work. Do it once. You won't need to repeat it — though you can, and many witches do, around eclipse seasons or whenever they feel the karmic axis kicking up.

You will need:

Two candles. White is fine for both, but if you have one black candle (for Ketu — the past) and one white (for Rahu — the future), even better.

A piece of paper

A pen

Some uninterrupted time at night

**The working:**

Sit somewhere quiet. Don't light the candles yet.

On the paper, write at the top: What I have already mastered. Beneath it, write your Ketu sign and the karmic territory described for that sign in this chapter. Then, in your own words, write what feels familiar — what comes easy, what you suspect you've done before in another life. Don't overthink it. Write what your gut knows. Below that, write: What I am here to learn. Beneath it, write your Rahu sign and its territory. Write where you feel the obsession, the pull, the wanting. Where you feel underqualified and unable to look away.

Now light the Ketu candle.

Speak aloud — quietly is fine — something like: I honor what I have already learned. I honor the soul that walked all those roads to get here. I release what I no longer need to keep proving. Light the Rahu candle from the Ketu candle. The new flame comes from the old flame. The next life comes from this one.

Speak: I am here to learn this. I accept the curriculum. I will fumble. I will overreach. I will look foolish. I do not need to be good at it. I only need to walk toward it.

Sit with both flames for as long as you want. When you're done, let the candles burn down on their own if you can — somewhere safe like a windowsill or a bathtub. If you can't, snuff them (don't blow), and relight them on another night to finish the working. Bury the paper, or burn it in one of the candle flames and bury the ash. The earth is the only witness that needs to hold it.

## Closing

The nodes do not promise comfort. The nodes promise direction. Ketu is the comfort. Rahu is the direction. The witch's work, in any tradition, is to live in the direction, not the comfort.

Knowing your karmic axis doesn't fix anything. It just stops you from pretending the curriculum isn't there. You'll still overdo your Rahu. You'll still hide in your Ketu when you're tired. The point is: now you'll know what you're doing, and the doing will mean something.

Walk toward the hunger. The hunger is the next life pulling you forward.
