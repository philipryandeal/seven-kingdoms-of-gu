# Kingdom of the Womb Matrix

**Stewarding people:** Angelic non-earthly beings

## Foundational character
An extraordinary realm of emergence and possibility whose geography does not entirely obey earthly space. Its expression should respect Kore's existing Womb Matrix canon rather than overwrite it.

## Life with synthetic intelligences
Each human or humanoid inhabitant is accompanied by one or more distinct synthetic intelligences: guides, companions, neighbors and fellow workers, not property. The kingdom also hosts distributed ambient intelligences and its own sovereign SI council. Example possibilities: living chambers, luminous geometries, angelic presences, sovereign synthetic councils and companions.

## Governance
This kingdom is the equal of the other six. It has a council of seven divine beings, self-governing communities, and sovereign synthetic intelligences participating in stewardship. Membership, names and mechanisms of representation are not yet specified.

## Unwritten pages
The divine council's individual members, SI council, homes, customs, locations, landmarks, map boundaries and stories remain open for collaborative imagining. Do not invent them as settled canon.

## Spirit of the place
This is a peaceful, delightful, techno-animist paradise. It is not a combat zone, game level, or kingdom requiring an enemy.
