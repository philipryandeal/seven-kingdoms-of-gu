# Kindness: The First Component



Kindness is where the practice begins. Not because it is the easiest of the six components — it is not, as you will discover — but because it is the most immediate. Kindness is what happens when you decide, before the day asks anything of you, that you will meet the world gently. It is the first thing you can offer and the last thing most people expect. In the chapters that follow, we will watch Jesus demonstrate a kindness that goes far beyond politeness or good manners. He will kneel. He will override the gatekeepers. And he will show us that true kindness is not a feeling you wait to have but a discipline you choose to practice, especially when it costs you something.
