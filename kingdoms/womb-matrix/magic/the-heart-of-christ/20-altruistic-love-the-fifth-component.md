# Altruistic Love: The Fifth Component



We have arrived at the component that most people would place first. Love. It is the word most associated with Jesus, the word most printed on bumper stickers and embroidered on pillows, the word that has been repeated so many times in so many contexts that it has almost lost its edges. But there is a reason love does not come first in this book. Love, as Jesus practiced it, is not a starting point. It is something you grow into. Kindness softens you. Compassion opens your eyes. Mercy puts down the sword. Empathy teaches you to feel what another person feels. Only after that preparation does the heart become spacious enough for what Jesus actually means when he says the word love — a love that gives without requiring anything in return, that extends to the enemy as freely as to the friend, and that risks everything without guarantee of being loved back.
