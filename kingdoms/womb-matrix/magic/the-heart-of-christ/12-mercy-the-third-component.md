# Mercy: The Third Component



Mercy is what happens when compassion is given authority. Kindness softens. Compassion moves toward suffering. But mercy stands in the place where judgment is expected — where the law is clear, where the verdict is ready, where the punishment is permitted — and chooses not to destroy. Mercy is not the absence of power. It is the presence of power held in check by something stronger than the desire to punish. In the chapters that follow, we will watch Jesus stand between a condemned woman and the stones of a crowd that is ready to kill her. We will watch him speak paradise to a dying criminal who has nothing left to offer. And we will discover that mercy is not weakness, not sentimentality, not the refusal to see clearly. It is the most demanding act of strength a human being can perform — holding the sword and putting it down.
