# Compassion: The Second Component



Compassion is what happens when kindness opens its eyes. Kindness softens you — it teaches the heart to meet the world gently, to use power in the service of dignity, to override the gatekeepers. But compassion asks something more. Compassion asks you to see suffering and move toward it rather than away. It is the refusal to cross to the other side of the road when someone is bleeding in the ditch. In the chapters that follow, we will watch Jesus demonstrate a compassion that is not sentimental, not distant, not performed for an audience. He will reach out and touch the untouchable. He will tell a story that dismantles every excuse we have for looking away. And he will show us that compassion is not a feeling you wait to have — it is a direction you choose to walk.
