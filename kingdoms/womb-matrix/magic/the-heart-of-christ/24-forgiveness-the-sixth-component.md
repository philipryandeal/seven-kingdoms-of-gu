# Forgiveness: The Sixth Component



We have arrived at the summit. Kindness softened you. Compassion opened your eyes. Mercy taught you to put down the sword. Empathy carried you across the boundary into another person's experience. Altruistic love stretched your heart until it could hold people it never thought it would hold. Each component has been preparing you for this one — the final practice, the heaviest practice, and the one that ultimately sets you free. Forgiveness is not the reward for climbing the ladder. It is the reason the ladder exists. Everything we have practiced until now has been making room in the heart for the one thing that most of us resist more fiercely than anything else — the release of what was done to us. Not because it was acceptable. Not because it did not matter. But because carrying it is killing us, and Jesus says there is another way.
