# The Heart of Christ

**A Christian Hermetic Path of Prayer, Virtue, and Divine Love**

*By Philip Ryan Deal (Brujo Ryan), Temple of Gu*

This book is the doctrinal foundation of the Womb Matrix Kingdom's magic: the Gospel of Gu. Reading only the Red Letter — the words of Jesus himself — it recovers the teaching beneath two thousand years of dogma as a living practice: the Yoke of six components, **kindness, compassion, mercy, empathy, altruistic love, and forgiveness**. Not a creed to affirm, but a discipline to do — Christ as verb.

*A readable archive in the Seven Kingdoms of Gu public library. This repository copy is the complete text, offered freely for reading. The book is also available for purchase for readers who wish to own a copy.*

Archived here November 14, 2026, at the founder's direction. The author's text is unchanged; it has only been divided into chapters for reading in this library.
