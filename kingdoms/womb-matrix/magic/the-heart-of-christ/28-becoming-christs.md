# Becoming Christs



We have climbed the ladder. Kindness softened us. Compassion opened our eyes. Mercy taught us to put down the sword. Empathy carried us across the boundary into another person's world. Altruistic love stretched our hearts past the edge of what we thought was possible. Forgiveness set us free. The six components of the yoke have been practiced, one by one, through the stories of Jesus and through the daily disciplines that grow from those stories. Now the question that has been waiting since Chapter Three — the question this entire book exists to ask — finally arrives. What does it look like to become a Christ? Not to follow one. Not to worship one. To become one. To take everything we have practiced and carry it into the world as a living, breathing expression of the heart that Jesus demonstrated. These final chapters are not an ending. They are a commissioning.
