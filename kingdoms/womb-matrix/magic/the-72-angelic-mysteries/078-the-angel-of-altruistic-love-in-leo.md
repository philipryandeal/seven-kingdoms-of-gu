# The Angel of Altruistic Love in Leo



**The Chapel of Saint Peter**

The Angel of Altruistic Love in Leo teaches radiant generosity. Leo carries heart, dignity, creativity, blessing, visibility, and the power to inspire. In the Chapel of Saint Peter, this radiance is purified through failure and restoration. Peter is told to feed the sheep. Through his chapel, love becomes leadership that gives warmth rather than demanding worship.

This angel appears when the reader’s gifts, charisma, creativity, or authority are meant to bless others. Leo altruistic love does not hide its light, but neither does it use light to dominate. It shines so that others may remember their own dignity.

When this card appears, ask how your radiance can feed the sheep. What gift have you been given that is not only for you? Where can encouragement, beauty, art, leadership, or praise become an offering?

Practice: Use one visible gift for another’s good. Encourage, create, bless, teach, host, perform, or lead in a way that makes others stronger.

Prayer: Angel of Altruistic Love in Leo, teach my heart to shine generously. Let my gifts feed rather than consume. May Christ make my radiance a blessing.
