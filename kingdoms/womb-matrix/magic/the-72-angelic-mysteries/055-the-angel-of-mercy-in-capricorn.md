# The Angel of Mercy in Capricorn



**The Chapel of Saint Matthew**

The Angel of Mercy in Capricorn teaches mercy as accountable rebuilding. Capricorn carries responsibility, structure, authority, consequence, time, and the labor required to restore trust. In the Chapel of Saint Matthew, this mercy is shaped by conversion. Matthew leaves the tax table and becomes an apostle. Through his chapel, mercy does not erase the past. It gives the soul a new vocation.

This angel appears when mercy requires maturity. The reader may need to accept consequences, rebuild slowly, make restitution, create better structures, or submit to a process of repair. Capricorn mercy is not sentimental. It understands that trust may need time. It honors the weight of harm by making restoration disciplined.

When this card appears, ask where repentance must become structure. What must change so the same harm is not repeated? What system, boundary, habit, or commitment is required? Mercy becomes credible when it can endure.

Practice: Write one concrete rebuilding plan. Include what must stop, what must begin, who must be protected, and how accountability will be practiced. Offer the plan to Christ.

Prayer: Angel of Mercy in Capricorn, teach me to rebuild with integrity. Let consequence become conversion. May Christ make my responsibility a vessel of mercy.
