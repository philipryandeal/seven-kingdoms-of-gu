# The Angel of Kindness in Leo



**The Chapel of Saint Peter**

The Angel of Kindness in Leo teaches the kindness of generous radiance. Leo carries the fire of the heart, the dignity of the person, and the power to bless through presence. In the Chapel of Saint Peter, this radiance is placed in service to the Church of love. Peter is passionate, flawed, restored, and called to feed the sheep. Through his chapel, kindness becomes leadership that warms rather than dominates.

This angel appears when the reader is asked to use influence kindly. Visibility is not evil. Power is not evil. Charisma is not evil. But all three must be converted. The kind leader does not make others smaller in order to feel large. The kind leader helps others remember their own dignity.

When this card appears, ask how your light affects the room. Are you performing, competing, demanding attention, or truly blessing? The Angel of Kindness in Leo invites the soul to praise others, encourage growth, and share warmth without needing worship.

Practice: Offer sincere encouragement to someone whose light you respect. Do not flatter. Bless. Name the good you see in them and ask for nothing in return.

Prayer: Angel of Kindness in Leo, teach my heart to shine without pride. Let my warmth restore dignity. May my strength feed Christ’s sheep.
