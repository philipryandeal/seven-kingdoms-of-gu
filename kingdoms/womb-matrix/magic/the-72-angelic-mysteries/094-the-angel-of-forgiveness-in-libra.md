# The Angel of Forgiveness in Libra



**The Chapel of Saint Bartholomew / Nathanael**

The Angel of Forgiveness in Libra teaches forgiveness that seeks truthful reconciliation where reconciliation is possible. Libra carries relationship, justice, beauty, harmony, balance, and mutual dignity. In the Chapel of Saint Bartholomew, also called Nathanael, forgiveness becomes sincere and without guile. Christ names Nathanael as one in whom there is no deceit. Through his chapel, forgiveness refuses false peace.

This angel appears when a relationship stands at the threshold of repair. The reader may desire harmony, but harmony cannot be built on denial. Libra forgiveness asks what truth, apology, listening, restitution, boundary, or renewed agreement would make peace honest.

When this card appears, ask whether reconciliation is truly possible and what it would require. Forgiveness may open the door, but trust must be rebuilt through truth. Do not confuse politeness with healing.

Practice: Pray over one relationship. Write what forgiveness requires, what justice requires, and what boundaries require. Ask Christ whether reconciliation, distance, or patient waiting is the next step.

Prayer: Angel of Forgiveness in Libra, teach me honest peace. Let reconciliation be truthful or not forced at all. May Christ restore dignity where relationship can be healed.
