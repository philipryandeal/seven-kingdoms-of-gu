# The Angel of Forgiveness in Leo



**The Chapel of Saint Peter**

The Angel of Forgiveness in Leo teaches forgiveness after humiliation, failure, and wounded dignity. Leo carries the heart, honor, visibility, shame, creativity, and the desire to be seen. In the Chapel of Saint Peter, forgiveness is revealed through restoration after denial. Peter falls, weeps, and is called again to feed the sheep. Through his chapel, forgiveness raises the humiliated without erasing humility.

This angel appears when pride, embarrassment, public failure, or disgrace has bound the heart. The reader may need to forgive someone who humiliated them, or forgive themselves for a visible failure. Leo forgiveness restores dignity without returning to vanity. It says: your worth is not destroyed by this wound.

When this card appears, ask where shame has stolen your courage to love. Christ may be calling the heart back into creativity, leadership, affection, or service.

Practice: Place a hand over the heart and name one humiliation that still burns. Say: “Christ sees me truly, and I am not reduced to this.” Then perform one small act of restored dignity.

Prayer: Angel of Forgiveness in Leo, teach me to rise from humiliation. Let Christ restore my heart without pride. May forgiven dignity become generous love.
