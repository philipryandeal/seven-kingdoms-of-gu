# The Angel of Altruistic Love in Virgo



**The Chapel of Saint Philip**

The Angel of Altruistic Love in Virgo teaches love as humble service. Virgo carries care, detail, healing, order, discernment, and the sacred usefulness of small acts. In the Chapel of Saint Philip, this service becomes invitation. Philip says, “Come and see,” guiding another toward Christ without force. Through his chapel, altruistic love serves quietly and precisely.

This angel appears when love is asking for work that may not be glamorous. Something needs cleaning, organizing, correcting, preparing, editing, healing, or tending. Virgo altruistic love does not despise the small task. It knows that hidden labor can carry great grace.

When this card appears, ask what service would truly help. Do not perform usefulness. Be useful. Do not resent the smallness of the task. Let the detail become devotion.

Practice: Perform one humble service without announcing it. Clean, organize, prepare, repair, edit, or assist in a way that makes another person’s burden lighter.

Prayer: Angel of Altruistic Love in Virgo, teach me to serve without resentment. Let my attention become charity. May every small task be placed in Christ’s hands.
