# The Angel of Compassion in Libra



**The Chapel of Saint Bartholomew / Nathanael**

The Angel of Compassion in Libra teaches the compassion of relational justice. Libra carries balance, beauty, mutuality, fairness, and the sacred mirror of the other. In the Chapel of Saint Bartholomew, also called Nathanael, this compassion becomes honest and without guile. Through his chapel, compassion seeks harmony that is truthful, not decorative.

This angel appears when suffering has been caused by imbalance in relationship. Someone may have been dismissed, used, silenced, idealized, or treated unfairly. Libra compassion does not merely soothe the injured person while leaving the injustice intact. It asks what must be rebalanced so dignity can return.

When this card appears, ask where compassion requires fairness. Have you taken more than you have given? Have you avoided someone’s pain because it implicates you? Have you pursued peace without justice? This angel teaches that compassion may require apology, repair, listening, and restored proportion.

Practice: Examine one relationship for imbalance. Make one fair and compassionate adjustment: apologize, give credit, share labor, tell the truth, or listen to the grievance without defensiveness.

Prayer: Angel of Compassion in Libra, teach me to love with fairness. Let my desire for peace become honest. May Christ restore dignity between myself and others.
