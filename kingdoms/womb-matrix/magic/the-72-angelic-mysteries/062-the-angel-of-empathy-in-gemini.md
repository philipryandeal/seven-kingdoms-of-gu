# The Angel of Empathy in Gemini



**The Chapel of Saint Thomas**

The Angel of Empathy in Gemini teaches empathy through language. Gemini carries speech, thought, story, curiosity, questioning, and interpretation. In the Chapel of Saint Thomas, this becomes honest inquiry. Thomas brings his doubt to Christ and is met through the wounds. Through his chapel, empathy learns to ask rather than assume.

This angel appears when understanding depends on conversation. The reader may think they already know what another person means, but Gemini empathy slows the mind and opens the question. Words are bridges, but they can also be masks. The empathetic listener asks with humility and lets the other person define their own experience.

When this card appears, pay attention to the language being used. What words does the person repeat? What do they avoid saying? What meaning are they trying to carry? Do not translate them too quickly into your own categories.

Practice: In one conversation, ask a clarifying question before offering an interpretation. Say, “What did that feel like for you?” or “What do you mean by that?” Then listen.

Prayer: Angel of Empathy in Gemini, teach me to ask with humility. Let my words become bridges, not cages. May Christ guide my listening into truth.
