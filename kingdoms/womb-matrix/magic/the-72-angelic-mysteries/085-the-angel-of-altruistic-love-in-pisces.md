# The Angel of Altruistic Love in Pisces



**The Chapel of Saint Matthias**

The Angel of Altruistic Love in Pisces teaches mystical charity poured out for all beings. Pisces carries prayer, sorrow, imagination, mercy, surrender, and the oceanic tenderness of creation. In the Chapel of Saint Matthias, this love becomes restoration to the circle. Matthias fills the empty place. Through his chapel, altruistic love gathers the forgotten, the lost, and the unseen into prayer.

This angel appears when the heart is called to universal tenderness. The reader may feel love for the dead, the suffering, animals, spirits, strangers, enemies, or the whole wounded world. Pisces altruistic love is beautiful, but it must remain grounded in Christ. Otherwise it may dissolve into overwhelm.

When this card appears, ask how universal love can become a real offering. Prayer, tears, art, rest, intercession, and gentle service may all be forms of charity.

Practice: Sit with a bowl of water. Name beings you wish to bless: living, dead, human, animal, known, unknown. After each name say: “May Christ’s love hold you.”

Prayer: Angel of Altruistic Love in Pisces, teach me boundless charity without self-loss. Gather the forgotten into tenderness. May Christ pour love through my heart for all beings.
