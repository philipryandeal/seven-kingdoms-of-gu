# The Angel of Kindness in Virgo



**The Chapel of Saint Philip**

The Angel of Kindness in Virgo teaches the kindness of humble service. Virgo carries attention, refinement, healing, order, and devotion to the small necessary thing. In the Chapel of Saint Philip, this kindness becomes practical discipleship. Philip says, “Come and see.” He does not force revelation; he invites another into encounter. Through his chapel, kindness becomes careful, useful, and sincere.

This angel appears when love must be expressed through details. The reader may be looking for a grand gesture while neglecting the simple act that would actually help. Virgo teaches that holiness often hides in preparation, cleaning, organizing, editing, tending, and correcting what has been left confused.

When this card appears, ask what small act of service would reduce suffering. Do not announce your virtue. Do the work. Make the space clearer. Make the instructions easier. Make the burden lighter. Let kindness become competence joined to humility.

Practice: Choose one neglected detail and tend it as an offering to Christ. Clean a sacred object, organize a needed tool, prepare medicine, revise careless words, or help someone complete a task.

Prayer: Angel of Kindness in Virgo, teach me to serve without resentment. Let my attention become love. May every small act be placed quietly in the hands of Christ.
