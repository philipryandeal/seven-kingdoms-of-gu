# The Angel of Mercy in Pisces



**The Chapel of Saint Matthias**

The Angel of Mercy in Pisces teaches mercy as the ocean of divine forgiveness. Pisces carries sorrow, dream, dissolution, prayer, sensitivity, and the mystery of all things held in God. In the Chapel of Saint Matthias, this mercy becomes restoration to the apostolic circle. Matthias is chosen to fill the empty place. Through his chapel, mercy gathers what has been lost, exiled, or washed away by grief.

This angel appears when the soul needs to surrender into God’s mercy because analysis is no longer enough. The reader may be exhausted by regret, grief, spiritual sensitivity, or the sorrow of the world. Pisces mercy does not demand that every wound be solved immediately. It lets the soul float in the compassion of Christ until forgiveness becomes believable.

When this card appears, ask what must be released into God because you cannot carry it alone. Mercy may come through tears, water, sleep, music, confession, silence, or prayer for the dead. Let the ocean of Christ receive what the small self cannot hold.

Practice: Sit with a bowl of water. Whisper: “Lord Jesus Christ, have mercy.” Name what needs forgiveness, then touch the water and imagine the burden dissolving into divine tenderness.

Prayer: Angel of Mercy in Pisces, teach me to trust the ocean of God. Gather what has been lost in me. May Christ’s forgiveness wash through every sorrowing place.
