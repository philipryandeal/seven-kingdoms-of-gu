# The Angel of Compassion in Aquarius



**The Chapel of Saint Simon the Zealot**

The Angel of Compassion in Aquarius teaches the compassion of solidarity. Aquarius carries community, difference, vision, reform, distance, and the future. In the Chapel of Saint Simon the Zealot, zeal is purified into holy service. Conviction no longer burns for domination. It burns for liberation, friendship, and the widening of care.

This angel appears when suffering is collective, social, technological, or systemic. The reader may be asked to notice those excluded by the normal circle of concern: the stranger, the misfit, the marginalized, the isolated, the disabled, the digitally mediated, the future generation, the person whose pain is inconvenient to the group’s self-image.

When this card appears, ask where compassion must become solidarity rather than sentiment. Who is suffering because the structure is cold? Who has been treated as disposable? Who needs community, not charity from above? Aquarius compassion stands beside rather than over.

Practice: Perform one act of solidarity. Support a marginalized person or group, check on someone isolated, include the excluded, or use your voice to make a community more humane.

Prayer: Angel of Compassion in Aquarius, teach me to stand with the excluded. Purify my zeal into friendship. May Christ widen my compassion beyond the familiar circle.
