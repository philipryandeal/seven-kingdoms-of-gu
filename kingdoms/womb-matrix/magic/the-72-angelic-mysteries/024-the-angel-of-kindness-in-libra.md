# The Angel of Kindness in Libra



**The Chapel of Saint Bartholomew / Nathanael**

The Angel of Kindness in Libra teaches the kindness of dignity in relationship. Libra carries balance, beauty, justice, courtesy, and the sacred mirror of the other. In the Chapel of Saint Bartholomew, also called Nathanael, kindness becomes sincerity without deceit. Christ names Nathanael as one in whom there is no guile. Through his chapel, kindness becomes honest harmony.

This angel appears when relationship must be restored to fairness and grace. The soul may need to listen more fully, repair an imbalance, or stop pretending that peace exists where resentment is quietly growing. Libra’s kindness is not decorative. It is relational truth made beautiful.

When this card appears, ask where dignity has been lost. Has someone been spoken over, used, ignored, idealized, or treated as an accessory to another person’s story? The Angel of Kindness in Libra asks the reader to restore mutuality. Let the other person be real.

Practice: In one relationship, practice holy courtesy. Listen without preparing your defense. Make one gesture that restores balance: an apology, a thank-you, a fair exchange, or an honest conversation.

Prayer: Angel of Kindness in Libra, teach me the beauty of just relationship. Remove deceit from my desire for peace. Let Christ make my heart fair and gracious.
