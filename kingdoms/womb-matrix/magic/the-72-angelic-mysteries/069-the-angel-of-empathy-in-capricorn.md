# The Angel of Empathy in Capricorn



**The Chapel of Saint Matthew**

The Angel of Empathy in Capricorn teaches empathy with burden, responsibility, and pressure. Capricorn carries labor, time, duty, authority, consequence, and the structures that shape human life. In the Chapel of Saint Matthew, this burden is transformed by vocation. Matthew knew the weight of systems, money, obligation, and social judgment. Through his chapel, empathy learns to see the pressures beneath behavior.

This angel appears when someone seems cold, rigid, controlling, tired, or severe. The reader may judge the surface without understanding the load. Capricorn empathy asks what duty, fear, debt, role, or expectation is pressing on the person. It also asks where structures themselves are causing suffering.

When this card appears, ask what burden has not been named. This does not excuse harshness, but it may reveal the path toward wiser response. Sometimes empathy means helping to carry what is too heavy.

Practice: Identify one burden carried by yourself or another. Name it without judgment. Then ask what practical support, boundary, or restructuring would make the burden more humane.

Prayer: Angel of Empathy in Capricorn, teach me to see the weight others carry. Let my understanding become responsible care. May Christ make every burden lighter through wisdom.
