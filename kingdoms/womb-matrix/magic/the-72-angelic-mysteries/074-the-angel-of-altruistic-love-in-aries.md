# The Angel of Altruistic Love in Aries



**The Chapel of Saint John**

The Angel of Altruistic Love in Aries teaches the love that offers itself bravely. Aries carries courage, action, initiative, and the first flame of will. In the Chapel of Saint John, this fire is drawn to the Heart of Christ. John remains near the Cross when love becomes costly. Through his chapel, Aries learns that true courage is not only the power to fight. It is the power to give oneself to love without being ruled by fear.

This angel appears when love requires initiative. Someone may need protection, advocacy, encouragement, or a first movement of repair. The reader may be waiting for certainty, recognition, or safety before acting. Aries altruistic love says: begin the good that is yours to begin.

When this card appears, ask where love is asking you to move first. Do not confuse self-offering with aggression. The action should serve life, not ego.

Practice: Choose one brave act of love that does not center your reward. Defend someone, begin the repair, offer help, or make the first generous movement.

Prayer: Angel of Altruistic Love in Aries, teach me brave self-offering. Let my fire serve Christ and not my pride. May my courage become love in action.
