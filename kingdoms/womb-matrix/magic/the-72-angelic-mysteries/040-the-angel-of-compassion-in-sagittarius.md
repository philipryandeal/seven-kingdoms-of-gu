# The Angel of Compassion in Sagittarius



**The Chapel of Saint James the Greater**

The Angel of Compassion in Sagittarius teaches the compassion of the wounded pilgrim. Sagittarius carries pilgrimage, teaching, distance, meaning, blessing, and the long road of the soul. In the Chapel of Saint James the Greater, this movement becomes sacred journey. James walks with those who travel, seek, leave, return, and learn through the road itself.

This angel appears when suffering is part of transition. Someone may be leaving an old life, crossing a threshold, losing a map, seeking truth, or trying to find God after disappointment. Sagittarius compassion does not offer cheap certainty. It blesses the search and walks beside the traveler long enough for wisdom to emerge.

When this card appears, ask who needs companionship on the road. The person may not need a lecture. They may need courage, perspective, humor, prayer, or permission to keep walking. Compassion becomes a lantern for the path.

Practice: Pray for someone in transition. Imagine them walking a long road under the protection of Christ and Saint James. Then ask where you also need courage for your own pilgrimage.

Prayer: Angel of Compassion in Sagittarius, teach me to bless the wounded traveler. Let my wisdom encourage without controlling. May Christ guide every searching soul toward holy ground.
