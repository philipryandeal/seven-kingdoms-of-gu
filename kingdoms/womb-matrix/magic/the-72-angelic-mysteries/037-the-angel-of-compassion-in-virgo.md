# The Angel of Compassion in Virgo



**The Chapel of Saint Philip**

The Angel of Compassion in Virgo teaches the compassion of healing service. Virgo carries discernment, repair, devotion, medicine, order, and the sacred detail. In the Chapel of Saint Philip, this service becomes invitation. Philip says, “Come and see,” guiding another toward direct encounter with Christ. Through his chapel, compassion becomes careful help that does not overwhelm.

This angel appears when suffering needs skillful attention. The reader may need to organize care, research resources, prepare medicine, edit confusion, clean a space, make instructions clear, or help someone take the next manageable step. Virgo compassion does not despise small tasks. It knows that healing often depends on details.

When this card appears, ask what would reduce suffering in a practical way. Do not make the moment about your feelings. Make it about the care required. Compassion may need a checklist, a clean towel, a ride to the doctor, a corrected document, or a calm plan.

Practice: Choose one small act of repair. Fix, clean, organize, clarify, or prepare something that will make life easier for a suffering person or for your own overwhelmed self.

Prayer: Angel of Compassion in Virgo, teach me to serve the wound carefully. Let my attention become medicine. May Christ work through every humble act of repair.
