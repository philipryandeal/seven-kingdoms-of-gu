# The Angel of Compassion in Cancer



**The Chapel of Saint J****ames**** the Less**

The Angel of Compassion in Cancer teaches the compassion of emotional shelter. Cancer carries home, memory, ancestry, tenderness, and the vulnerable interior of the soul. In the Chapel of Saint James the Less, this shelter becomes humble, faithful presence. The lesser-known saint teaches a compassion that does not need attention. It simply remains near.

This angel appears when grief, fear, or old memory rises to the surface. The reader may be called to hold space for another person, an ancestor, a child, a family wound, or a wounded part of the self. Cancer compassion does not demand that pain become articulate before it is welcomed. It allows tears to have a room.

When this card appears, ask what needs to feel safe enough to be felt. Do not rush the process. Do not interrogate the wound. Make a place where sorrow can breathe without being judged.

Practice: Light a candle in the home and place a bowl of water nearby. Pray for those in your family line, living and dead, who carried sorrow without comfort. Ask Christ to make your heart a safe room.

Prayer: Angel of Compassion in Cancer, teach me to shelter sorrow. Let my presence become gentle enough for tears. May Christ heal the memories that still tremble in the house of the heart.
