# The Angel of Altruistic Love in Gemini



**The Chapel of Saint Thomas**

The Angel of Altruistic Love in Gemini teaches love through free communication, truthful teaching, and shared understanding. Gemini carries language, thought, curiosity, exchange, and the movement of meaning. In the Chapel of Saint Thomas, this becomes honest inquiry before the wounds of Christ. Through his chapel, altruistic love speaks in order to liberate, not to impress.

This angel appears when knowledge, words, or explanation must be given generously. The reader may have insight that could help another, but must offer it without superiority. Gemini altruistic love does not hoard understanding to maintain power. It shares wisdom in a form the other can receive.

When this card appears, ask whether your words are serving the other’s freedom. Are you teaching, or displaying? Are you clarifying, or controlling? Are you listening as well as speaking?

Practice: Share one helpful truth, instruction, or encouragement without demanding recognition. Make something easier for another person to understand.

Prayer: Angel of Altruistic Love in Gemini, teach me to speak for the good of others. Let my knowledge become service. May Christ make my words clear, humble, and freeing.
