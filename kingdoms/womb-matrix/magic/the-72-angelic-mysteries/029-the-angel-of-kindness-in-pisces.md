# The Angel of Kindness in Pisces



**The Chapel of Saint Matthias**

The Angel of Kindness in Pisces teaches the kindness of mystical gentleness. Pisces carries mercy, dream, sorrow, compassion, imagination, and the dissolving of hard separations. In the Chapel of Saint Matthias, this tenderness is joined to restoration. Matthias is chosen to fill the empty place among the apostles. Through his chapel, kindness becomes the grace that returns the lost part to the circle.

This angel appears when the soul is tired, porous, grieving, or spiritually overwhelmed. Pisces kindness does not demand immediate strength. It allows tears, dreams, silence, music, prayer, and holy rest. It knows that some wounds heal through being held in the ocean of God rather than being analyzed too quickly.

When this card appears, ask what lost or exiled part of the self is ready to be welcomed home. Kindness may need to be offered inwardly. The reader may have been gentle with everyone except themselves. The Angel of Kindness in Pisces says: you too are a creature of God.

Practice: Sit quietly with a bowl of water, a candle, or an image of Christ. Whisper the names of those who feel lost, including yourself. Ask that all who are scattered be gathered into divine tenderness.

Prayer: Angel of Kindness in Pisces, teach me the gentleness of Christ. Gather what has been lost. Let my heart become an ocean where no sorrow is abandoned.
