# The Angel of Forgiveness in Sagittarius



**The Chapel of Saint James the Greater**

The Angel of Forgiveness in Sagittarius teaches forgiveness that opens the road beyond the wound. Sagittarius carries pilgrimage, meaning, teaching, distance, hope, and the horizon of the soul. In the Chapel of Saint James the Greater, forgiveness becomes a blessing for the traveler who must continue. James guards the road after loss, error, and exile.

This angel appears when the past has become a place where the soul keeps camping. The reader may understand the wound, speak of the wound, and even honor the wound, but still refuse the road beyond it. Sagittarius forgiveness does not deny the injury. It asks whether the journey may continue.

When this card appears, ask what wisdom can be carried forward and what burden must be left behind. Forgiveness may be the grace that lets the soul walk again.

Practice: Take a short walk as prayer. With each step say: “I continue with Christ.” At the end, bless the road ahead and release one piece of the past into God’s care.

Prayer: Angel of Forgiveness in Sagittarius, teach me to walk beyond the wound. Let pain become wisdom, not prison. May Christ bless the road that opens before me.
