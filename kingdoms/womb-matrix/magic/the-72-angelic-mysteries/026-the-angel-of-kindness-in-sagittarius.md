# The Angel of Kindness in Sagittarius



**The Chapel of Saint James the Greater**

The Angel of Kindness in Sagittarius teaches the kindness of blessing the journey. Sagittarius carries pilgrimage, vision, teaching, distance, story, and the search for meaning. In the Chapel of Saint James the Greater, this movement becomes holy pilgrimage. James is the apostle of the road, the saint of the path walked with faith. Through his chapel, kindness becomes encouragement for the traveler.

This angel appears when the reader or another person is in motion. A new path may be opening, or an old map may no longer serve. The temptation may be to preach, correct, or impose certainty too quickly. But Sagittarius kindness blesses the search without abandoning truth. It gives courage to continue walking.

When this card appears, ask who needs encouragement on the road. Perhaps someone is learning, leaving, studying, recovering, migrating, or seeking God through unfamiliar terrain. Kindness may take the form of a blessing rather than an answer.

Practice: Offer a blessing for someone in transition. Speak or write: “May Christ guide your road. May wisdom meet you. May your steps be protected.” Then bless your own path as well.

Prayer: Angel of Kindness in Sagittarius, teach me to bless the pilgrim. Let my wisdom encourage rather than control. May every road lead deeper into Christ.
