# The Angel of Compassion in Aries



**The Chapel of Saint John**

The Angel of Compassion in Aries teaches the compassion that moves quickly toward danger. Aries carries the fire of action, courage, and holy immediacy. In the Chapel of Saint John, this fire is joined to the Heart of Christ. John rests close to divine love, but he also stands at the Cross. Through his chapel, compassion learns not to flee when love requires presence at the place of suffering.

This angel appears when the reader must respond rather than merely feel. Someone may be in distress. A situation may require protection, advocacy, or immediate care. The temptation may be to wait until the perfect words arrive or until someone else takes responsibility. Aries compassion says: move toward the wound with courage, but do not strike blindly.

When this card appears, ask where suffering requires your holy initiative. Compassion may need to become a phone call, a defense, a meal, a boundary, an interruption, or a simple act of showing up first. Do not confuse hesitation with discernment.

Practice: Place one hand over the heart and one hand forward, palm open. Say: “Christ, make my courage compassionate.” Then take one concrete step toward someone or something that needs brave care.

Prayer: Angel of Compassion in Aries, teach me to move toward suffering without violence. Let my courage serve the wounded. May the Heart of Christ guide my first response.
