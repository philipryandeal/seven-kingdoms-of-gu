# The Angel of Mercy in Virgo



**The Chapel of Saint Philip**

The Angel of Mercy in Virgo teaches mercy as careful repair. Virgo carries discernment, healing, detail, service, repentance, and the sacred work of making things clean and useful again. In the Chapel of Saint Philip, this repair becomes invitation. Philip says, “Come and see,” guiding others toward encounter rather than forcing transformation. Through his chapel, mercy becomes humble correction joined to practical help.

This angel appears when something can be repaired, but only through attention. The reader may need to make amends, correct an error, clean up a mess, revise careless words, seek treatment, organize help, or return to a neglected discipline. Virgo mercy does not shame the broken thing. It studies what is needed for healing.

When this card appears, ask what small repair mercy requires. Do not drown in regret. Regret that does not become repair turns stagnant. Begin with one detail. One apology. One corrected habit. One honest inventory. One act of service.

Practice: Choose one mistake that can be repaired in part. Take one precise step. Send the correction, clean the space, make the appointment, return the object, or clarify the misunderstanding.

Prayer: Angel of Mercy in Virgo, teach me the grace of repair. Let my repentance become useful. May Christ guide my hands in the small work of restoration.
