# The Angel of Forgiveness in Virgo



**The Chapel of Saint Philip**

The Angel of Forgiveness in Virgo teaches forgiveness through careful repair and amends. Virgo carries discernment, service, healing, repentance, order, and the sacred detail. In the Chapel of Saint Philip, this repair becomes invitation. Philip says, “Come and see,” leading another toward encounter. Through his chapel, forgiveness becomes practical, humble, and precise.

This angel appears when forgiveness must be joined to action. A vague apology may not be enough. A pattern may need correction. A mess may need cleaning. A debt may need repayment. A habit may need changing. Virgo forgiveness understands that repair is often made one detail at a time.

When this card appears, ask what can actually be repaired. If you were harmed, what repair would be truthful? If you harmed another, what amends are possible? Do not drown in shame. Begin with the next faithful detail.

Practice: Write one sentence of apology or repair. Make it specific. Avoid excuses. Then take one practical step that proves the apology has entered the body.

Prayer: Angel of Forgiveness in Virgo, teach me the humility of repair. Let repentance become useful. May Christ guide my hands in every act of amends.
