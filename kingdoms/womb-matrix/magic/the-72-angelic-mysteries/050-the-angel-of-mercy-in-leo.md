# The Angel of Mercy in Leo



**The Chapel of Saint Peter**

The Angel of Mercy in Leo teaches the mercy that restores dignity after failure. Leo carries the heart, honor, visibility, courage, and the longing to shine. In the Chapel of Saint Peter, this mercy is unmistakable. Peter denies Christ, weeps in shame, and is later restored with the command to feed the sheep. Through his chapel, mercy becomes the grace that raises the fallen without erasing the lesson of the fall.

This angel appears when shame has attacked the dignity of the soul. The reader may have failed publicly, disappointed someone, lost confidence, or become trapped in humiliation. Leo mercy does not flatter the ego. It restores the heart to its vocation. It says: you are more than your collapse. Rise, and feed the sheep.

When this card appears, ask where Christ is restoring dignity without removing responsibility. Mercy may be calling you back into leadership, creativity, service, or visible love after a season of failure.

Practice: Name one failure that still carries shame. Then say aloud: “This is not the end of my vocation.” Ask Christ for one restored action that serves others.

Prayer: Angel of Mercy in Leo, teach me to rise after failure. Let shame become humility, not exile. May Christ restore my heart so I may feed His sheep.
