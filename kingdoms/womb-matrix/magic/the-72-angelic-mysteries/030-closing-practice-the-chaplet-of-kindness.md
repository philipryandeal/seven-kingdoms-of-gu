# Closing Practice: The Chaplet of Kindness



To close this chapter, the reader may practice the Chaplet of Kindness. This is a simple contemplative devotion for softening the heart and consecrating ordinary action.

Sit before a candle, cross, icon, saint card, or angel card. Place both feet on the ground. Let the breath become slow. Say:

“Lord Jesus Christ, teach me the kindness of Your Heart.”

Then name the place where kindness is most needed. It may be a relationship, a household, a workplace, a memory, a conflict, a body, a habit, or a hidden grief. Do not rush to fix it. Hold it before Christ.

Pray slowly through the twelve chapels:

May kindness begin with courage.
May kindness become steady care.
May kindness bless my speech.
May kindness shelter the tender.
May kindness radiate with dignity.
May kindness serve in small things.
May kindness restore relationship.
May kindness enter the hidden wound.
May kindness bless the road.
May kindness become responsibility.
May kindness widen the circle.
May kindness gather the lost.

After each line, breathe once and imagine the corresponding angel placing a small light in the heart.

When the prayer is complete, ask for one act of kindness to perform within the next day. Let it be specific. Let it be humble. Let it be possible.

The Angels of Kindness do not ask for grand performance. They ask for conversion in the next available moment. A word can become a door. A gesture can become a blessing. A pause can become an altar. A cup of water can become a sacrament of welcome.

Where kindness enters, the world becomes less afraid. Where the world becomes less afraid, the soul becomes more able to hear Christ. And where the soul hears Christ, the chapel opens.
