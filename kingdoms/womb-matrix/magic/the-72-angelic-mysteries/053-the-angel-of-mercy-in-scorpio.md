# The Angel of Mercy in Scorpio



**The Chapel of Saint Jude Thaddaeus**

The Angel of Mercy in Scorpio teaches mercy in the chamber of sin, shame, secrecy, and despair. Scorpio carries depth, death, taboo, hidden grief, survival, and transformation. In the Chapel of Saint Jude Thaddaeus, this descent is held by desperate hope. Jude is called upon when the soul believes the situation may be beyond saving. Through his chapel, mercy enters the place others have condemned as lost.

This angel appears when shame has become a tomb. The reader may be hiding a wound, addiction, resentment, betrayal, obsession, or memory that feels too dark for prayer. Scorpio mercy does not excuse what destroys life. It brings Christ into the depths so that truth can become liberation rather than annihilation.

When this card appears, ask what you have believed was beyond mercy. Do not expose it carelessly. Do not dramatize it. Bring it before Christ, Saint Jude, and the angel of this chapel. Desperate places are not empty of God.

Practice: In private, write the sentence: “I ask mercy for this hidden place.” Name the hidden place honestly. Place the paper beneath a candle, cross, or image of Saint Jude. Let it rest there overnight.

Prayer: Angel of Mercy in Scorpio, descend with me into the hidden chamber. Let Christ meet shame with truth and hope. May nothing in me be sealed away from divine mercy.
