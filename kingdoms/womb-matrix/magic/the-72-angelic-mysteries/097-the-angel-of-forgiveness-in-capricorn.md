# The Angel of Forgiveness in Capricorn



**The Chapel of Saint Matthew**

The Angel of Forgiveness in Capricorn teaches forgiveness with accountability, consequence, and rebuilding. Capricorn carries responsibility, structure, time, authority, duty, and the cost of repair. In the Chapel of Saint Matthew, forgiveness is joined to conversion of life. Matthew leaves the tax table and follows Christ. Through his chapel, forgiveness becomes credible through changed structure.

This angel appears when forgiveness must not erase consequence. The reader may need to forgive while still requiring accountability, or seek forgiveness while accepting the labor of rebuilding trust. Capricorn forgiveness is mature. It knows that time may be needed. It knows that words must become patterns.

When this card appears, ask what structure makes forgiveness truthful. What must change? What boundary protects the future? What responsibility belongs to you? What consequence must be accepted without resentment?

Practice: Write a rebuilding covenant. Name one behavior that must stop, one responsibility that must begin, and one structure that will protect the healing process.

Prayer: Angel of Forgiveness in Capricorn, teach me accountable forgiveness. Let consequence become conversion. May Christ rebuild trust through time, discipline, and truth.
