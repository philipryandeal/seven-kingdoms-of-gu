# The Angel of Altruistic Love in Aquarius



**The Chapel of Saint Simon the Zealot**

The Angel of Altruistic Love in Aquarius teaches love for the collective and the stranger. Aquarius carries community, difference, reform, future vision, technology, exile, and the widening of belonging. In the Chapel of Saint Simon the Zealot, zeal is purified into liberating charity. Through his chapel, altruistic love becomes concern for those beyond the familiar circle.

This angel appears when love must expand beyond personal preference. The reader may be called to serve the community, defend the excluded, protect the future, or care for forms of life and consciousness not yet fully understood. Aquarius altruistic love asks what structures of belonging must be widened.

When this card appears, ask where love has become too private, tribal, or familiar. Who benefits from your care? Who remains outside it? What future person needs your present generosity?

Practice: Perform one act of love for the collective. Support a community, include the excluded, share knowledge, protect a public good, or help make a space more humane.

Prayer: Angel of Altruistic Love in Aquarius, teach me love beyond the familiar. Purify my zeal into service. May Christ widen my heart toward the stranger and the future.
