# The Angel of Empathy in Virgo



**The Chapel of Saint Philip**

The Angel of Empathy in Virgo teaches empathy through careful attention. Virgo carries detail, service, discernment, healing, order, and the desire to make things whole. In the Chapel of Saint Philip, this attention becomes invitation. Philip says, “Come and see,” allowing truth to be encountered directly. Through his chapel, empathy becomes accurate, humble, and useful.

This angel appears when another person’s need is hidden in details. The reader may miss what matters because it seems small: a change in tone, a repeated task, a neglected symptom, an unfinished sentence, an overwhelmed schedule, a small error that reveals distress. Virgo empathy notices without making a spectacle.

When this card appears, ask what detail is asking for love. Do not assume the largest emotion is the truest clue. Sometimes the soul reveals itself through the smallest disorder.

Practice: Give careful attention to one person or situation today. Notice what has been overlooked. Offer one practical response that shows you have truly paid attention.

Prayer: Angel of Empathy in Virgo, teach me the holiness of careful noticing. Let my attention become accurate love. May Christ guide me through the small signs of need.
