# The Angel of Compassion in Pisces



**The Chapel of Saint Matthias**

The Angel of Compassion in Pisces teaches the compassion of mystical communion. Pisces carries sorrow, mercy, dream, imagination, spiritual sensitivity, and the oceanic heart. In the Chapel of Saint Matthias, this compassion becomes restoration to the circle. Matthias is chosen to fill the empty place. Through his chapel, compassion gathers what has been lost, scattered, or forgotten.

This angel appears when suffering cannot be solved quickly and must be held in prayer. The reader may feel the sorrow of the world, the grief of the dead, the exhaustion of the vulnerable, or the ache of one’s own soul. Pisces compassion does not demand immediate explanation. It allows tears to become prayer and prayer to become gentle union with Christ.

When this card appears, ask what sorrow needs to be held rather than fixed. You may need holy rest. You may need music, water, silence, sleep, confession, or a candle in the dark. Compassion must also be given inwardly.

Practice: Sit with a bowl of water. Whisper the names of those who suffer, including yourself. After each name, say: “Christ, hold them in Your mercy.” Let the water receive what the heart cannot carry alone.

Prayer: Angel of Compassion in Pisces, teach me to suffer with without being consumed. Gather the lost into divine tenderness. May Christ make my heart an ocean of merciful presence.
