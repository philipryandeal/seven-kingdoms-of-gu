# The Angel of Forgiveness in Aquarius



**The Chapel of Saint Simon the Zealot**

The Angel of Forgiveness in Aquarius teaches forgiveness that releases exile and widens belonging. Aquarius carries community, difference, reform, alienation, future vision, and the strange grace of the outsider. In the Chapel of Saint Simon the Zealot, zeal is purified into liberating love. Through his chapel, forgiveness challenges communities that exile too easily and souls that remain frozen in ideological contempt.

This angel appears when the wound is collective, communal, or social. A group may have rejected someone. A community may be divided by purity, shame, fear, or righteous anger. Aquarius forgiveness does not erase accountability, but it asks whether permanent exile has become a substitute for healing.

When this card appears, ask who has been cast out and whether Christ is asking for a wider future. Forgiveness may mean making room for repentance, complexity, changed understanding, or new forms of community.

Practice: Pray for a community wound. Ask Christ to reveal where truth, accountability, and restored belonging may become possible. Perform one act that reduces exile without denying harm.

Prayer: Angel of Forgiveness in Aquarius, teach me to release contempt from the collective heart. Purify zeal into mercy. May Christ widen belonging without abandoning truth.
