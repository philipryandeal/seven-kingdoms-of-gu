# The 72 Angelic Mysteries

**Oracle Cards for Contemplative Prayer and Christian Hermeticism**

*By Philip Ryan Deal (Brujo Ryan), Temple of Gu*

The complete system of magick of the Womb Matrix Kingdom, built on the doctrine of [The Heart of Christ](../the-heart-of-christ/README.md). Twelve zodiacal chapels, each with the face of an apostle-saint syncretized with its sign; inside each chapel, six angels — emissaries of that saint — one for each of the six liberating virtues. Twelve chapels × six virtues = seventy-two angelic mysteries. The wheel is rooted in the Dhruva Galactic Center / Middle of Mūla ayanāṁsa, with the Sacred Heart and the Rose Cross at its center.

The angels are not summoned or commanded. They are approached as ministers of grace and companions in prayer: *do not ask the card what will happen; ask the angel what virtue must become embodied through you.* The crown practice is the Rite of the Sixfold Heart.

*A readable archive in the Seven Kingdoms of Gu public library. This repository copy is the complete text, offered freely for reading. The book is also available for purchase for readers who wish to own a copy.*

Archived here November 14, 2026, at the founder's direction. The author's text is unchanged; Part Two has been divided so that each angel's mystery stands on its own page, as each card stands on its own in the deck.
