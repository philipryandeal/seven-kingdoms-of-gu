# The Angel of Altruistic Love in Scorpio



**The Chapel of Saint Jude Thaddaeus**

The Angel of Altruistic Love in Scorpio teaches sacrificial love in the depths without control. Scorpio carries secrecy, grief, desire, death, shame, loyalty, and transformation. In the Chapel of Saint Jude Thaddaeus, this depth is joined to difficult hope. Jude stands near the place that seems almost beyond repair. Through his chapel, altruistic love learns to remain faithful in darkness without possessing the outcome.

This angel appears when love is costly, intense, or hidden. Someone may be suffering in a place that cannot be quickly healed. The reader may be tempted to rescue, control, expose, or merge. Scorpio altruistic love stays reverent. It can keep vigil, pray, protect confidentiality, and love without devouring.

When this card appears, ask whether your love is freeing or binding. Are you serving transformation, or trying to own the mystery?

Practice: Hold a hidden suffering before Christ without trying to control it. Light a candle for difficult hope and say: “I offer love without possession.”

Prayer: Angel of Altruistic Love in Scorpio, teach me faithful love in the depths. Let sacrifice be purified of control. May Christ bring hope where I cannot force healing.
