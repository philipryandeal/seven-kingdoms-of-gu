# The Angel of Altruistic Love in Cancer



**The Chapel of Saint J****ames**** the Less**

The Angel of Altruistic Love in Cancer teaches nurturing without possession. Cancer carries home, family, memory, ancestry, emotional safety, and the instinct to protect what is tender. In the Chapel of Saint James the Less, this love becomes quiet, humble care. It does not need attention for its devotion. It shelters because shelter is needed.

This angel appears when the reader is called to nurture a person, household, child, ancestor, vulnerable part of the self, or fragile beginning. Cancer altruistic love feeds and protects, but it must not clutch. The one being nurtured must be allowed to grow, separate, and belong to God.

When this card appears, ask whether your care gives life or creates dependency. Are you sheltering, or enclosing? Are you loving, or needing to be needed?

Practice: Offer one nurturing act while inwardly releasing ownership. Cook, comfort, bless the home, pray for family, or tend a vulnerable place, then say: “You belong to God.”

Prayer: Angel of Altruistic Love in Cancer, teach me to nurture without possession. Let my care shelter life and release it into God’s hands. May Christ dwell in this house of love.
