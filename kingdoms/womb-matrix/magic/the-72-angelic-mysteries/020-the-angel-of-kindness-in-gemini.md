# The Angel of Kindness in Gemini



**The Chapel of Saint Thomas**

The Angel of Kindness in Gemini teaches the kindness of speech. Words can open wounds or close them. They can confuse, mock, bless, clarify, invite, or condemn. Gemini carries the power of communication, and in the Chapel of Saint Thomas this power is joined to honest inquiry. Thomas is not the enemy of faith; he is the apostle who brings doubt into contact with the wounds of the risen Christ. Through his chapel, kindness learns to speak truth without shaming uncertainty.

This angel appears when the reader must become more conscious of language. Perhaps there has been sarcasm where gentleness was needed, explanation where listening was needed, or silence where a good word should have been spoken. The Angel of Kindness in Gemini asks the soul to consecrate the tongue.

When this card appears, pay attention to the next conversation. Do not rush to win. Do not use intelligence as a weapon. Ask better questions. Let another person clarify their meaning. Speak in a way that makes truth easier to receive.

Practice: Before an important conversation, pray over your mouth with your thumb. Say: “May my words serve healing.” Then speak one sentence today that gives courage, clarity, or relief.

Prayer: Angel of Kindness in Gemini, teach my tongue to bless. Let my questions be honest and my answers gentle. May my speech become a bridge to Christ.
