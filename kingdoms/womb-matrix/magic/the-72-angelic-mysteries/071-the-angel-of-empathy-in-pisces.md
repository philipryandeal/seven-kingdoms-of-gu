# The Angel of Empathy in Pisces



**The Chapel of Saint Matthias**

The Angel of Empathy in Pisces teaches empathy as mystical participation without self-loss. Pisces carries dream, sorrow, imagination, prayer, sensitivity, and the oceanic unity of creation. In the Chapel of Saint Matthias, this sensitivity becomes restoration to the circle. Matthias fills the empty place. Through his chapel, empathy gathers what has been lost while remaining anchored in Christ.

This angel appears when the reader feels the emotions of others, the atmosphere of a room, the grief of the dead, the ache of the world, or the sorrow of the soul too strongly. Pisces empathy is beautiful, but it must be grounded. To feel everything without discernment is not holiness. It is drowning.

When this card appears, ask where empathy needs prayerful boundaries. You may be called to feel with another, but not to disappear into them. You may be called to weep, but not to abandon your center.

Practice: Sit with a bowl of water. Name what you are feeling. Then ask: “What is mine, what is another’s, and what belongs to God?” Touch the water and release what is not yours to carry.

Prayer: Angel of Empathy in Pisces, teach me to feel with creation without drowning. Gather the lost into tenderness. May Christ anchor my heart in divine love.
