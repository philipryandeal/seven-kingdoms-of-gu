# The Angel of Forgiveness in Aries



**The Chapel of Saint John**

The Angel of Forgiveness in Aries teaches the forgiveness that interrupts vengeance. Aries carries fire, anger, defense, courage, and the immediate desire to act. In the Chapel of Saint John, this fire is brought close to the Heart of Christ. John stands at the Cross, where violence is answered by a love stronger than retaliation. Through his chapel, Aries learns that not every wound must become a weapon.

This angel appears when the first impulse is to strike back. The reader may feel insulted, betrayed, challenged, or attacked. The desire for revenge may appear righteous because the injury is real. The Angel of Forgiveness in Aries does not deny the fire. It asks Christ to govern it before it becomes another harm.

When this card appears, ask where forgiveness must begin as restraint. You may still need to speak, leave, protect, or confront. But do not let vengeance choose the form.

Practice: Before acting from anger, place both hands over the heart and say: “Christ, stand between my wound and my will.” Wait until the body softens enough for wisdom to enter.

Prayer: Angel of Forgiveness in Aries, teach me to stop vengeance at the gate. Let my courage protect truth without becoming cruelty. May Christ rule my fire.
