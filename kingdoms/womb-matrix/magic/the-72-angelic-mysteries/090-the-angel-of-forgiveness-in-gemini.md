# The Angel of Forgiveness in Gemini



**The Chapel of Saint Thomas**

The Angel of Forgiveness in Gemini teaches the forgiveness that purifies the story. Gemini carries speech, thought, memory, interpretation, and the power of words. In the Chapel of Saint Thomas, this becomes honest encounter with the wounds of Christ. Thomas is invited to touch what is real. Through his chapel, forgiveness does not lie about the wound, but it stops making the wound the only story.

This angel appears when the injury is being repeated through speech or thought. The reader may retell the event inwardly until resentment becomes a liturgy. Gemini forgiveness asks whether the story is serving truth or feeding bondage. Some stories must be spoken. But every telling should move the soul closer to freedom, not deeper into captivity.

When this card appears, ask how the story can be told truthfully without becoming a prison. What details are needed? What embellishments keep anger alive? What new sentence might open the door?

Practice: Write the story of the wound in simple factual language. Then write: “This happened, and Christ is still present.” Read both sentences aloud.

Prayer: Angel of Forgiveness in Gemini, teach my words to serve release. Let my memory become truthful without becoming captive. May Christ speak freedom into my story.
