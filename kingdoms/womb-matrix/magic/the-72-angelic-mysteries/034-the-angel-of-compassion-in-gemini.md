# The Angel of Compassion in Gemini



**The Chapel of Saint Thomas**

The Angel of Compassion in Gemini teaches the compassion of listening. Gemini carries language, thought, exchange, curiosity, and the movement of meaning. In the Chapel of Saint Thomas, this becomes honest encounter with wounded truth. Thomas brings his doubt to the risen Christ and is invited to touch the wounds. Through his chapel, compassion learns to ask without shaming and to speak without wounding.

This angel appears when someone’s suffering needs to be heard clearly. The reader may be tempted to interrupt, compare, analyze, or correct. Gemini compassion asks for a consecrated tongue and an attentive mind. Listen until the person becomes more real to you than your response.

When this card appears, ask whether your words are serving the wound or defending your comfort. Sometimes the most compassionate sentence is, “Tell me more.” Sometimes it is, “I believe you.” Sometimes it is, “I do not fully understand, but I am here.”

Practice: In one conversation today, listen without turning the focus back to yourself. Afterward, pray for the person by name and ask Christ to bless what you did not understand.

Prayer: Angel of Compassion in Gemini, teach me to listen at the wound. Let my questions be gentle and my words be true. May my speech become a place where Christ can enter.
