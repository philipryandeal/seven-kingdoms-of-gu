# The Angel of Compassion in Taurus



**The Chapel of Saint Andrew**

The Angel of Compassion in Taurus teaches the compassion of embodied care. Taurus carries steadiness, food, touch, shelter, patience, and the wisdom of the material world. In the Chapel of Saint Andrew, this care becomes simple discipleship. Andrew brings others to Christ not through spectacle, but through faithful nearness. Through his chapel, compassion becomes practical and dependable.

This angel appears when suffering needs more than words. Someone may need rest, food, money, a clean room, medicine, presence, or a patient body beside them. Taurus compassion does not rush to spiritualize pain. It asks whether the person has eaten, slept, been held, been warmed, or been allowed to stop performing strength.

When this card appears, ask how compassion can become tangible. The answer may be humble. Make soup. Pay attention to the animal. Wash the cup. Sit on the couch. Bring a blanket. Let love enter the senses.

Practice: Offer one act of material comfort to yourself or another creature. Prepare food, tend a body, make a space restful, or repair something that has become burdensome.

Prayer: Angel of Compassion in Taurus, teach me the holiness of embodied care. Let my love become bread, shelter, patience, and peace. May Christ comfort through my hands.
