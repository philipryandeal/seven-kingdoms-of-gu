# The Angel of Compassion in Leo



**The Chapel of Saint Peter**

The Angel of Compassion in Leo teaches the compassion that restores dignity. Leo carries the heart, radiance, courage, creativity, and personal honor. In the Chapel of Saint Peter, this radiance is transformed by failure and restoration. Peter denies Christ, weeps bitterly, and is later commanded to feed the sheep. Through his chapel, compassion becomes the power to lift the fallen without humiliating them.

This angel appears when shame has dimmed someone’s light. The reader may encounter a person who feels disgraced, embarrassed, unseen, or unworthy of love. Leo compassion does not flatter them falsely. It calls forth the dignity that suffering has obscured. It says: your wound is real, but it is not the whole of you.

When this card appears, ask where compassion must become encouragement. Who needs to be reminded of their worth? Who needs warmth after failure? Who needs to be restored to the circle without being reduced to their worst moment?

Practice: Offer one sincere word of dignity to someone, including yourself. Name a strength, beauty, gift, or goodness that suffering has not destroyed.

Prayer: Angel of Compassion in Leo, teach me to restore dignity. Let my heart warm the ashamed and weary. May Christ feed His sheep through my courage to bless.
