# The Angel of Compassion in Capricorn



**The Chapel of Saint Matthew**

The Angel of Compassion in Capricorn teaches the compassion of responsible support. Capricorn carries structure, duty, labor, time, authority, and endurance. In the Chapel of Saint Matthew, this responsibility is transformed by vocation. Matthew leaves the tax table and becomes an apostle. Through his chapel, compassion becomes the willingness to build conditions where healing can last.

This angel appears when suffering requires structure. Feeling sorry is not enough. The problem may need planning, money, accountability, discipline, leadership, documentation, policy, boundaries, or long-term care. Capricorn compassion asks what must be built, repaired, or sustained so the vulnerable are not abandoned again.

When this card appears, ask where compassion must become responsibility. Have you made promises without systems? Have you offered care without endurance? Have you avoided authority when protection was needed? This angel teaches that mature compassion may look like work.

Practice: Take one responsible step that supports healing over time. Make the appointment, set the boundary, create the plan, pay the bill, organize the help, or accept accountability.

Prayer: Angel of Compassion in Capricorn, teach me to support suffering with maturity. Let my discipline become protection. May Christ make my labor a lasting shelter.
