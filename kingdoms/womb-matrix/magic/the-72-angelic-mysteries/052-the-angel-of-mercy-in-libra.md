# The Angel of Mercy in Libra



**The Chapel of Saint Bartholomew / Nathanael**

The Angel of Mercy in Libra teaches mercy held in balance with justice. Libra carries relationship, beauty, fairness, harmony, and mutual recognition. In the Chapel of Saint Bartholomew, also called Nathanael, mercy becomes sincere and without guile. Christ names Nathanael as one in whom there is no deceit. Through his chapel, mercy is purified of false peace.

This angel appears when reconciliation is desired, but truth must not be bypassed. The reader may be tempted to forgive too quickly in order to avoid discomfort, or to punish too long in order to avoid vulnerability. Libra mercy asks for balance. What is fair? What is beautiful? What is honest? What would allow dignity on all sides without sacrificing the wounded?

When this card appears, ask where mercy and justice must speak to each other. Mercy may require apology, restitution, mediation, distance, renewed agreement, or the graceful release of a relationship that cannot safely continue.

Practice: Write two columns: “Mercy” and “Justice.” Under each, name what the situation requires. Pray for Christ to reveal the action that honors both.

Prayer: Angel of Mercy in Libra, teach me truthful reconciliation. Let my desire for peace become honest and fair. May Christ balance mercy and justice in my relationships.
