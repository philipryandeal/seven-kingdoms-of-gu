# The Angel of Forgiveness in Cancer



**The Chapel of Saint J****ames**** the Less**

The Angel of Forgiveness in Cancer teaches forgiveness in memory, family, and the tender rooms of the heart. Cancer carries home, childhood, ancestry, protection, grief, and emotional inheritance. In the Chapel of Saint James the Less, forgiveness becomes humble and quiet. It does not need public display. It enters the house like a candle.

This angel appears when the wound belongs to family, history, or early life. The reader may carry pain from parents, children, siblings, ancestors, homes, churches, or communities that shaped the heart before the heart had defenses. Cancer forgiveness does not require pretending the past was safe. It lets Christ enter the memory as protector and healer.

When this card appears, ask which younger part of the self needs release from old pain. Forgiveness may be directed toward another, but it may also be directed toward the self who survived imperfectly.

Practice: Light a candle for the home of your heart. Say: “Christ, enter the rooms of memory.” Bless the younger self and pray for the family line without denying truth.

Prayer: Angel of Forgiveness in Cancer, teach me to forgive without betraying memory. Heal the house within me. Let Christ shelter the child, the ancestor, and the wounded heart.
