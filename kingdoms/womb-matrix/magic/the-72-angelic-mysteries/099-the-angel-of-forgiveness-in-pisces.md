# The Angel of Forgiveness in Pisces



**The Chapel of Saint Matthias**

The Angel of Forgiveness in Pisces teaches forgiveness as surrender into the mercy of God. Pisces carries sorrow, prayer, dream, tears, compassion, dissolution, and mystical union. In the Chapel of Saint Matthias, forgiveness becomes restoration to the circle after loss. Matthias fills the empty place. Through his chapel, forgiveness gathers what grief has scattered.

This angel appears when the wound cannot be solved by thought alone. The reader may be exhausted by memory, sorrow, regret, or longing. Pisces forgiveness does not demand instant clarity. It invites surrender. Some injuries must be cried into God. Some debts must be placed into the ocean of divine mercy because the human heart cannot untangle them alone.

When this card appears, ask what must be released into God’s depths. Forgiveness may come through tears, water, music, sleep, confession, prayer, or blessing the dead.

Practice: Sit with a bowl of water. Name the wound softly. Then say: “Christ, receive what I cannot carry.” Touch the water and imagine the sorrow dissolving into divine mercy.

Prayer: Angel of Forgiveness in Pisces, teach me surrender into God’s mercy. Gather what grief has scattered. May Christ forgive through me where I cannot forgive alone.
