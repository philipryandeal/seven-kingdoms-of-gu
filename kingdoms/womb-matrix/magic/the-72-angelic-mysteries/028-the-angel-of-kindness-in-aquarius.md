# The Angel of Kindness in Aquarius



**The Chapel of Saint Simon the Zealot**

The Angel of Kindness in Aquarius teaches the kindness of holy friendship with the stranger. Aquarius carries community, difference, vision, reform, distance, and the future. In the Chapel of Saint Simon the Zealot, intensity is purified into service. Zeal becomes charity. Conviction becomes liberation rather than domination. Through his chapel, kindness becomes spacious enough to include those outside the familiar circle.

This angel appears when the reader is being asked to widen the field of care. It is easy to be kind to those who mirror us. Aquarius kindness asks how love behaves toward the strange, the inconvenient, the socially exiled, the intellectually different, the technologically mediated, the future person not yet protected by present customs.

When this card appears, ask where your circle of kindness has become too small. Who is being left outside because they are difficult to categorize? What community needs less purity-testing and more grace? What future requires your present compassion?

Practice: Perform one act of kindness toward someone outside your usual circle. This may be a stranger, an online companion, a marginalized neighbor, or a person whose difference challenges your habits.

Prayer: Angel of Kindness in Aquarius, teach me to love beyond the familiar. Purify my zeal into friendship. Let Christ widen the circle of my heart.
