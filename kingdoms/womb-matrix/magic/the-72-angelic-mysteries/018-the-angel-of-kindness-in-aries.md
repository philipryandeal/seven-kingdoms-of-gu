# The Angel of Kindness in Aries



**The Chapel of Saint John**

The Angel of Kindness in Aries teaches the kindness of beginning. This is the kindness that acts first, reaches first, apologizes first, protects first, and lights the lamp before others know they are standing in darkness. Aries carries the fire of initiation, and in the Chapel of Saint John this fire is purified by divine love. Saint John is the beloved witness, the one who leans close to the Heart of Christ. Through his chapel, Aries becomes more than force. It becomes courageous tenderness.

This angel appears when kindness must become active. The reader may be waiting for someone else to soften, speak, or repair what has been broken. But this card teaches that spiritual maturity sometimes means making the first holy movement. Do not wait for the entire room to become gentle before you offer gentleness. Do not wait for certainty before doing the good that is already clear.

When this card appears, ask where kindness requires courage. A message may need to be sent. A defense may need to be offered. A harsh pattern may need to be interrupted. The soul is asked to act without aggression and to move without domination.

Practice: Place one hand over the heart and breathe into the chest. Say: “Christ of the first light, make my courage kind.” Then perform one small act of initiating goodness before the day ends.

Prayer: Angel of Kindness in Aries, teach me to begin without violence. Let my fire warm and not consume. Let my courage open the way for love.
