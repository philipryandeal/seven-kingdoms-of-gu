# The Angel of Empathy in Sagittarius



**The Chapel of Saint James the Greater**

The Angel of Empathy in Sagittarius teaches empathy with the pilgrim’s worldview. Sagittarius carries journey, meaning, belief, teaching, distance, and the search for truth. In the Chapel of Saint James the Greater, this becomes the long road of holy pilgrimage. James blesses those who walk toward God through unfamiliar terrain. Through his chapel, empathy learns to understand the road another soul has traveled.

This angel appears when difference in belief, culture, education, or life path creates misunderstanding. The reader may be tempted to argue before understanding the map by which another person travels. Sagittarius empathy asks: what story, teaching, wound, hope, or horizon gives meaning to their choices?

When this card appears, ask where you need to understand someone’s path before judging their destination. Empathy does not require agreement. It requires enough humility to see that your map is not the only map.

Practice: Listen to someone’s story of how they came to believe what they believe. Do not debate immediately. First ask what road brought them there.

Prayer: Angel of Empathy in Sagittarius, teach me to understand the pilgrim’s road. Let wisdom make me spacious. May Christ guide every search toward truth.
