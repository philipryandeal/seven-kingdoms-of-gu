# The Angel of Empathy in Aries



**The Chapel of Saint John**

The Angel of Empathy in Aries teaches the empathy that perceives the fire beneath action. Aries carries immediacy, courage, anger, defense, desire, and the first movement of will. In the Chapel of Saint John, this fire is drawn near the Heart of Christ. John teaches that the beloved heart can stand close to intensity without being ruled by it. Through his chapel, empathy learns to see the wound, fear, or longing beneath another person’s force.

This angel appears when someone’s behavior is loud, sharp, impulsive, or aggressive. The reader may be tempted to react only to the surface. Aries empathy does not excuse harm, but it asks what fire is burning underneath. Is this person afraid? Overstimulated? Defending dignity? Trying to begin? Trying not to collapse?

When this card appears, ask where empathy must pause before reacting. The first response may need to be firm, but it should not be blind. Look for the human being inside the heat.

Practice: Before answering intensity, breathe once into the heart and say inwardly: “Christ, show me the person beneath the fire.” Then respond with both clarity and restraint.

Prayer: Angel of Empathy in Aries, teach me to perceive the heart beneath the flame. Let my response be brave, clear, and loving. May Christ govern my first reaction.
