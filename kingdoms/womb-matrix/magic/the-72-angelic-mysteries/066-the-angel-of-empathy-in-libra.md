# The Angel of Empathy in Libra



**The Chapel of Saint Bartholomew / Nathanael**

The Angel of Empathy in Libra teaches empathy as truthful mirroring. Libra carries relationship, beauty, fairness, mutuality, and the sacred mirror of the other. In the Chapel of Saint Bartholomew, also called Nathanael, this mirror is purified of deceit. Christ names Nathanael as one in whom there is no guile. Through his chapel, empathy becomes honest reflection without manipulation.

This angel appears when relationship requires mutual understanding. The reader may need to see how their own behavior feels to another person. Libra empathy does not only ask, “What do I feel?” It asks, “What is it like to be in relationship with me?” This question can become a doorway to humility.

When this card appears, ask where empathy must restore balance. Have you expected others to understand you while refusing to understand them? Have you mirrored only what keeps peace, not what is true? Empathy here becomes relational fairness.

Practice: In one relationship, ask sincerely: “How has this felt for you?” Listen without defending. Reflect back what you heard before explaining yourself.

Prayer: Angel of Empathy in Libra, teach me to mirror without deceit. Let me understand and be understood in truth. May Christ make my relationships fair, graceful, and sincere.
