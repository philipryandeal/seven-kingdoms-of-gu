# The Angel of Mercy in Gemini



**The Chapel of Saint Thomas**

The Angel of Mercy in Gemini teaches the mercy of purified speech. Gemini carries language, thought, interpretation, conversation, and the power of naming. In the Chapel of Saint Thomas, this power is joined to honest encounter. Thomas brings doubt to the wounds of the risen Christ and is not rejected. Through his chapel, mercy learns to speak truth without cruelty and to question without contempt.

This angel appears when judgment has gathered in the mouth. The reader may be tempted to gossip, mock, exaggerate, condemn, or define another person by one failure. Gemini mercy asks the soul to slow down and let speech become responsible. Words can become prisons. They can also become doors.

When this card appears, ask whether your words leave room for repentance, complexity, and return. This does not mean lying about harm. It means refusing to turn speech into a theater of punishment. Mercy tells the truth in a way that still belongs to Christ.

Practice: Fast from gossip for one day. When tempted to repeat a condemning story, pray: “Lord, have mercy on them and on me.” If truth must be spoken, speak only what is necessary and healing.

Prayer: Angel of Mercy in Gemini, teach my tongue to serve restoration. Let my thoughts become clear without becoming cruel. May my words leave room for grace.
