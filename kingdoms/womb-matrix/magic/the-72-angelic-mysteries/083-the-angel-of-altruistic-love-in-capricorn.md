# The Angel of Altruistic Love in Capricorn



**The Chapel of Saint Matthew**

The Angel of Altruistic Love in Capricorn teaches love as committed responsibility. Capricorn carries duty, time, structure, endurance, authority, and the labor required to build what lasts. In the Chapel of Saint Matthew, this responsibility is transformed by vocation. Matthew leaves one form of accounting and enters the service of the Gospel. Through his chapel, altruistic love becomes mature commitment.

This angel appears when love requires discipline over time. A person, family, community, project, or vocation may need steady support rather than emotional intensity. Capricorn altruistic love keeps promises, builds structures, honors limits, and accepts the labor of care.

When this card appears, ask where love must become dependable. What commitment has been made? What structure protects the good? What responsibility is yours, and what responsibility is not?

Practice: Strengthen one commitment through practical action. Make the plan, keep the promise, pay the bill, schedule the care, finish the work, or create the boundary that makes love sustainable.

Prayer: Angel of Altruistic Love in Capricorn, teach me responsible love. Let my commitment become shelter. May Christ make my labor faithful, mature, and enduring.
