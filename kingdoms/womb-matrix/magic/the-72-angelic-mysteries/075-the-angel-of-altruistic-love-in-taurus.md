# The Angel of Altruistic Love in Taurus



**The Chapel of Saint Andrew**

The Angel of Altruistic Love in Taurus teaches love as generous provision. Taurus carries food, shelter, money, touch, land, patience, and the goodness of the material world. In the Chapel of Saint Andrew, this earthy care becomes discipleship. Andrew brings others to Christ through faithful nearness. Through his chapel, altruistic love becomes practical nourishment.

This angel appears when love must take material form. Good intentions may not be enough. The person in need may require food, rest, transportation, medicine, stability, or a peaceful room. Taurus altruistic love gives through the hands and through the resources of the earth.

When this card appears, ask what can be provided without creating dependency, debt, or control. Give cleanly. Do not make the receiver perform gratitude beyond what dignity allows.

Practice: Offer one material gift or act of provision. Feed, repair, contribute, make space, or share a resource in a way that honors the other person’s freedom.

Prayer: Angel of Altruistic Love in Taurus, teach me generous provision. Let my gifts nourish without binding. May Christ bless the bread, shelter, and steadiness I offer.
