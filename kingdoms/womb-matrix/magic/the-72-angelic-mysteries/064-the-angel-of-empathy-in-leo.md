# The Angel of Empathy in Leo



**The Chapel of Saint Peter**

The Angel of Empathy in Leo teaches empathy with dignity, visibility, and the longing to be seen. Leo carries the heart, creativity, honor, recognition, leadership, and the wound of humiliation. In the Chapel of Saint Peter, this longing is purified through failure and restoration. Peter is seen by Christ in both weakness and vocation. Through his chapel, empathy learns to recognize the person beneath shame and performance.

This angel appears when someone wants to be seen, appreciated, or restored to confidence. The reader may dismiss this as ego, but Leo empathy looks deeper. Sometimes the need to shine hides a wound of being ignored. Sometimes pride protects a terrified heart. Sometimes performance asks for blessing.

When this card appears, ask where dignity needs recognition. Who is longing to be seen rightly? Who has been reduced to failure, appearance, role, or reputation? Empathy restores the full person to view.

Practice: Offer one sincere recognition. Name something true and dignifying in another person, especially someone who feels unseen. Do the same for yourself without embarrassment.

Prayer: Angel of Empathy in Leo, teach me to see the heart beneath the performance. Let my recognition restore dignity. May Christ see through me with love.
