# The Angel of Mercy in Sagittarius



**The Chapel of Saint James the Greater**

The Angel of Mercy in Sagittarius teaches mercy as the reopening of the road. Sagittarius carries pilgrimage, teaching, distance, meaning, blessing, and the courage to continue. In the Chapel of Saint James the Greater, mercy becomes the grace given to the traveler who has stumbled, wandered, or lost the path. James blesses the pilgrim who must keep walking.

This angel appears when failure has made the soul believe the journey is over. The reader may have taken a wrong road, trusted a false map, spoken foolishly, abandoned discipline, or lost faith in the path. Sagittarius mercy does not trap the pilgrim at the site of the mistake. It teaches, blesses, and points toward the next horizon.

When this card appears, ask where mercy is opening the road again. What lesson has been learned? What burden must be left behind? What truth must be carried forward? The journey continues, but not in ignorance.

Practice: Take a short walk as a prayer. With each step say inwardly: “Mercy opens the road.” At the end, ask Christ what wisdom must guide your next steps.

Prayer: Angel of Mercy in Sagittarius, teach me to continue after error. Let my wandering become wisdom. May Christ bless the road that opens beyond failure.
