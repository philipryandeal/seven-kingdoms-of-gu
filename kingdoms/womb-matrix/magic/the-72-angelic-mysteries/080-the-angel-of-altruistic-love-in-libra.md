# The Angel of Altruistic Love in Libra



**The Chapel of Saint Bartholomew / Nathanael**

The Angel of Altruistic Love in Libra teaches love that honors mutual dignity. Libra carries relationship, fairness, beauty, harmony, and the sacred recognition of the other. In the Chapel of Saint Bartholomew, also called Nathanael, this love becomes sincere and without guile. Through his chapel, altruistic love seeks the good of the relationship without deceit, domination, or self-erasure.

This angel appears when love must become fair. One person may be giving too much, taking too much, pleasing too much, or demanding too much. Libra altruistic love does not confuse sacrifice with imbalance. It asks whether both souls are honored before God.

When this card appears, ask what love requires for mutual dignity. The answer may be generosity, apology, honest conversation, restored boundaries, or a more beautiful form of exchange.

Practice: Bring one relationship into prayer. Ask: “Where is dignity missing?” Then take one action that restores fairness without withdrawing love.

Prayer: Angel of Altruistic Love in Libra, teach me love without deceit. Let my giving honor both self and other. May Christ make my relationships truthful, beautiful, and just.
