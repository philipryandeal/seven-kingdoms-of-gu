# The Angel of Empathy in Taurus



**The Chapel of Saint Andrew**

The Angel of Empathy in Taurus teaches empathy through the body. Taurus carries touch, hunger, comfort, fatigue, beauty, money, land, and the wisdom of the senses. In the Chapel of Saint Andrew, this embodied awareness becomes simple discipleship. Andrew brings others to Christ through faithful nearness. Through his chapel, empathy learns to notice what words may not say.

This angel appears when another person’s body is speaking. Someone may be tired, hungry, tense, overstimulated, under-resourced, or in need of quiet comfort. The reader may be listening only to words while ignoring the body beneath them. Taurus empathy asks: what does this creature need in flesh, space, rhythm, and care?

When this card appears, ask how the body changes the meaning of the situation. A conflict may be partly exhaustion. A mood may be hunger. A refusal may be overwhelm. A silence may be the body asking for safety.

Practice: Offer embodied empathy. Ask yourself or another: “Have you eaten? Have you rested? Do you need quiet?” Let care begin with the creaturely body.

Prayer: Angel of Empathy in Taurus, teach me to listen through the senses. Let me perceive hunger, fatigue, and need with tenderness. May Christ make my presence steady and kind.
