# The Angel of Altruistic Love in Sagittarius



**The Chapel of Saint James the Greater**

The Angel of Altruistic Love in Sagittarius teaches love that blesses the other’s journey. Sagittarius carries pilgrimage, teaching, distance, meaning, freedom, and the search for truth. In the Chapel of Saint James the Greater, this love becomes blessing for the road. James walks with the pilgrim and honors the holy path.

This angel appears when someone must grow, travel, learn, leave, seek, or become more than the role they have played in your life. Sagittarius altruistic love does not imprison the beloved for comfort. It blesses the journey, even when the road leads beyond familiar closeness.

When this card appears, ask where love must release control over another person’s path. Can you bless their becoming without needing to be the center of it? Can you teach without owning the student?

Practice: Pray a blessing for someone on a path you cannot control. Say: “May Christ guide your road. May truth meet you. May your freedom become holy.”

Prayer: Angel of Altruistic Love in Sagittarius, teach me to bless the pilgrim. Let my love encourage freedom and truth. May Christ guide every road beyond my grasp.
