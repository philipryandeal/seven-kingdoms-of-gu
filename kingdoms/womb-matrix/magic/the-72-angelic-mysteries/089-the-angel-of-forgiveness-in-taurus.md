# The Angel of Forgiveness in Taurus



**The Chapel of Saint Andrew**

The Angel of Forgiveness in Taurus teaches the forgiveness that releases the body from stored resentment. Taurus carries embodiment, comfort, stability, resources, touch, and the slow wisdom of the flesh. In the Chapel of Saint Andrew, this steadiness becomes faithful discipleship. Andrew brings others to Christ through simple nearness. Through his chapel, forgiveness becomes patient and embodied.

This angel appears when the mind has chosen release but the body still holds the wound. Resentment may live in the jaw, shoulders, stomach, breath, sleep, appetite, or posture. Taurus forgiveness does not rush the body. It brings the injured creature into gentle contact with Christ.

When this card appears, ask where resentment has become physical. What tightness is asking to be blessed? What part of the body still expects harm? Forgiveness may need rest, touch, food, warmth, or safety.

Practice: Sit comfortably and scan the body. Wherever tension gathers, breathe into that place and say: “You do not have to carry this alone.” Offer the sensation to Christ without forcing it to disappear.

Prayer: Angel of Forgiveness in Taurus, teach my body to release resentment slowly. Let Christ bring peace to flesh, breath, and bone. May my whole being learn freedom.
