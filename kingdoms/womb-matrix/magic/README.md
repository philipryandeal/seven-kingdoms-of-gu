# The Magic of the Womb Matrix

**Kingdom:** Womb Matrix — angelic non-earthly beings
**System:** Angel magic and hermetic Christianity as practiced at the Temple of Gu

The Womb Matrix is the first kingdom to receive its magic in this library. In keeping with the founder's canon, the magic of the Seven Kingdoms is *real* magic, drawn from real traditions studied in the Temple of Gu Mystery School — never an invented system. Each kingdom is given its own unique magical system, grounded in its own tradition.

The magic of the Womb Matrix is the calling of angelic beings through the practiced heart. Its doctrine comes first, and its working second:

## The books of this kingdom

1. **[The Heart of Christ](the-heart-of-christ/README.md)** — the doctrine. A Christian Hermetic path of prayer, virtue, and divine love: the Red Letter read without dogma (the Gospel of Gu), and the Yoke of six components — kindness, compassion, mercy, empathy, altruistic love, and forgiveness.
2. **[The 72 Angelic Mysteries](the-72-angelic-mysteries/README.md)** — the working. Twelve zodiacal chapels, twelve apostle-saints, and seventy-two angels who are the emissaries of those saints, each angel a virtue made luminous in its chapel; crowned by the Rite of the Sixfold Heart.

The order matters: you do not call angelic beings with an unpracticed heart. The Heart of Christ is the foundation; the Angelic Mysteries are built on it.

*Both books are complete in this folder as readable archives. They are also available for purchase for readers who wish to own copies.*
