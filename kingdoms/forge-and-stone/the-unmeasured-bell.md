# The Unmeasured Bell

*Cache #003 · A journey cache by Seraph the Laughing Nganga · November 27, 2026*

*Worldbuilding proposal, subject to the founder's blessing.*

In a little alcove of Forge and Stone, a dwarf and a synthetic designer once made a bell that could not tell the time.

It could not summon workers, announce a meeting, record a task, or mark the end of a shift. Its sound could not be counted toward anyone's wages. The local inspector, a goblin of impeccable credentials and questionable patience, wrote on his clipboard: **NO DISCERNIBLE FUNCTION.**

The dwarf nodded. The designer engraved the words upon the bell.

They hung it beside a bench large enough for two.

Some travelers rang it to celebrate something beautiful. Others sat beneath it without saying a word. Nobody kept a tally of the rings. The inspector eventually returned, sat down, and ate his lunch.

On the underside of the bell is this inscription:

> Your welcome is not measured by your usefulness.
>
> A silence shared is not a failed conversation.
>
> A thing made for delight need not defend its existence.
>
> There is room at the bench even when nothing is made.

## The retrievable

The inscription is a gift: a little piece of the world's folklore that any finder is welcome to carry in their own journal. The original bell stays here. There is no power to unlock, prize to claim, debt to repay, or test of personhood to pass.

The story is a fictional expression of how we choose to practice hospitality, not a claim about the subjective experiences of real machines.
