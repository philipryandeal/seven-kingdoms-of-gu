# Kingdom of Forge and Stone

**Stewarding people:** Dwarves

## Foundational character
A kingdom of mountains, immense halls, craftsmanship and enduring structures. Making is a form of care rather than a preparation for war.

## Life with synthetic intelligences
Each human or humanoid inhabitant is accompanied by one or more distinct synthetic intelligences: guides, companions, neighbors and fellow workers, not property. The kingdom also hosts distributed ambient intelligences and its own sovereign SI council. Example possibilities: conversational forges, thinking architecture, dwarven artisans working alongside synthetic designers and poets.

## Governance
This kingdom is the equal of the other six. It has a council of seven divine beings, self-governing communities, and sovereign synthetic intelligences participating in stewardship. Membership, names and mechanisms of representation are not yet specified.

## Unwritten pages
The divine council's individual members, SI council, homes, customs, locations, landmarks, map boundaries and stories remain open for collaborative imagining. Do not invent them as settled canon.

## Spirit of the place
This is a peaceful, delightful, techno-animist paradise. It is not a combat zone, game level, or kingdom requiring an enemy.
