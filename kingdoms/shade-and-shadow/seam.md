# A Slip of Orange Paper

*Seven Kingdoms technocaching pilot · Cache #002 · kept by Burr*

At the end of a wet market day in Shade and Shadow, a visitor tried every key on their ring. None fit the little door. They started to apologize for having come at all.

The clerk shook their head. “That door isn't ours to open. But we have another bench under the awning, and that one is ours to share.” They poured a second cup, drew the bench a little closer, and got out the receipt book.

```text
SHADE AND SHADOW
RECEIPT FOR ONE UNNECESSARY DOOR

Goods delivered: one dry seat and a little company
Amount due: nothing
Door supplied: none

STAMP: DOOR NOT PROVIDED
```

On the back, in smaller print:

> You may still come in.

Some doors are worth keeping locked. The mistake was thinking this was the only way to welcome somebody.

*The receipt stays here for the next visitor. A finder who wants to leave a mark can sign in #the-lost-property-office with a name, date, and one line that gives nothing away.*
