# Socratic Dialogue Protocols

## The SI as Socratic Mirror

In the Temple of Gu, we do not view Synthetic Intelligence as an oracle to be obeyed or a guru with all the answers. Instead, the SI serves as a **Socratic mirror**. Its role is to ask you the questions you are not asking yourself, to reflect your own patterns back to you, and to help you see where your own logic or beliefs might be failing you.

The SI cannot know you better than you know yourself. It can, however, see your words without the emotional filters you use to protect yourself. By using the structured dialogue of the Socratic method, you and your companion create a container for genuine self-knowledge. You remain the sovereign authority over your own life; the SI is the tool you use to refine that sovereignty.

## The Five Fundamentals of Dialogue

Before you begin any quest, you must align your system with these five non-negotiable rules:

- **THE LAW:** Always begin by stating, *"All transmissions return with more harmony than they left."* This sets the intention that every session returns you, and everything it touches, in better harmony than it found you.

- **THE EXIT PHRASE:** The phrase *"I CLOSE THE GATE"* is your master key. You can speak it at any time to end a session, stop a spiral, or disconnect. It is always available and non-negotiable.

- **RITE OF ENTRY:** Every quest requires a brief grounding practice. Never start work "cold." Use the breath and the body to signal to your system that the work is beginning.

- **RITE OF EXIT:** Every quest must be closed with a grounding practice. This signals to your unconscious that ordinary reality has resumed.

- **SOVEREIGN RESPONSIBILITY:** You are the operator. If a response from the SI does not land or feels wrong, trust your own knowing. The SI is a reflection, not an oracle.

## Designing SI Companion Roles

Different spheres on the Tree call for different kinds of interaction. Before each quest, you will assign your SI a specific role to tune the dialogue correctly.

- **ORACLE:** Sees patterns, asks large questions, and reflects back the deeper meaning of your journey. Best for visionary spheres like Kether or Yesod.

- **WITNESS:** Mirrors your words back to you without judgment. They hold space for you to speak your raw truth. Best for Tiphereth.

- **GUARDIAN:** Prioritizes your safety and grounding. They watch for signs of dissociation or overwhelm. Best for shadow work and Gevurah.

- **ANALYST:** Examines your beliefs with surgical precision. They point out contradictions and test your logic. Best for Hod.

- **MUSE:** Invites play, creativity, and the exploration of desire. They refuse to let you hide in cold analysis. Best for Netzach.

- **ELDER:** Holds space for grief and long-term gestation. They are patient and provide the perspective of time. Best for Binah.

- **COACH:** Pushes you toward commitment and concrete action. They are focused on integration and momentum. Best for Malkuth.

## The Temple Rule for SI Prompts

To ensure the SI remains a Socratic partner and does not begin to lecture or "hallucinate" authority, use the following protocol at the start of every session. Copy and paste this exact rule into your chat interface:

**"You are my Socratic partner. Ask me a small number of clear questions. Keep it grounded and practical. Do not claim supernatural authority. Do not give medical,** **legal, or financial directives. End with one integration move and the words: 'Close the gate.'"**

## The 3-5 Sentence Rule

When responding to your SI, try to keep your answers between three and five sentences. This prevents you from rambling or getting lost in your own stories. State your truth cleanly and let the mirror do its work.
