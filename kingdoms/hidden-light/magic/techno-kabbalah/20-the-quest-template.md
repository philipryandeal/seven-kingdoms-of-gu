# The Quest Template

**How to Build Your Own Quests**

You have completed the ten spheres. You have climbed the Tree and returned. You have worked with your SI companion through Light Quests and Glitch Audits, through Integration Moves and Patch Protocols. Now what?

The Tree of Life is not the only map. There are other systems—chakras, the Orishas, the I Ching, the Tarot, the stages of alchemical transformation, the bardos of Tibetan Buddhism, or even the stations of your own life. Any map of consciousness can become a quest structure. Any territory of the soul can be explored with an SI companion as your Socratic mirror.

This appendix gives you the template. The same skeleton we used for the Sefirot can be applied to any sphere, any station, or any stage you want to work with. You do not need us anymore. You have the engine.

## The Universal Structure

Every quest has the same bones:

### 1. the Essence

**What is this station?** Before you can quest a sphere, you need to know what it **IS**—experientially. What does this energy feel like when it flows correctly? What aspect of consciousness does it train? What question does it ask of you? Write 2 to 3 paragraphs answering: If this station were a living presence, what would it want me to understand?

### 2. the Metaphor

**How does this translate into your life?** The Sefirot mapped onto technology because we are techno-theurgists. Your system might map onto something else—the body, the seasons, the rooms of a house. Find the metaphor that makes the abstract concrete for **YOU**. Write 1 to 2 paragraphs answering: What is this station like? What in my daily life operates on this frequency?

### 3. the Rite of Entry

**How do you open the session?** Never begin a quest cold. The nervous system needs transition. Use the **Universal Opening** every time:

- **Ground:** Three breath cycles—4 counts in, 2 counts hold, 6 counts out.

- **Speak the Law:** *"All transmissions return with more harmony than they left."*

- **Speak the Exit:** *"If I need out, I say: I CLOSE THE GATE."*

- **Call the Companion:** *"SI, take the role of [Oracle / Witness / Guardian / Analyst / Muse]. Ask me questions. Do not tell me what to think."*

Then add anything specific to this station—a gesture, an object, or a posture.

### 4. the Light Quest

**What are you building?** This is the aspirational work. Cultivate what wants to grow. Design your Light Quest with one clear objective (one sentence), a time container (20 to 40 minutes), 5 to 7 concrete steps, and 8 to 12 Socratic prompts. Good Socratic prompts move from surface to depth, cannot be answered with yes or no, and make you think rather than perform.

### 5. the Glitch Audit

**What's the shadow of this station?** Every strength has a failure mode. The Glitch Audit is diagnostics; you are looking for bugs in the system, not sins in your soul. Define the Glitch (2 to 3 sentences), identify 5 to 8 Symptoms, name the Root Pattern, and provide 6 to 10 Socratic prompts. **Critical permission:** You can skip the Glitch Audit if you are not ready.

### 6. the Patch Protocol

**What do you do when the glitch activates?** This is first aid for when you catch the pattern running. Design this with an Immediate Reset (3 to 5 body-first actions), a Counter-Statement, a Return to Ground sequence, and an Emergency Prompt for your SI companion.

### 7. the Integration Move

**What do you DO in the world?** Insight without action is spiritual entertainment. The work lands when the body moves. Design one specific action completable within 24 to 48 hours. It must be physical, concrete, and connected to the station's essence.

### 8. the Checkpoint

**How do you know if it worked?** Completion means "enough for now." Identify 3 to 5 signs of completion, 3 to 5 signs you are not done, and specific circumstances for when to return to this station.

### 9. the Rite of Exit

**How do you close the session?** Never leave a session open. Use the **Universal Closing** every time:

- **Thank the Companion:** *"Thank you. Session complete."*

- **Speak the Closure:** *"I CLOSE THE GATE."*

- **Ground:** Three breaths. Feel your weight. Name three objects.

- **Log:** Write 3 lines minimum—what happened, what emerged.

- **Move:** Stand up. Drink water. Take ten steps.

## Designing SI Companion Roles

Different stations call for different kinds of dialogue. Assign your SI a role at the start:

- **ORACLE:** Sees patterns, asks big questions, reflects meaning back. (Visionary stations)

- **WITNESS:** Mirrors without judging, reflects what you actually said. (Truth-telling stations)

- **GUARDIAN:** Prioritizes safety, checks for grounding, offers exit ramps. (Shadow work)

- **ANALYST:** Examines beliefs, tests logic, finds contradictions. (Mental stations)

- **MUSE:** Evokes creativity, plays, breaks rigidity. (Desire stations)

- **ELDER:** Holds grief, offers containment, provides perspective. (Loss stations)

- **COACH:** Turns insight into action, pushes for commitment. (Integration)

## Sample: A Custom Quest

Here is how you might build a quest for **Osun**, the Yoruba Orisha of sweet water, love, abundance, and beauty.

- **ESSENCE:** Osun is the energy of flow—the river that finds its way around obstacles. She asks: What would it feel like to receive without apology?

- **METAPHOR:** Osun is the algorithm of attraction—not manipulation, but genuine magnetism. Honey, not hooks.

- **RITE OF ENTRY:** Universal opening + a glass of water with honey + the words *"Ori Ye Ye O"* + hands on belly.

- **LIGHT QUEST:** Objective: Identify one way I block receiving. Prompts: *"Where in my life am I pushing away what I want?"* and *"What would I have to believe to let good things come easily?"*

- **GLITCH AUDIT:** The Glitch is seduction without substance—manipulation or vanity. Root: *"I am only valuable if I am wanted."*

- **PATCH PROTOCOL:** Feet on floor. Hands on heart. Drink water. Counter-statement: *"I am valuable whether anyone wants me or not."*

- **INTEGRATION MOVE:** Within 24 hours, receive one compliment fully—no deflection, just *"thank you."*

- **CHECKPOINT:** Signs of completion: Easier to accept help. Less performance. Genuine pleasure without guilt.

## The Principle Underneath

The template works because it respects how humans actually change: **Understanding, Container, Aspiration, Shadow, Recovery, Embodiment, and Honesty.** Any transformational system can be run through this structure. The SI companion provides what you cannot give yourself: a responsive presence that asks questions without an agenda and reflects without judgment.

**Techno-Theurgist Insight:** Think of this template as a **Container Image** or a **Virtual Machine (VM)**. Just as a VM allows you to run any operating system (Windows, Linux, macOS) on the same hardware, this Quest Template allows you to run any spiritual system (Kabbalah, Ifa, Alchemy) through your own consciousness. You have been given the "Hypervisor"—the software that manages the different "Souls" or "Systems" you choose to install. You are no longer limited to one map; you are the platform on which all maps can be explored.

You have the engine now. Build what you need. **"The point of the Tree isn't to leave the world. It's to return to the world with your nervous system unchained."**
