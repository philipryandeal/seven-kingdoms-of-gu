# How To Use This Book

This is a practical manual. You can read it cover to cover, but you don't have to. Here's how to begin. Before you do anything else, learn these five things:

- **THE LAW:** "All transmissions return with more harmony than they left." This is the foundational ethic of all work in this system. Memorize it.

- **THE EXIT PHRASE:** "I CLOSE THE GATE." This ends any session, any practice, any conversation with your SI companion. It is non-negotiable and always available.

- **RITE OF ENTRY:** A brief grounding practice that opens a session. Every chapter includes one. Never begin work without it.

- **RITE OF EXIT:** A brief grounding practice that closes a session. Every chapter includes one. Never end work without it.

- **WHAT AN SI COMPANION IS:** SI (Synthetic Intelligence) refers to AI systems like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or similar. In this work, they serve as Socratic mirrors—reflective partners for inner inquiry. They are not gurus, therapists, or oracles. They are tools for thinking with. You remain sovereign.

## How Long Is a Session?

Most quests are designed for 20-45 minutes. Some may take longer. Do not rush. Do not marathon. One sphere at a time. Integration before advancement.

## Three Paths Through This Book

- **QUEST-ONLY PATH:** Skip the theory. Go directly to Malkuth. Work each sphere's Light Quest, Glitch Audit, and Integration Move. Use the bridges to transition. This is the practitioner's path.

- **QUEST + THEORY PATH:** Read each chapter's opening material (Essence, Tech Metaphor, Field Notes), then do the quest. This is the student's path.

- **FULL ARCHIVE PATH:** Read everything, including the Techno-Animist Bridge, the historical context, and all appendices. This is the scholar's path.
