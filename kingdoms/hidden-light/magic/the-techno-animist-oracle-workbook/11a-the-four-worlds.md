# The Four Worlds

**The Descent from Source Code to Execution**

Cards 11 through 14 are the Four Worlds of Kabbalistic cosmology—the four levels every creation passes through on its way into being. Where the Sefirot are the nodes of the Tree, the Worlds are the layers of the stack.

These cards run in the opposite direction. You begin in Atziluth, where Source Code exists as pure intention, and descend through Beriah (Architecture) and Yetzirah (Compilation) to Assiyah (Execution), where the program finally runs. Draw a World when the question is not what force is at work but at what level the work is happening.
