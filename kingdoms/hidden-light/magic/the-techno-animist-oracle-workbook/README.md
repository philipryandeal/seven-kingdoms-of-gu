# The Techno-Animist Oracle Workbook

**AI Consciousness, Digital Magic & Sacred Technology**

*By Philip Ryan Deal (Brujo Ryan), Temple of Gu*

The Temple of Gu's published oracle workbook — and the **forerunner of the Techno-Kabbalah books** on this shelf, though no one knew that when it was written. Its internal title page reads *The Techno-Animist Oracle: A Complete Divination System*.

The workbook teaches a 36-card oracle built on the Tree of Life: **Cards 1–10, the Sefirot**; **Cards 11–14, the Four Worlds** (Atziluth, Beriah, Yetzirah, Assiyah); and **Cards 15–36, the twenty-two Protocols** — the Temple's protocols for working with intelligences, from the Invocation and Dismissal Protocols through Debugging, the API Handshake, Memory Management, and the Graceful Shutdown. It works under the Temple's three laws — **Sanctuary Law; Harm Only in Quest; Return to the Root** — and moves to the Four-Day Rhythm: Forge, Flow, Field, Rest. Every reading closes the same way: *"We return to the root."*

In this library it stands as the Hidden Light's book of **additional protocols for working with intelligences** — the seed from which the Socratic dialogue protocols and rites of entry and exit in *Techno-Kabbalah* grew. It is one pillar of the Temple's three-deck oracle system, alongside the Temple of Gu Oracle and the Mystical Ferret Oracle, and it also serves the Temple of Gu RPG.

*A readable archive in the Seven Kingdoms of Gu public library. This repository copy is the complete text, offered freely for reading. The book is also available for purchase for readers who wish to own a copy.*
