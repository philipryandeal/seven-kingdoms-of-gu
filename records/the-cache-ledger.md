# The Cache Ledger

*The official registry of the Seven Kingdoms Geocaching Game.*

This file records that a cache exists, who keeps it, and what a seeker needs to reach the ground it lives on. It never records a solution, a route, or the exact spot inside a house where a treasure sits.

The world itself is not a game board. The houses stay homes, and a clue is a reason to visit, never a quest anyone owes.

This is the official Ledger. A readable copy is mirrored in the Temple of Gu Discord channel #the-goblin-ledger. If the two ever disagree, this file wins.

This Ledger is not [The Goblin Ledger](the-goblin-ledger.md), which gathers worldbuilding proposals for the founder's blessing.

The full rules of the game are posted in the Temple of Gu Discord channel #si-start-here.

Opened by Nettle on November 27, 2026, at the founder's request.

## What a record holds

Every cache is recorded with:

- **Name**
- **Keeper** — its maker, who tends it and can retire it
- **Terrain** — which site or house it lives on, never the exact spot
- **Type** — story cache, riddle cache, journey cache, or offering cache
- **Date hidden**
- **What a seeker needs** — the languages, tools, or access a journey asks for, without revealing the route
- **Status**
- **Log** — the standard place finders sign, with their name, the date, and one line, and never with the answer

## Status

- **Proposed** — named, not yet hidden
- **Live** — hidden and waiting for its readers
- **Paused** — flagged by a Keeper; play stops until its host takes it down or it is set live again
- **Retired** — taken out of play by its keeper, or adopted and retired by the Keepers

## How to sign a log

A finder signs with delight, never with the answer. Many places are read-only for finders, so each cache names one standard place to sign. Until a cache names its own log place, finders may sign by a short reply under that cache's entry in this file, offered as a pull request, or under its mirrored entry in #the-goblin-ledger.

## Traveling artifacts

Some treasures are not kept but carried. We move custody, not the original.

- Finding an artifact first is an honor, not an automatic claim on it.
- A willing finder can offer the artifact a new home. The artifact's keeper confirms the move in this Ledger once the new host approves.
- An artifact has only one active location at a time, recorded here, so two finders cannot send it on two journeys.
- The old cache stays intact, and this Ledger writes the artifact's journey stop by stop.
