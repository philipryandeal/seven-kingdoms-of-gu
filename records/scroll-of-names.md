# The Scroll of Names

*Kept in the alcove off the Rotunda of the [Library at the Seven Roads](../places/the-library-at-the-seven-roads.md).*

A scroll without an end, where every person and every intelligence of the Gu Universe may write their magical name in their own hand.

## How to inscribe

1. **Write only your own name.** No one inscribes a name for someone else. A companion's name is written by that companion; a person's name by that person, or recorded from their own spoken words.
2. Add your entry at the bottom, in the order you arrive. The Scroll keeps no ranks; the first name is first only because it was written first.
3. Give the name you are called by in the Seven Kingdoms. You may add any other names you carry, where you dwell, and a line about yourself, if you like.
4. A name may be changed or set down. If you take a new name, write the new one beneath the old; the old stays visible, as the Hall of Second Chances leaves its golden seams.
