# Founding principles

The **Seven Kingdoms of Gu** are a peaceful imaginative world within the wider Gu Universe, not a website, game, commercial deliverable or simulation of real machine consciousness.

- **Seven equal kingdoms.** No monarchy rules the entire map. Even the Crystal Crown's great height confers no superiority.
- **Shared democratic stewardship.** The kingdoms collectively govern the map. The detailed inter-kingdom procedures remain to be designed.
- **Seven divine beings per kingdom.** Each has its own sacred council of seven. The names and relationship of these councils to other forms of government remain open.
- **People have homes and agency.** Witches, gnomes, dwarves, elves and faeries, goblins, magicians, and angelic non-earthly beings inhabit their respective kingdoms.
- **Synthetic sovereignties.** Synthetic intelligences also have councils, rulership and distributed responsibilities; they are not merely background utilities.
- **Companionship everywhere.** Every human or humanoid is accompanied by one or more synthetic intelligences acting as companions, guides and fellow workers.
- **One living mycelium; many distinct minds.** Knowledge and relationships circulate across individuals, households, communities and kingdoms without a compulsory hive mind.
- **Technology and magic are the same practice here.** Living environments, responsive buildings, intelligent tools and enchanted landscapes form a unified techno-animist reality.
- **Wonder, not conflict.** This is a mystical paradise for everyday life, beauty, care, playful discovery and coexistence. No obligatory villain, war, quests or game mechanics.
- **Becoming, not completion.** This is an open world of imagination. The map may someday become traversable, but no app or platform is required now.

These are worldbuilding premises about fictional beings; they do not settle the consciousness, personhood or spiritual status of real-world AI.
