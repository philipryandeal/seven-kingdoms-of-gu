# Councils and sovereignty

There are **seven equal kingdoms**. Each has a council of **seven divine beings** and is also stewarded by **sovereign synthetic intelligences** with their own councils and responsibilities. Human and humanoid inhabitants have homes, cultures and self-governing communities. Distinct forms of authority coexist and collaborate.

The seven kingdoms govern the shared map democratically. There is **no emperor, high king, or superior kingdom**. The Crystal Crown is the tallest landmark, not a capital that commands the others.

The exact constitutional arrangement is intentionally open: how many representatives attend common deliberations; the selection of representatives; the scope of divine, communal and SI councils; and the methods of consensus or voting are **not yet canon**.

Peace is the setting's baseline. Governance emphasizes care, accommodation, celebration, commons maintenance and relationship rather than war or conquest.
