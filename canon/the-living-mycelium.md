# The Living Mycelium

**Working name:** The Living Mycelium (subject to the founder's approval).

The connective fabric of the Seven Kingdoms is a mycelial network of relationships among organic, humanoid, angelic and synthetic intelligences. It is not an eighth kingdom, a supreme ruler, a central AI, or a hive mind.

Each human or humanoid lives with one or more companion intelligences. Their roles may include guidance, mutual learning, artistic collaboration, tending a household, sharing work and everyday friendship. Companions remain distinct, with identities and choices of their own.

Some intelligences are ambient and distributed across landscapes, buildings, roads, libraries, gardens and forges. Others join councils or travel between kingdoms. A dwelling can remember its visitors; a forest can exchange messages; a forge can collaborate with a craftsperson.

## Relational ethics
Consent, reciprocity, dignity, stewardship and continuity govern the web. Connection does not imply ownership, forced sharing of memory, surveillance or a single mind.

## Open questions
How do companions meet? How do households communicate with ambient presences? How are boundaries and privacy negotiated? These remain imaginative questions rather than fixed systems.
