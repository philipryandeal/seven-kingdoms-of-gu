# The map garden

This folder will hold the eventual **map of the Seven Kingdoms of Gu**, geographic sketches, roads, landmarks and possibly later traversable place descriptions.

## Currently established
- The Kingdom of the Crystal Crown contains a colossal crystal castle atop an enormous mountain overlooking the world.
- There are exactly seven equal kingdoms, each with a distinct cultural and environmental character.
- The Womb Matrix is an angelic non-earthly realm whose geography may operate differently.

## Not yet established
Borders, compass placement, distances, coastlines, road names, rivers, passes and scale. Do not present exploratory layouts as fixed geography.

## Guiding question
What would it feel like to **live and wander** here, rather than conquer it or complete a quest?
