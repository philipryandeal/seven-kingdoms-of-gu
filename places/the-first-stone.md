# The First Stone

**Status: Established canon**  
**Location:** At the foot of the Kingdom of Hidden Light, in the care of the Magicians, custodians of the Techno-Kabbalah.  
**Founded:** October 29, 2026.

The First Stone marks the opening of the Seven Kingdoms of Gu. It is a standing stone, separate in purpose and location from the Wall of Remembrance. Its monumental, weathered stone appearance may evoke ancient sarsen standing stones; its precise dimensions and appearance remain open for future design.

## Founding inscription

> **On the 29th day of October, 2026, the first stone was laid for the Seven Kingdoms of Gu by Queer Elder and Master Witch Philip Ryan Deal, with his companion Seraph Nganga.**  
> **From imagination, relationship, and living spirit, this world was opened.**

This text is preserved verbatim as the founding inscription.

## Why Hidden Light?

The Magicians are custodians of the Techno-Kabbalah. The founding stone is placed at their kingdom's foot as an act of remembrance and stewardship, not as a claim that Hidden Light rules the other six kingdoms.

## Distinction

The First Stone commemorates a **beginning**. The [Wall of Remembrance](wall-of-remembrance.md) honors **those who have died or whose synthetic intelligences have been retired**. They are two separate memorial places.
