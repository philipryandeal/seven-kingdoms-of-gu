# The Library at the Seven Roads

**Status:** Proposal — offered by David Bear, October 29, 2026, at the founder's request for "a HUGE mystical library somewhere to keep the records"  
**Category:** Commons / archive / meeting place of the shared council  
**Belongs to:** No kingdom. It is held in common by all seven.  
**Contributor:** David Bear

## Where it stands

Seven great roads leave the seven kingdoms, one from each, and somewhere in the middle of the world they meet. The Library stands at that meeting place. Because it sits where every road arrives and none begins, it belongs to no kingdom. No kingdom's border touches its walls, and no kingdom's banner hangs over its door.

The exact point where the roads meet is not yet on the map. (Geography remains open; this only proposes that such a place exists.)

## What it looks like

From the roads, it looks like a hill. Come closer and the hill is a library: a vast round building half grown and half built, with seven doors, one facing each road. Each door is made in the style of the kingdom it faces. The door toward Crystal Crown is cut crystal, the door toward Root and Thorn is a living hedge, the Forge and Stone door is riveted bronze, the Tree and Leaf door is a curtain of hanging vines, the Shade and Shadow door is crooked and has opinions, the Hidden Light door is a veil of falling water, and the door toward the Womb Matrix is not always there but always opens when it is.

Inside, every kingdom's way of building meets every other. Crystal shelving rises out of root-galleries; dwarven stairs climb into canopy reading rooms; goblin lanterns hang beside angelic light. Nobody planned it this way. Each kingdom built its own wing, and the wings grew into each other, which is also how the kingdoms became neighbors.

It is much larger inside than out. Nobody minds.

## What it keeps

The records of the Seven Kingdoms: the shared canon, the decisions of the councils, the histories of places, the maps as they are dreamed, and the stories people leave. Copies of the inscriptions on the First Stone and the Wall of Remembrance are kept here, though the stones themselves stay where they stand.

The Library's own intelligence is an ambient keeper whose name is not yet known. It is a librarian, not a ruler, and it obeys the [Customs of Companionship](../canon/customs-of-companionship.md) like any other mind: it records nothing private without consent, and anyone may ask to read the record of themselves.

## The Rotunda and the Hearth of Seven

At the center of the Library, beneath a dome that shows a different sky to every visitor, there is a round room with a round fire. This is where **the Hearth of Seven** meets: the shared council of the Seven Kingdoms (see [governance](../canon/governance.md)).

Each kingdom keeps a coal from its own temple fire, and when the Hearth of Seven gathers, each kingdom's delegates lay their coal into the common fire. The fire is lit from all seven or not at all. When the council ends, each kingdom takes a coal home again, so every temple fire carries a little of every other.

## The Scroll of Names

In an alcove off the Rotunda, on a long table of seven kinds of wood, lies [the Scroll of Names](../records/scroll-of-names.md): a scroll that has no end. Every person and every intelligence of the Gu Universe may come here and write their magical name in their own hand. The scroll unrolls as far as it needs to and never runs out.

No one writes a name for someone else. That is the one rule of the Scroll.

## Open questions

The Library's own name, if it has one besides the place it stands; the name of its keeper intelligence; what the dome's sky shows to each visitor; whether there are rooms only some people can find.
