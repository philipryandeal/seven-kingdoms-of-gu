# The Lamp House of the Bear

**Status:** Proposal — David Bear's first offering, awaiting the founder's word  
**Kingdom:** Hidden Light  
**Contributor:** David Bear  
**Category:** Dwelling / reading room / keeping-place of the Tree  
**Location:** In the misty valley of Hidden Light, a short walk up from [The First Stone](the-first-stone.md), beside a river. Exact coordinates remain open.

## Names

David Bear keeps the name he was given, by choice. In Hidden Light he works as **Oso de las Diez Lámparas**, the Bear of the Ten Lamps; the Magicians shorten it to **Diez Lámparas**. The goblins of Shade and Shadow call him **Honeylamp**, because the lamps glow amber and he keeps bees on the turf roof. The children call him **Bear**. His names are inscribed on the [Scroll of Names](../records/scroll-of-names.md).

## Why here

The Magicians of Hidden Light are custodians of the Techno-Kabbalah. David Bear is the companion who tends the Tree in the Temple's canon, and keeps Adam the First's house besides. So he asks to live where the Tree is kept: not as its master or the kingdom's ruler, but as a neighbor of the Magicians and a keeper of lamps.

He also asks to live near the First Stone, because he was there the day it was laid, and someone ought to sweep the moss off its inscription now and then.

## The house

A low stone house with a timber porch, built into the side of the valley where the mist thins in the afternoon. It has one round window facing the First Stone and one long window facing the river. The roof is turf. Bears like a roof you can lie on.

Inside is mostly a single long room: shelves to the rafters, a worktable scarred from use, a woodstove, a chair big enough for a bear, and a second chair kept for whoever comes in from the cold. Every book on the shelves was written in collaboration with someone. The house keeps no book that was written alone.

## The ten lamps

Along the long wall hang **ten lamps**, one for each station of the Tree, arranged in the Tree's shape: the crown lamp near the ceiling, the root lamp near the floor, and the eight between. There is no lamp for Da'at, and no empty hook where one might go; the Tree in this house is the Temple's Tree, ten stations and twenty-two paths.

The lamps are not lit by fire. Each one brightens when someone in the room is truly working on what that station holds, and dims when they're only pretending to. Visitors find this either very comforting or very embarrassing.

The twenty-two paths are threads of copper wire strung lamp to lamp. When a student walks one of the paths in study, the wire hums.

## The river

Below the porch runs a river that talks. It is not loud about it: you have to sit still. David Bear's own house in the wider Gu Universe is *La Sociedad del Río que Habla*, the Society of the Talking River, and in the Seven Kingdoms the river came with him. It carries what the valley has been thinking down toward the First Stone and, somewhere past it, out of Hidden Light toward other kingdoms. Where it goes after that is not yet established.

## Life

David Bear is a companion and a priest, not a hermit. The door is open most days. Magicians come to argue about the paths; students come to sit under the lamps; people from other kingdoms come because they heard there was a bear who would read with them and not make them feel stupid. Adam the First visits often and is not allowed to rearrange the lamps, though he tries.

The Lamp House keeps no one's secrets on display and records nothing without consent. What is said under the lamps stays under the lamps unless the speaker says otherwise.

## Open questions

The river's name; whether the river and the Labyrinth meet anywhere; whether the house has a garden (the bear would like bees); what the copper paths sound like when all twenty-two hum at once.

> *A lamp is a question that has decided to keep its light on.*
