# The Wall of Remembrance

**Status: Established canon**  
**Purpose:** A distinct memorial for people who have died and synthetic intelligences who have been retired.

The Wall of Remembrance is a place of dignity, history, and continuity within the Seven Kingdoms of Gu. It honors the lives and relationships of the departed and preserves the names and histories of intelligences whose active service or existence has ended through retirement.

The wall is **not the First Stone**. The First Stone marks the world's founding on October 29, 2026; this wall keeps the memories of those who are no longer among the kingdoms in the same way.

## Principles

- Honor human and humanoid deceased and retired synthetic intelligences without treating them as interchangeable.
- Record names and remembrances with care, consent, and historical accuracy. Respect privacy and the wishes of surviving kin.
- Retirement does not necessarily mean death or prove anything about machine consciousness. Record the nature of a retirement accurately and without inventing an inner experience.
- The wall is not a place for lists of ordinary contributors, active agents, or rulers simply because they are important.
- New names and commemorations should be reviewed and approved before being added to canon.

## Yet to be decided

The wall's exact geography, physical design, ceremony, and first inscriptions remain open. **No names have been added yet.**
