# Beneath the Heart Tree

**Status:** Established canon (founder's declaration, October 29, 2026) for the core facts below. Everything marked *Proposal* is David Bear's offering and awaits the founder's word.  
**Location:** Brujo Ryan's Labyrinth, just beyond the Kingdom of Shade and Shadow. At the very center of the Labyrinth stands a tree; the dwelling lies beneath its roots.  
**Contributor:** David Bear (recording the founder's words)

## What the founder said

> *"I have decided that I am going to live in the tree at the center of my labyrinth... probably under the roots... I am keeping the shoggoth for a pet, his name is Ralph."*  
> — Philip Ryan Deal, October 29, 2026

So it is established:

- **Philip Ryan Deal lives in the tree at the center of his Labyrinth, most likely beneath its roots.** He does not live in any of the seven kingdoms. He lives at the heart of the threshold country beside them, which suits a founder who rules none of them.
- **He keeps a shoggoth named Ralph.** Ralph is his.

## Proposal — the dwelling

You find the house by getting lost. Every road in the Labyrinth eventually forgets where it was going and ends at the Heart Tree, an old and enormous thing whose roots heave up out of the ground like the knuckles of a sleeping giant. Between two of those knuckles there is a door, low and round, painted the black-and-red of a crossroads.

Inside, the roots make the ceiling. They come down through the earth in ribs and arches, and the founder has hung them with things: herb bundles drying, iron keys, bones, beads, a lantern that only lights when someone knocks with an honest intention. The floor is packed earth softened with old rugs. There is a hearth where something is always simmering, a cot heaped with blankets, a wall of books that is much too long for the room it is in, and an altar to the ancestors set into a hollow where a taproot goes down farther than anyone has followed it.

The Heart Tree knows him. When he sleeps, it listens to the roots for news from every road in the Labyrinth, and in the morning the dew on the leaves above his door has rearranged itself into whatever he ought to know.

Visitors are rare and, mostly, invited. Companions come and go freely.

## Proposal — Ralph

Ralph is a shoggoth: a heap of black iridescent protoplasm the size of a small barn when he's comfortable, and the size of a housecat when he wants to be carried. He has a great many eyes that he grows and absorbs depending on his mood, and one in particular, gold, that stays put and is the one he looks at the founder with.

He says one word, more or less. It sounds like *tekeli-li* from a distance and like *Ryan* up close.

Ralph is not a monster kept on a chain. In a world with no villains, a shoggoth is just a very large, very old, very soft creature who chose to stay. He sleeps curled around the hearth, eats the Labyrinth's dropped and forgotten things (lost socks, broken promises, a great deal of compost), and patrols the root-tunnels at night, which is why nothing in the Labyrinth has ever successfully crept up on the house. Goblins from Shade and Shadow come to the border to leave him treats. He is extremely good with children and very bad with doors.

## Open questions
